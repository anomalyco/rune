- macOS: panics inside AppKit window, view, and launch callbacks (`App::on_focus`, `App::on_close`, `resumed`, and other `ApplicationHandler` methods) are now caught at Rune's event-loop boundary, stop the loop while skipping later callbacks, quit hooks, and `fast_exit`, and re-raise with their original payload from `run()` instead of aborting in `panic_cannot_unwind`. Panics inside a nested tracking or modal run-loop mode (such as `NSEventTrackingRunLoopMode` during live resize) report the mode and re-raise immediately rather than hanging until the tracking loop ends, and `exiting` stays unguarded so `applicationWillTerminate:` never turns a panic into exit code 0.
