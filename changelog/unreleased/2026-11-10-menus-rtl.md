- **Native menus read the app's way: `labels::set_direction`.** Right to left, a Windows pop-up, window and tray menu lays out right to left (`TPM_LAYOUTRTL`: checkmarks and submenu arrows on the left, shortcuts at the left edge) and opens under its anchor's right edge or leftward from its point, and Alt+Space opens the window menu that way at the window's top right corner. On macOS a pop-up menu and each menu of the menu bar align their items right, and a pop-up opens leftward from its point or lines up with its anchor's right edge, as a right-to-left localization's do. Left to right until the app says.
