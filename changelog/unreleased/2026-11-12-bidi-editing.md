- **Fields: words by Unicode's boundaries, and Ctrl+Shift sets a field's direction.** A word back and forward (Ctrl+Left, Ctrl+Right, Ctrl+Backspace, Ctrl+Delete) goes by UAX #29 word boundaries rather than spaces: `src/util/format.ts` stops at `src`, `util` and `format.ts`, `مرحبا(اختبار)` loses `اختبار)` and keeps `(`,, a word forward stops at the next word's start in right-to-left text too (Ctrl+Left there, as Chrome does), and a Persian word with a zero-width non-joiner (`می‌شود`) is one word. On Windows and Linux, Ctrl+Left Shift and Ctrl+Right Shift pressed together and released with nothing between them set a field left to right or right to left, every line of it, as Notepad and Word do. A shortcut such as Ctrl+Shift+P never sets it. The runtime watches every physical key for the chord, so it works in any field whatever the app does with modifier keys.
