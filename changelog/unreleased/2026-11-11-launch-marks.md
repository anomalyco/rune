- **`rune::marks`: launch and quit marks.** Rune records when the process first reaches each step to a window's first frame and out of the last one (event loop, window, DX12 probe, GPU instance, adapter, device and surface, renderer pipelines and which uber shader, text system fonts, first frame built, encoded, presented, GPU-finished and shown, first frame with every glyph drawn, input ready; then quit start and hooks, window close, hide, the windows and their GPU dropped, loop exited), plus glyph totals before the first present (glyphs, GPU fills, rasterise and outline-build time in nanoseconds). An app reads `marks()`, `metrics()` and `notes()` to write a launch trace, and adds its own steps with `mark`.
