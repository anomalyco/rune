- **Slow interactions report themselves, by page and control.** With `RUNE_PERF=1`, or `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT` on loopback, every input that runs a handler is timed from arrival to present and named by page, control and action (`settings`, `settings/toolbar/save`, `on_press`), with ids and prose normalized away and typed text never sent. One over 16.7 ms (`RUNE_PERF_SLOW_MS`) goes to the local collector at once as a `ui.interaction` trace: queue, handler, view, commit, layout, text, paint, apply, encode and present wait, the commit reasons, and the keyed subtrees it rebuilt. The rest count toward a per-signature `ui.rollup` sent each minute. Off, a frame checks one cached flag; headless runs need `RUNE_PERF=1`. `examples/slow_click` shows one and `examples/interaction_cost` measures the cost.
