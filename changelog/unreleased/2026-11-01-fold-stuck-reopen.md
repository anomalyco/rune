- **A fold opened again as it shuts from its stuck header no longer jumps.** Reopening an `El::fold_glide` row while its contents rode up under the held header used to glide to the window's edge and then snap: the contents under the header jumped by hundreds of points as the part cut above came back below. Now the contents ride back down on the fold's spring, and what went above comes back above as the glide lands, out of sight, with the viewport's offset taking it up. At the end of a timeline, where the header had started coming down, the fold grows from its bottom instead, the header rising back to the top on the same spring.
