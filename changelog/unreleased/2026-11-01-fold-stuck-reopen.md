- **A fold opened again as it shuts from its stuck header no longer jumps.** Reopening an `El::fold_glide` row while its contents rode up under the held header used to glide to the window's edge and then snap: the contents under the header jumped by hundreds of points as the part cut above came back below. Now the contents ride back down on the fold's spring, and what went above comes back above as the glide lands, out of sight, with the viewport's offset taking it up. At the end of a timeline, where the header had started coming down, the fold grows from its bottom instead, the header rising back to the top on the same spring. A lifted fold's contents also stop at its top now, rather than showing over the rows above it for a few frames while the header came down or the fold reopened.
- **A long fold shut from its stuck header at the end of a timeline is one motion.** What showed of it used to ride its bottom edge up for two or three frames, stop dead when the timeline's end held the row below, and leave the header to come down over it alone, so the steps it covered landed out of sight. Now the header gives way from the start, coming down the share of the shut the end can't take, so the bottom edge eases into its rest on the fold's own spring while the header comes down, and the two land together.
