- **macOS: visible idle rest (`REST_AFTER`) now polls once non-blockingly to retire completed Metal command buffers.** Previously `Host::give_back(false)` and `OverlayCore::give_back(false)` skipped `Device::poll(Poll)` unless oversized staging chunks were in flight, leaving the final presented frame(s) of a resize or animation burst pinned in the Metal backend's retire queue (holding their `MTLCommandBuffer`, `CAMetalDrawable` IOSurface reference, and `Keep` texture/buffer references) indefinitely while the window rested.
