- Secondary presses and context-menu keys (`Menu` / `Shift+F10`): disabled (`El::disabled`) and `El::aria_disabled` text inputs (including those with `El::on_edit` or inside a disabled ancestor) no longer resolve as secondary targets, open the default or `App::on_field_menu` field menu, or fire their own `on_secondary_press` / `on_pill_secondary`, falling through to enabled ancestors instead while keeping `El::read_only` inputs unaffected.
