- Hover groups: a member can follow its group on curves of its own (`El::group_motion`), so one revealed element fades while the others cut, as a CSS `transition` on that element does; `El::hover_group_pointer` is a group only the pointer lights (keyboard focus inside it no longer does), and `El::group_focus_within(key)` shows a member while keyboard focus is on or inside its ancestor with that key (CSS `group-focus-visible/<name>`). A control still shows for its own focus. Existing groups and members are unchanged.
