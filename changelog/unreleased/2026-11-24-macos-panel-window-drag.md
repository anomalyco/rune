- macOS: `El::window_drag()` inside ordinary windows adopted by `Panel::adopt_window` (`MacWindowOptions::nonactivating_panel(true)` and `WindowOptions::launcher_panel()`) now starts the native window drag on the adopted `NSPanel` (`Win::drag_window` dispatches through `Panel::drag_window`, and `panel_bridge` forwards `performWindowDragWithEvent:` for ordinary adopted panels) instead of targeting winit's hidden source `NSWindow` (`examples/launcher` and `crates/rune/examples/window-panel.rs` mark their panel drag regions).
