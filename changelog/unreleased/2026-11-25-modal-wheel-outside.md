- Modals: added `El::modal_wheel_outside(bool)` (default `false`) so a dismissible modal (`El::modal` / `El::modal_tx`) can close on an outside non-momentum wheel event and repost that wheel to the scroll viewport or `on_wheel` handler beneath once the dismissal commits and rebuilds hit-testing (dropped if the modal refuses to close or a new modal replaces it; respects outer modals and inert targets).
