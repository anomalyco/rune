- Windows: a window's swap chain has a third buffer at frame latency 1, so a frame needn't wait for the compositor to give back the one it showed: scrolling and animation present at the display's rate (240 Hz on an Intel Iris Xe, where two buffers gave 120). `FrameEncoded::paced` says a frame waited for swap-chain room, so the gaps between paced frames are the present interval. `RUNE_PRESENT_LOG=<file>` logs each present's DXGI frame statistics and each waitable signal; `RUNE_SWAP_BUFFERS=2` keeps two buffers.
