- **A quit always ends, and says where it waited.** `rune::exit_deadline(after)` ends the process `after` from the call if it's still alive then, logging the last quit step reached and (on Windows) the threads still alive, and marking `quit.deadline`: for an app to arm once nothing of its own is left to lose, after its storage has closed. A quit still running 3 s after it started reports the same on stderr, every 3 s.
