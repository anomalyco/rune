- **Kit's `Spinner` is OpenCode's Soft Radar.** The old 4×4 pulse grid is replaced by `@opencode/ui`'s activity spinner: a 5×5 grid of dots whose lit head sweeps clockwise around each square ring every 1.8 s, leaving a fading trail, with the centre dot steady. Dots, keyframes and resting poses are computed as `spinner-radar.ts` does, every spinner on screen shares one phase on the wall clock, and under reduced motion each dot holds its frozen pose. `TabStrip`'s and `Sidebar`'s busy marks draw it. `Loader` takes a `color`.
