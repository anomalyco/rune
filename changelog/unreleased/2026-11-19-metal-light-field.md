- **`rune-light` shadows, contact ambient occlusion, and GI work on Rune's native Metal backend.** On macOS and iOS with `metal-backend`, compute pipelines did not bind threadgroup memory for WGSL `var<workgroup>` globals, leaving `rune-light`'s distance-field pass with an empty tile list that wrote its `256.0` clamp value to every texel. Cast shadows vanished, god-ray shafts passed unblocked through occluders, contact AO around raised surfaces disappeared, and GI radiance-cascade marches skipped over emitters and cards. Compute pipelines now bind their workgroup memory lengths on Metal so `rune-light` matches the `wgpu` backend.
