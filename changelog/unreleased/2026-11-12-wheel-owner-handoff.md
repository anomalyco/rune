- **Wheel: a delta queued before the next gesture still reaches the handler that took the gesture.** When a wheel or trackpad gesture began while deltas of the last one waited for `on_wheel` handlers, the first of those to run handed the wheel lock to the new gesture and reset its owner, so a later queued delta outside the handler scrolled the viewport under the pointer instead of reaching the handler that owned the gesture. The lock's owner and whether it belongs to the gesture arriving now are one `WheelRun` (`Arriving`/`Previous`) instead of a gesture number beside the owner, and the owner only moves on with the arriving gesture's own first delta, so the queued deltas finish with the owner the gesture picked.
