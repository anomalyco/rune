- **Wheel: a delta queued before the next gesture still reaches the handler that took the gesture.** When a wheel or trackpad gesture began while deltas of the last one waited for `on_wheel` handlers, the first of those to run handed the wheel lock to the new gesture and reset its owner, so a later queued delta outside the handler scrolled the viewport under the pointer instead of reaching the handler that owned the gesture. The lock's owner and the gesture it belongs to are one `WheelRun` (`Arriving`, or `Previous { gesture, owner }`) instead of a gesture number beside the owner: every queued delta keeps its own gesture's owner, however many gestures began since, and it scrolls on its own gesture's axis policy rather than the newcomer's.
