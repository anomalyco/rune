- **Web: a spinner's step patches its dots and redraws only where they are.** The ordinary paint records the instance each eligible dot draws (a hidden, decorative leaf with one plain fill and an opacity cycle, `runtime/paint/cycle.rs`). On the loop's own wake, with nothing else due, `Runtime::paint_cycle_tick` samples the cycles into those instances: no view, layout, paint or accessibility mirror runs, and the app isn't polled (as on a resting window, its timers, wakes and input each take an ordinary frame). The renderer writes only those instances and redraws only the rectangles they draw in, with only the instances that reach them, into the frame it keeps, which it copies to the canvas (`Renderer::arm_cycle`, the Windows damage path, now on the web too). Pixels are the same as a whole frame's.
