- **A `Tx::scroll_center` glide stops once its target is removed for good.** Removing the element it was centring used to leave the glide running to where the element had been; now, when the target is neither mounted in the viewport nor a row of a virtual list there at two frame ends running, the glide comes to rest where it is. A virtual row out of the list's window, an element remounted under its key on the next frame, and an exit undone on the next frame keep the glide going as before.
