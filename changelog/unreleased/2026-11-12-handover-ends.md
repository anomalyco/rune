- **Windows: a CPU-first window's hand-over to the GPU always ends, and says how.** A window covered from launch handed over only once it was uncovered (4,197 ms in the repro, never while it stayed behind another window); it now draws the frame both renderers share in sight or not (308 ms). Every step has a deadline: a GPU thread still making the GPU after 500 ms runs at normal priority, the shared frame is asked for every 100 ms until drawn, and a GPU that doesn't draw or finish it in 5 s fails the hand-over. A failure is logged with its reason (`gpu.window.failed`, note `gpu.window.why`) and tried again after 30 s, three tries in all, then the window stays on the CPU.
