- **`Cursor::ResizeEw`, `Cursor::ResizeNs`, `Cursor::ResizeNwse`, and `Cursor::ResizeNesw`: bidirectional and diagonal resize cursors (CSS `ew-resize`, `ns-resize`, `nwse-resize`, `nesw-resize`).** Mapped across winit (Windows, X11, Wayland, macOS, and web CSS), Wayland `cursor-shape-v1` layer-shell overlays, and macOS AppKit (`frameResizeCursorFromPosition:inDirections:` on macOS 15+ with fallback to legacy/private selectors before arrow); `examples/sketch` shows them in its handle strip and `--scene handles`.
