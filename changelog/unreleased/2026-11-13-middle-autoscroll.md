- **Windows and Linux: a middle click autoscrolls, as Chromium does.** A middle press that no element takes, over a viewport that can scroll, scrolls it toward the pointer on each axis it scrolls on: nothing within 15 points of where the press landed, then 0.04·d^2.2 points a second (Blink's curve, measured in Chromium at a 1x raster: 29 at 20 points, 616 at 80, 4,632 at 200). Released after the pointer left that radius it stops; released inside it, it keeps going until the next press of any button or any key, which it takes. The pointer shows Chromium's pan cursors (`pan_*`). It asks for frames only while it moves something: a pointer resting inside the radius, or a viewport at its end, wakes nothing.
