- **Kit: a torn-out tab can carry its drag into a window of its own.** `TabStrip::on_tear_move` reports each move of a torn-out tab while the button is down, and its return to the strip, with the pointer in window points and in desktop pixels and where the press landed in the tab, so an app can open the tab's window under the pointer and move it there. `TabDragState::hand_off` stops the strip drawing the tab while that window shows it, until the gesture ends or the tab docks again. `TabTearOut::screen_point` is the pointer in desktop pixels now (`Drag::screen_point`), the space `WindowOptions::position` takes, and `window_point` is always set.
