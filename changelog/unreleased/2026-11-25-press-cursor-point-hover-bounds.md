- Pointer & hover: added opt-in `Cx::press_cursor(Cursor)` for a view-wide default cursor on pressable elements (winning over inherited ancestor cursors while keeping explicit `.cursor(..)`, `Cursor::Default` on disabled controls, and `Cursor::Text` on text fields), `Tx::press_point() -> Option<PointerEvent>` for local and window coordinates on pointer-driven `on_press_tx` / `on_background_press_tx` / `on_double_press_tx` / `on_middle_press` activations (`None` on keyboard or assistive-technology activation), and `El::hover_tracks_bounds()` so `on_hover_change` and hover/press paint tracks follow element bounds and coordinate-less window leave during a held press without ending capture.
