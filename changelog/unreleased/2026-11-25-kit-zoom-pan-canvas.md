- **`rune-kit` adds `Canvas` and `ZoomPan`, a zoom/pan pane for any content.** A tree, diagram or chart lays out at its own size and draws scaled in a clipped pane: a background drag pans, Ctrl or ⌘ + wheel (or a pinch) zooms about the pointer, a plain wheel pans overflowing content, a double-click fits, and `+`/`-`/`0` step and fit while the pane has focus. The view fits the content until a hand touches it and refits on resize only until then. `.min_scale`, `.max_scale`, `.padding` and `.grows` set the limits; `ZoomPan::scale` and `Canvas::scaled(lens, size, |scale| ..)` let content drop detail that would be unreadable; `ZoomPan::to_content`/`to_viewport` map pane points through the transform (#720).
