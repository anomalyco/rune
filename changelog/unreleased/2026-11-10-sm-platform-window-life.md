- **A window's show, open and launcher reveal are one transition, and its life one state (`Life`: not shown yet, shown, hidden, closed for good):** `AppHandle::open` and `activate` now read the current accent and scroller style before building what changed while the window was hidden, as `show` did; opening a covered window lands the motion it missed, as showing one did; `show` on a window already on screen no longer lands its running motion; and a launcher's reveal hands a resting wake's timers back as every other show does. A window hidden without `keep_running` that let its native window go (`App::release_gpu_when_hidden`, or any hidden window on Wayland) now runs `on_close` and closes on a close, as one that kept its window does. A window closed for good runs its `on_close` once, even when that hook asks to close again (`--desktop` ran it up to eight times).
