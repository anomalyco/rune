- Windows 11 (`windows-system-material`) and Linux Wayland `El::system_material`: an existing material element with an opaque fill (`alpha >= 1.0`) is now drawn opaque and unblurred (since #2349 on Windows and since Wayland blur landed) so themed opaque surfaces such as `rune-kit::Toolbar` stay solid and idle—drop `.fill(...)` to use the material's own tint or make the fill translucent (`.alpha(...)`) to tint over the blur; documented the three-case fill rule and WinUI 3 light-theme dark-text guidance on `El::system_material`, `SystemMaterial`, and `crates/rune/README.md`.
