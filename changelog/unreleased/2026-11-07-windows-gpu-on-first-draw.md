- **A windowless Windows app touches no GPU at launch.** Rune no longer asks whether a DX12 adapter exists as the event loop starts; the DX12-or-Vulkan choice is made on the first GPU use, once per process, by a surface, an overlay's show, `warm_gpu` or `Gpu::headless`. Enumerating DX12 adapters makes a temporary device on every adapter, and NVIDIA's App takes a process that makes a GPU device as a launched game, which stopped Instant Replay for a tray app that only sits in the notification area (hex#1308). A cold first show without `warm_gpu` now pays the DX12 check, roughly 150 ms more; `warm_gpu` makes it ahead of the show.
