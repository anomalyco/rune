- A secret copy's early clear (`clear_now`, `Tx::clear_secret_clipboard`) on a clipboard with a change count (macOS, Windows) no longer gives up when the wipe is refused or the clipboard can't be read for a moment: the copy keeps holding and its delay tries again, after checking the clipboard hasn't changed. It used to report `Cleared` (refused wipe) or `Replaced` (unreadable) and leave the secret there. The delay's own try, and any try where there's no change count (Linux), gives up as before.
