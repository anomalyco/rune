- **`rune-kit` `MarkdownStyle` adds opt-in table column rules, body-row hover, and widest-first column shrinking.** `MarkdownStyle::table_column_rules(Option<Color>)` draws a 0.5-point vertical hairline at the leading edge of every column after the first (on the right edge in RTL tables) without changing column widths; `MarkdownStyle::table_row_hover(Option<Color>)` draws a hover overlay over body rows (never the header); and `MarkdownStyle::table_shrink(TableShrink::WidestFirst)` (re-exported as `rune_kit::TableShrink`, defaulting to `TableShrink::Proportional`) narrows the widest columns first across both flex and known-width scrolling layouts while preserving proportional widths when extra room is available. All three are off by default.
