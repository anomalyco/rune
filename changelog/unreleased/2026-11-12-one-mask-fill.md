- **Text: an atlas mask is the same bytes whether the CPU or the GPU fills it.** The fill's shader rounds the coverage to the R8 target's levels itself, ties to even, instead of leaving it to the GPU's float-to-unorm conversion, which rounded down texels just above a half level (0.06% of texels on Intel, 0.8% on NVIDIA). `text::outline::fill_mask` is the CPU's fill, with the fill instance's arithmetic and the shader's per-piece integrals: measured against both GPUs (`Renderer::fill_drift`), the same bytes except texels within about 1e-4 of a rounding boundary (0 to 0.006%, one level). It integrates only the pieces that cross each texel, 4.7x faster than the per-texel form. The glyph atlas pages can be read back (`COPY_SRC`).
