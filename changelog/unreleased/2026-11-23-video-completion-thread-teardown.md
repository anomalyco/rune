- **Native video surfaces stop and join their completion thread before releasing the GPU device.** `SurfaceRenderer` previously left its `rune-video-completion` worker detached and dropped its `wgpu::Device` first, letting an in-flight `Device::poll` race device or driver teardown (segfaulting on Vulkan/NVIDIA at exit). `SurfaceRenderer` now drops its timing queue and stops and joins the completion worker in `Drop`, handling idle shutdown, in-flight polls, lingering callback wakers, lost devices, panics, and re-entrant drops without polling after teardown.
