- **A link in text can have its own tooltip.** `Link::help(text)` gives one link of a text a
  tooltip, as `El::help` gives an element: it shows after the hover delay while the pointer rests
  on that link's glyphs, under the link, and a press, scroll, key or leaving the link hides it.
  On macOS it registers as an AppKit tooltip over the link's line boxes. Kit's
  `Markdown::link_help(|url| …)` sets one per link by destination (where it goes, say), for
  links with a handler. The element tree reports it as the link's help.
