- **A scroll viewport's vertical motion is one explicit mode.** `ScrollState` keeps a `ScrollMode` (at rest, pinned to its bottom, coasting on momentum, or gliding to the bottom, an offset or a shrinking stack's end) in place of a velocity, a pinned flag, a glide velocity, a glide target and a shrink-glide flag, so a viewport can no longer follow its bottom while it glides or coast while a glide carries it. Hands, apps, reveals, keeps and touches land a viewport through one transition (pinned again only at its end), and the last drawn step's direction carries across a glide landing on a pinned end, so its settle holds its pixel from the first frame. No output changes in OpenCode (Rune)'s motion gate cells for scrolling, jump to latest, streaming and queue landings.
