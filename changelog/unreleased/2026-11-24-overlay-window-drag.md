- Overlays: window-backed overlays now honour `El::window_drag()` and `El::no_window_drag()` (mouse press and touch drag past slop), starting the native move loop (`performWindowDragWithEvent:` on the macOS `NSPanel`, `Window::drag_window()` on Windows and X11), ignoring titlebar double-clicks, clearing native pointer capture and drag-out state when a drag starts, refreshing Windows mouse-hook geometry on moves, and keeping a user-dragged overlay's position across shows, resizes and pointer following until the app sends a new placement request (`examples/overlay-lab` adds a draggable grip to its pill).
