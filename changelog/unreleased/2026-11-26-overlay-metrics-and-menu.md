- **rune-kit overlay metrics follow `Kit::radius` and `Kit::metrics`, and `Menu` themes/submenus inherit menu spacing.** Themes whose `Kit::radius` differs from the default scale (`xs: 2, sm: 4, md: 6`) now see `Dialog`, compact `Menu`, `Select` listbox, and `PopupList` corners follow `k.radius.{sm,md}` automatically (default themes remain bit-identical). `Kit::metrics` adds `dialog` (`DialogMetrics`), `popup_row` (`PopupRowMetrics`), `select` (`SelectMetrics`), `popover` (`PopoverMetrics`), and `menu` (`MenuMetrics`: `padding`, `standard_padding`, `separator_above`, `separator_below`, `standard_separator_above`, `standard_separator_below`, `standard_inner_border`). Open submenus now inherit their parent `Menu`'s `.padding(..)`, `.separator_spacing(..)`, and `.inner_border(..)` overrides (changing #2372's default-look submenu behaviour), non-finite floats clamp to `0.0`, and blank `Menu::separator_keyed` keys fall back to positional `sep{i}`.
