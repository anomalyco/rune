- Runtime: `AppSpec::poll` now runs again in the same frame after non-`live_only` event handlers (alternating with input queued by `Tx::send_key` up to `MAX_POLL_ROUNDS` = 4 follow-up rounds before commit), so work queued by handlers and `Latest::request_if_changed` called from `poll` execute immediately without scheduling a wake. Apps whose `poll` counts calls per frame will observe the follow-up call on frames where a non-`live_only` handler ran; idle frames, animation frames, `Tx::live_only` handlers, layout observers, and apps without `poll` are unaffected. `Tx::request_poll()` now asks for another round of the same frame's loop when called during a frame (such as from a `Tx::live_only` handler) and schedules the next turn when called outside a frame.
