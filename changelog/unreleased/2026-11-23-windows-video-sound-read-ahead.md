- Windows video: a clip's own sound is no longer queued too late to play at 60 pictures a second and while the clip plays on its layer. The decoder read sound and pictures as they came in the file and only while the picture queue had room, so the sound was never more than three pictures (50 ms at 60 fps; one picture on the layer) ahead of the clock, less than the sound output writes ahead, and every sample came too late. It now reads each stream by its own index: the sound until a second is queued whether or not a picture is wanted, a seek's first picture before its sound, and never more than 8 sound samples between pictures. A clip that hasn't opened yet keeps the sound its decoder was asked for, instead of closing the sound output and opening it again.
