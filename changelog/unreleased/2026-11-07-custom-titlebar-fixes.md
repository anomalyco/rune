- **Windows: the custom titlebar's caption buttons, window menu and frame behave as native ones do.** Alt+Space opens the window menu from the message loop when no handler takes it, and never types a space into a focused field (`KeyEvent` Alt+Space types nothing off macOS). A press on a caption button holds the mouse until its release: dragging off cancels it, dragging back resumes it, no other button lights up meanwhile, and no release leaves a button stuck pressed. The buttons stay in the accessibility tree and act while the view holds a modal, in a frame of Rune's own that no border, clip or transform on the view's root moves. A window that isn't resizable never maximizes (no maximize button on any platform): its maximize button is dimmed with no snap layouts, and one that can neither minimize nor maximize shows only Close. A move to a display with another scale resizes the window once; maximizing onto another display fits that display and its auto-hiding taskbar; dragging an edge draws each size at once; Windows 10 keeps its top border. `Cx::titlebar_height(h)` changes the band's height as the app runs, and the caption buttons take it, so they line up with the app's row at every UI zoom; memos rebuild when `Cx::chrome_insets` changes, the playground's staged stories no longer take the window's insets or caption colour, and hot reload keeps the titlebar.
