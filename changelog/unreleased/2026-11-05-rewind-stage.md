- **Rewind's scrubber opens with a slow-down: the window eases to a stop, steps back onto a dark stage, and the track rises beneath it.** `Scrub::stage(app, lens, on_send)` replaces laying `Scrub::view` over the app: it runs the app's clock down from full speed to zero over about half a second (`Scrub::app_speed`), shrinks the frozen window with rounded corners and a shadow so the bar sits below it rather than over it, and outlines what moved on the shrunk window. Closing reverses it. Call `Scrub::poll(now, tx)` from the app's poll; the motion runs on the window's clock, and `Scrub::settle` jumps it to its end; it asks for frames while the scrubber opens or closes and resumes recording only once the window is back in place, so the step back isn't recorded. Elements keyed `rewind…` are never recorded.
