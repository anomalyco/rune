- **Linux: dmabuf importer stops and joins its read thread before releasing the GPU device.** `native_surface::linux::Importer` previously spawned a detached `rune-dmabuf-read` thread and dropped its `wgpu::Device` first, letting an in-flight or queued `Device::poll` race Vulkan device teardown. `Importer` and `SurfaceRenderer` now stop and join the read worker in `Drop` before releasing the device, discarding unpolled items on shutdown and exiting cleanly on device loss, panics, and re-entrant drops.
