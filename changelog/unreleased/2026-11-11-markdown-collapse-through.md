- **Markdown: a quote's and a list's edge margins collapse through them, as CSS margins do.** A quote or list has no vertical padding or border, so its first child's top margin and its last child's bottom margin (and a list item's gap) pass out of it: a quote that ends a list item now spaces the list 16 from what follows rather than the list's 12, and items either side of one ending or starting in a quote sit 16 apart rather than the list gap (`render::collapsed`).
