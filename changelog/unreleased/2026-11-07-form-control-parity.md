- **Select, TextInput and Switch match `@opencode/ui`'s form controls.** A focused `TextInput`, `TextArea` or field `Select` draws its 1 point `border_focus` ring just inside the box (`outline-offset: -1px`) rather than outside it, and the field `Select` shows it whenever focus is in the closed field; the inline `Select` shows none, as on the web. Text fields lose the white sheen the web doesn't draw. `Switch::web()` draws the compact switch as the web does: a 24 x 16 track with 4 point corners and a 12 point thumb. Forced hover and press colours mix in sRGB in a window that blends in sRGB, so a hovered dark field is the web's shade instead of a much lighter one.
