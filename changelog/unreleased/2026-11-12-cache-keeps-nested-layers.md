- **A cached subtree keeps the layers inside it that the window can't see yet.** When an `El::cache` texture is drawn, the isolated layers inside it (a faded row, a turned icon) are drawn against the cache's whole content, not the window or the cache's own clip, so a later hit that moves, turns or clips the cache differently shows them as a fresh draw would. Before, a layer offscreen or clipped out on the frame that filled the texture stayed missing after it scrolled or turned into view.
