- **What a scroll carries is measured in the window.** `ElementInfo::scrolled`, and the `sc` a `--trace` writes, were the scroll viewports' offsets in their own points, while an element's `rect` is in the window after every `El::scale` and `El::rotate` around them. Inside a 1.6× zoom the motion gate and `scripts/motion-audit/analyze.py` took back about 1/1.6 of the scroll, so a row that only rose on screen and only moved down its list read as a dip and climb and reported `reversal`. The offsets are now scaled and turned as the viewport's content draws (a zoom inside the viewport scales about a point the scroll carries, so it doesn't count). A real wobble in a zoomed list is still reported.
