- **`App::font_url_later` adds a font a web app fetches after its first frame**, for faces the first frame doesn't need (one a setting picks, a terminal's): no download size or start time on the page; until it arrives, text in its family sets in the slot's fallback, then every window takes it up on its next frame. Native reads it at once, as `App::font_url`. On web, an app whose `AppSpec::fonts` bring Inter (or IBM Plex Mono) no longer gets the embedded faces of that family registered beside its own, nor the full Inter faces fetched after its first frame and swapped in over its own at weights 400, 500 and 600: its text sets in its fonts, as natively.
