- Colour-only `Span`s stay out of `text_hash` and the shaped-layout cache key, and resolve each glyph's ink from sorted disjoint span intervals at emission (before mask dilation and gamma lookup), so unstyled text with search highlights or syntax tokens reuses existing shaped and truncated layouts without reshaping; a colour range that overlaps a layout-affecting span (`weight`, `mono`, `size`, `italic`, `bg`, `underline`) still splits that span's edges in the key when flattened.
