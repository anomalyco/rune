- **A middle click is an event of its own (`El::on_middle_press`), and a middle click on a link opens it.** `on_middle_press` runs when the middle button goes down on an element (or one inside it that doesn't take the button) and comes up over it, as a browser's `auxclick`: a tab closes, a row opens in the background, without branching `on_press` on `Tx::button` or keeping `on_drag` from hearing the button (`El::pointer_buttons` stays for canvases and terminals that want the button's drags). A text's links hear the middle button too: `on_link` runs with `Tx::button` `Middle`, so an app opens the address as a browser opens it in a new tab, or ignores it. Kit's `TabStrip` closes a tab through it, and `rune-luau` nodes take `onMiddlePress` (a `middle-press` event).
