- **The motion gate's pixel check no longer reports a glide's last fractions of a point as snaps.** Pixel snapping draws a list settling its scroll (or any box gliding under 0.25 pt a frame) as an occasional whole-pixel step, and the region check read each step as a `snap` or `changes-twice` because no element had moved enough to cover it. Any measurable motion of an element now covers its cells.
