- **The motion gate's pixel check no longer reports a glide's last fractions of a point as snaps.** Pixel snapping draws a list settling its scroll (or any box gliding under 0.25 pt a frame) as an occasional whole-pixel step, and the region check read each step as a `snap` or `changes-twice` because no element had moved enough to cover it. Any measurable motion of an element now covers its cells.
- **The motion gate's raster no longer counts an icon's filtering apron as ink.** Off the pixel grid an icon's quad grows one transparent texel each side; on the grid it doesn't. The raster read the apron as coverage, so an icon gliding through a whole pixel showed as a `flash` in the cells around it. While the gate records, the runtime notes which icons it drew through their apron (`icon_aprons`), and the raster leaves those texels out; text glyphs keep their whole quad.
- **The motion gate leaves Rune's own tooltip out of its pixel checks.** An `El::help` tooltip shows at once after the pointer rests, as AppKit's does, so its frame has no input; the region check read it as a `snap`. `Recording::tips` now holds each frame's tooltip box and the region check skips those cells.
- **The motion gate's pixel check lets a field's words land at once without a key.** Words set in a field from a poll (dictation, a restored draft) or cleared after a send read as a region `snap`, because a field's words were left out of the words that change. `Track::edits` now records them, and the region check skips the field's cells on those frames; `text-swap` still ignores fields.
