- Added opt-in `GpuLossPolicy::Terminal` (default `GpuLossPolicy::Recover`) via `App::gpu_loss_policy` and `WindowSpec::gpu_loss_policy`. Under `Terminal`, GPU device loss, D3D12 device removal, uncaptured `wgpu::Error`s, initialization failure, or failed same-device surface configuration permanently latches the first reason on `AppHandle::window_error()`, keeps open windows frozen on their last frame and closable, wakes `AppSpec::poll` once without redrawing, drops GPU resources via `Compositor::release_gpu`, tears down owned `App::overlay` overlays with `Availability::Unavailable`, and refuses all device replacement or CPU fallback (including on reopen and on `wasm32`).
