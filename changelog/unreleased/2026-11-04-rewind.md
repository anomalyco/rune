- **Rewind: step back through the last minute of a window's frames.** `rune::rewind` is an
  opt-in recorder for catching one-frame glitches after they happen. Attach a `Rewind` with
  `Tx::record_rewind(Some(&rewind))` and each presented frame records the rect and opacity of
  every keyed or trace-named element that changed, delta encoded, in a ring that keeps the last
  60 seconds within 24 MB (`Rewind::seconds`, `Rewind::max_bytes`). Nothing is attached by
  default, and then a frame only checks for it. `Rewind::freeze` pauses it and returns a `Clip`:
  any frame's elements, what changed in each frame, an element's path, and jumps (a move of
  4 pt or more, over 2.5× what it moved in the frames either side). `Scrub` is a ready-made
  scrubber to lay over the app's view (with the app's subtree at `El::time_scale(0.0)`): the
  moved elements outlined and labelled ("title ↓7.5 pt"), a track that lights up where things
  moved and turns red at jumps, ←/→ to step, ⇧ to select, ⌥ to go to the next frame that moved,
  a note, and Send. `Clip::write_bundle` writes a stretch as a folder: a motion-lint
  `trace.jsonl`, a `contact.png` drawn from the recorded rects, and a `summary.md`. It records
  geometry, not pixels: reading frames back from the GPU would stall it. On a 400-row streaming
  transcript (`examples/rewind_cost.rs`), recording adds under 0.01 ms to a frame's median and
  holds 0.8 MB after a minute.
