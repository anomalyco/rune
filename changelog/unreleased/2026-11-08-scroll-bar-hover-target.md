- **A scroll bar can show while the pointer is over another element, and run its track in a column's gutter, as `@opencode/ui` ScrollView's `thumbHoverTarget` and `thumbContainer` do.** `ScrollBar::scroll_bar_hover(viewport_key)` makes an element (a timeline's root that also holds a button floating over it) reveal that viewport's overlay bar while the pointer is anywhere over it, so the thumb stays up while the pointer is on the button; with `ScrollBarStyle::hover_target` only that element and the thumb reveal it, not the viewport itself. `ScrollBarStyle::track_offset(top, end)` starts the track further down the viewport and ends its strip in from the end edge, the thumb sized to what's left, for a page whose thumb sits beside one column under its sticky header. Any hover handler the target has keeps running.
