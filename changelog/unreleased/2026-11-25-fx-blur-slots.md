- **`rune-fx` frosted backdrop blur supports 255+ frosted lenses per frame inside a bounded 64 KiB uniform pool.** `FxRenderer::new` writes constant `1.0` once into `blur_uniforms` slot `0`, `blur_backdrop` reuses slot `0` for every non-crossfade pass and deduplicates per-frame crossfade weights across slots `1..256` (`BLUR_SLOTS = 256`, up from `64`), so frames with more than ~10 frosted lenses blur all of them evenly regardless of draw order while frames within the old limit draw bit-identically and layers past 255 distinct fractional weights still fall back gracefully to the 8-tap in-shader blur.
