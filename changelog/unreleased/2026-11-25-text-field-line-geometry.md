- Text fields: added `Runtime::text_line(key, byte)` and `Tx::text_line(key, byte, f)`, which read a `text_input`'s visual line holding a byte, focused or not, from the last layout: a `TextLine` with its box in window points (after scrolling) and the first bytes of it and the visual lines before and after it, in the field's model text; while an IME composes, the visual lines the composition covers read as one line. With `text_offset_at` for the reverse (point to byte), an app can align a gutter, per-line notes or rules with the field's own lines.
