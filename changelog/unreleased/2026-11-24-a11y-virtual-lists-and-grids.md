- Accessibility: non-item rows (such as headings) in `virtual_rows` lists no longer receive `position_in_set`; `El::position_in_set` only sets a container's `size_of_set` on ordered-set containers (never on `row`, `grid`, or `table`) and preserves the shallowest hierarchical level's size in flat trees; added `El::row_count`, `El::column_count`, `El::row_index`, and `El::column_index` mapped to AccessKit and `accessibility_json`, with `virtual_rows` directly on a `grid` or `table` populating `row_count` and row `row_index` instead of set positions.
