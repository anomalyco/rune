- **`TabStrip`'s edges fade, and an icon-only tab's badge sits in a cutout, as Desktop's tab strips do.** Side tabs feather the top and bottom by as much as is left to scroll past each, up to the fade width (`ScrollFade::feather`, Desktop's `--vertical-tabs-fade-*` mask), and titlebar tabs show their 24 point linear fade at an edge once anything is past it (Desktop's `titlebar-tabs-fade-*`), instead of a strength that eased in over 15 points. An icon-only tab's status dot sits on its avatar's corner cut clear by `El::cutout_circle` (`radial-gradient(circle 4.5px at 100% 0, transparent 4px, black 5px)`) rather than on a disc of the tab's colour.
