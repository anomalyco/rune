- Apps can read each frame's `El::system_material` rects, so they can drive their own backdrop under them where Rune hosts no native material (Windows, Linux). `MaterialDraw` (element id, material, rect and corner radius in physical pixels, continuous corners, opacity, and `points(scale)`) is now public, `Runtime::system_materials` lists the last built frame's, and `ConfiguredOverlay::on_materials` hears every presented overlay frame's (`MaterialFrame`: its draws and window scale) just before it presents. An `El::cache()` holding a material element now draws live on every platform, not only with native materials, so its rect stays listed on frames where the cache would have reused its raster. Cost: on Windows, Linux and the web, a cached subtree containing a fallback-only material now repaints its whole subtree on every frame the window draws, instead of compositing its saved raster, even in apps that never read the rects; the pixels are unchanged. Move material elements out of large cached subtrees if that repaint shows up.
