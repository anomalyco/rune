- Apps can read each frame's `El::system_material` rects, so they can drive their own backdrop under them where Rune hosts no native material (Windows, Linux). `MaterialDraw` (element id, material, rect and corner radius in physical pixels, continuous corners, opacity, and `points(scale)`) is now public, `Runtime::system_materials` lists the last built frame's, and `ConfiguredOverlay::on_materials` hears every presented overlay frame's (`MaterialFrame`: its draws and window scale) just before it presents. An `El::cache()` holding a fallback-only material element still reuses its cached raster while replaying its recorded material draws (translated and scaled with the layer, with zero steady-state allocations) into the frame's material list.
