- **Right-to-left windows mirror their frame, tooltips and inline icons.** When the app's language reads right to left (`labels::set_direction`), Windows' caption buttons sit at the window's top-left with Close first, as Windows, Chrome and Edge mirror them, and `Cx::chrome_insets` reports their room as `leading`. A tooltip in a right-to-left app reads from the right, its keycaps after its words, and right-to-left words now draw inside the tooltip rather than off its edge. An inline icon (`El::inline_icon`, a link's favicon) in right-to-left text keeps its gap between the icon and its word.
