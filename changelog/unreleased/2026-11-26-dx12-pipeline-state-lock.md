- Windows DX12: vendored `wgpu-hal` now serializes raw `ID3D12Device2::CreatePipelineState`, `ID3D12Device::CreateGraphicsPipelineState`, and `ID3D12Device::CreateComputePipelineState` FFI calls behind a process-wide mutex, preventing an access violation (`0xC0000005` reading `0x8` at `dxilconv.dll+0xB28E5`) caused by an unlocked `llvm::PassRegistry` `DenseMap` race when `D3D12Core.dll` converts DXBC shaders to DXIL across concurrent threads on a cold shader cache.
