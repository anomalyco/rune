- **rune-kit `Kit::metrics` covers controls and containers, and `Switch::flat()` completes flat switch chrome.** `SegmentedControl`, `Collapsible` triggers and `Keybind` keycaps now read `k.radius.md`, `k.radius.sm` and `k.radius.xs` by default (bit-identical for themes on the default 2/4/6 radius scale; themes with another radius scale now see those corners follow it). `Metrics` adds `segmented` (`SegmentedMetrics`), `disclosure_header_height` (for `Collapsible` and `Accordion` headers), `progress` (`ProgressMetrics`; `border_width: 0.0` omits the rim), `divider_thickness`, `keybind` (`KeybindMetrics`), `text_area_min_height`, and `standard_switch` (`StandardSwitchMetrics`, clamping pressed thumb stretch inside the track). Building on `Metrics::switch_look` (`SwitchLook`), `Switch::flat()` opts a single compact or web switch into plain fills without sheen, knob rim or shadows, and `SwitchLook::track_border` now clamps negative/non-finite widths to `0.0` and skips drawing a border when the active border colour is fully transparent.
