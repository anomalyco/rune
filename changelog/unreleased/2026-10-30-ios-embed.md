- **`rune::embed::LayerHost` and `target_os = "ios"` builds.** `LayerHost::from_core_animation_layer` mounts a `Runtime` and Metal `Renderer` (with optional `LayerHost::with_compositor` effect-slot passes) onto an existing `CAMetalLayer` (for UIKit, SwiftUI and AppKit views that own their own window and display link), sharing one `Gpu` across hosts with `embed::prewarm()`. `rune` now builds cleanly for `aarch64-apple-ios` and `aarch64-apple-ios-sim` (skipping `arboard` on iOS/Android and walking `/System/Library/Fonts` for Apple symbol, SF Mono, emoji and CJK fallbacks).
