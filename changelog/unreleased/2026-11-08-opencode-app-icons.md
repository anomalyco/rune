- **OpenCode theme: the app marks Desktop's Open in menus draw.** `rune_theme_opencode::app_icons::named(id, dark)` gives the mark for VS Code, Cursor, Zed (with its dark variant), File Explorer, PowerShell, Sublime Text, iTerm2, Ghostty, Android Studio and Antigravity, in their own colours, generated from `@opencode/ui` by `tools/app-icons.py`. The marks Desktop ships as PNGs (Finder, Terminal, TextMate, Warp, Xcode) aren't included.
