- **`Cx::mark_texts`: text stays painted as selected after the selection goes.** A `TextMark` (from `SelectedText::mark`, or a text's path, a byte range and the bytes it held) paints in that text's selection colour while the view keeps declaring it, as a CSS custom highlight styled like `::selection` does: square boxes over each line's glyphs, a line tall, without a newline's width. A mark paints only while its text still holds the marked bytes there, so a quote's source can stay lit in a transcript while its comment is written.
