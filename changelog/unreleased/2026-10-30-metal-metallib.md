- **The native Metal backend loads a precompiled uber shader.** With `metal-backend` on and the backend picked, `Renderer::new` loads `uber_native.metallib` (the MSL that backend emits, compiled ahead) instead of translating WGSL and compiling Metal source. The first renderer takes 0.9 ms and 7.1 million instructions instead of 4.0 ms and 40 million on a warm shader cache, 90 ms instead of 222 ms on a cold one (the first launch after an install or update), and each later renderer 0.2 ms instead of 2.6 ms. The WGSL path stays the fallback, and `RUNE_DISABLE_METALLIB=1` still selects it. Shader edits now need `--features metal-backend` on the freshness test, and `scripts/precompile-metal-shaders.sh` rewrites both libraries.
