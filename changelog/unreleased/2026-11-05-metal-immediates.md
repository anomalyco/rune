- **Rune's Metal backend offers `wgpu::Features::IMMEDIATES`, so a compositor that asks for immediates keeps drawing with it on macOS.** A compositor whose `Compositor::features` returned `IMMEDIATES` got a wgpu device for its window or overlay, so every frame took wgpu's slower path. The backend already sent immediates as inline vertex and fragment bytes; it now advertises the feature with a 4 KB `max_immediate_size` (what `setVertexBytes` takes), and its devices always carry it, so the prewarmed device serves such a compositor instead of being dropped. Immediates are zeroed when a pass changes pipeline layout, as wgpu does.
