- **`Thumbnails`, `ThumbnailRequest`, `Thumbnail`, `ThumbnailPrecision`, and `MediaRequest::thumbnails`: off-main-thread video still-frame extraction for clip grids and filmstrips.** Decodes requested timestamps on a shared FIFO queue capped at 4 concurrent decoders with zero idle threads and no hidden global pixel cache, writing straight into `Pixels` handles that auto-wake drawn windows, supporting `Exact` and `NearestKeyframe` precision, `max_size` downscaling (`1..=8192`, default `320`, never upscaling), ascending deduplicated decode, and drop-to-cancel. Backed by CPU Media Foundation (`IMFSourceReader`, BT.601/BT.709 NV12 to sRGB RGBA8, rotation, linear-light downsampling) on Windows; platforms without a backend yet settle immediately with a clear error instead of blank images (`examples/video-player` adds a filmstrip under the scrub bar with hover preview).
