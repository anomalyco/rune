- **Hovering a `TabStrip` tab runs no view: the title's fade, the slot's ground and the separators follow the pointer by hover groups, as the wash and close button already did.** The strip's own hover handler says `tx.live_only()`; an `on_hover` handler that changes nothing the view draws should too. Leaving the strip commits only when it lets go of a close's width lock. In OpenCode, hovering across the tabs went from a commit per frame to none, and UI-thread CPU per hover frame from 4,207 to 2,978 µs (top tabs) and 7,212 to 2,643 µs (side tabs).
