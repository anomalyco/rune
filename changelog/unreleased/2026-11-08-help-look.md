- **`Cx::help_look` gives `El::help` tooltips an app's own look, and `El::help_keys` adds keycaps.** A `HelpLook` sets the tooltip's fill, text colour, ring, radius, padding, type, box shadows, gap from its element, window margin, open delay, skip delay (just after one tooltip hides, the next shows at once) and keycap style; `HelpLook::system` is Rune's look as before. With a look set, Rune draws its tooltip on macOS too, in place of AppKit's. `.help("Toggle review").help_keys(["Ctrl", "Shift", "R"])` draws the command's keys as keycaps after its text, as a web tooltip shows a keybind; the element tree and assistive technology read them as words. rune-kit's `TooltipStyle::help_look` turns a kit tooltip into one, placed and timed as `@opencode/ui`'s `Tooltip` sets Kobalte's (4 pt gutter, 8 pt overflow padding, 400 ms, 300 ms skip) with `keybind-v2`'s neutral keys, and compact and large kit tooltips set tabular figures as `tooltip.css` does.
