- `rune-fx`: `Fx::MotionBlur` now boxes its `MotionSamples` (`Fx::MotionBlur(MotionBlurParams, Box<MotionSamples>)`), so each `Fx` is 152 bytes instead of about 2.1 KiB and each `FxLayer` is 216 bytes. About 2,400 layers used to take about 5 MB; they now take about 0.5 MB. `Fx` and `FxLayer` are no longer `Copy`. Code that reuses a layer after a by-value builder (`layer.at(t)`), or puts one in an array more than once, needs `.clone()` or a borrow (`std::slice::from_ref(&layer)`). `Fx::motion_blur` keeps its signature.
