- **`rune-fx`: `Fx::MotionBlur` boxes its `MotionSamples` (`Fx::MotionBlur(MotionBlurParams, Box<MotionSamples>)`), shrinking `Fx` from 2,104 B to 152 B and `FxLayer` from 2,164 B to 216 B (~10× smaller).** 2,400 layers drop from 5,193,600 B (~4.95 MiB) to 518,400 B (~506 KiB) with zero heap allocations for the 10 non-motion-blur variants, enforced by compile-time `size_of` assertions (`<= 168` / `<= 256`) and `crates/rune-fx/tests/alloc.rs`. `Fx` and `FxLayer` now implement `Clone` and `Debug` instead of `Copy` (`MotionSamples` and all other param structs stay `Copy`, and `Fx::motion_blur(p, s)` keeps its signature). Migration for downstream code: reuse after a by-value builder (`layer.at(t)` -> `layer.clone().at(t)`), single-layer slices (`&[layer]` -> `std::slice::from_ref(&layer)`), by-value pattern matches (`match layer.fx` -> `match &layer.fx`), and direct variant construction (`Fx::MotionBlur(p, s)` -> `Fx::motion_blur(p, s)` or `Fx::MotionBlur(p, Box::new(s))`); persistent motion-blur layers can overwrite `**samples` in place with zero per-frame allocations.
