- **macOS: a right-to-left app's traffic lights sit at the window's right.** When the app's language reads right to left (`labels::set_direction`), the traffic lights Rune places (`Chrome::traffic_lights`, a titlebar's spot) sit their `x` in from the right edge with Close outermost, as AppKit places a right-to-left localization's (measured on macOS 27: a native window's Close at 687-701 of 720 points, Rune's at 1,071-1,087 of 1,100 against 13-29 left to right). `Cx::chrome_insets` then reports their room as `trailing`, so a Kit titlebar keeps it on the right.
