- Motion: `Beat::translate(x, y)` and `Beat::rotate(radians)` let an `El::sequence` move and turn an element, like CSS `@keyframes` with `translate` and `rotate`: a row that rises into place, a field that shakes, a star that turns as it bursts. Translate and rotate add to `El::offset` and `El::rotate` and carry the subtree and hit testing. Scale and rotate pivot on `El::origin`. An `El::live_offset` or `El::live_rotation` keeps its channel, and beats leave it alone. While a sequence plays (a repeating one from its start until it's dropped), and while a one-shot waits out its `delay` holding its first beat, `El::offset` and `El::rotate` changes land at once. Once a one-shot has finished, or before a repeating one starts, they spring as usual (a finished one-shot's toward its last beat). Under Reduce Motion, a one-shot sequence lands at its last beat. If the element drops a sequence, one-shot or repeating, it springs back to its own offset and rotation. `Beat` gains public `translate` and `rotate` fields, so code that builds a `Beat` as a struct literal has to add them. This covers part of #567: beats still have no separate x and y scale.
