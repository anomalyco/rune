- **Wheel: a delta queued for the handler that has the gesture still reaches it when the next gesture scrolls first.** The wheel lock keeps who has each gesture's deltas: the arriving gesture's own owner sits beside the other one's while a delta of that gesture still waits, and an owner whose last queued delta has run gives its place up to the next gesture's. A delta queued outside the handler, when the gesture then ends and the next one begins or scrolls before the frame, reaches the handler that took the gesture instead of scrolling the viewport under the pointer.
