- **Wheel: a delta queued for the handler that has the gesture still reaches it when the next gesture scrolls first.** The wheel lock keeps who has each gesture's deltas: the arriving gesture's own owner sits beside the older gesture's while that one's queued deltas wait. A delta queued outside the handler, when the gesture then ends and the next one scrolls before the frame, reaches the handler that took the gesture instead of scrolling the viewport under the pointer.
