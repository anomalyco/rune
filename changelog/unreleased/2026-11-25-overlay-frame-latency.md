- Overlays: added `ConfiguredOverlay::frame_latency(n)`, which lowers how many frames an overlay's swap chain may hold that the compositor hasn't shown. It is clamped to between one and the platform default: two on macOS and Linux, one on Windows. On macOS, `frame_latency(1)` keeps two drawables instead of three, which saves one overlay-sized drawable: about 7 MB for a 488x873 pt panel at 2x. (#2247)
