- **`rune::machine` for explicit state machines, and timers on the handler's clock.** `Attempt` and `Seq` fence async answers and timers, and `Effects<E>` is the effect list a `step(&mut State, Event)` transition returns (two inline, more on the heap). `Tx::clock()` reads the window's logical clock (the one `poll` gets, virtual in headless runs) and `Tx::wake_at(due)` wakes `poll` at a deadline on it, so a timed state stores its deadline and fires it as an event. `AGENTS.md` and the README describe the pattern.
