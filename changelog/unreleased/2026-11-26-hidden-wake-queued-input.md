- Hidden, covered, minimized, suspended, and occluded wakes (`RuntimeCore::hidden_wake` / `covered_wake`, desktop hosts, overlays, and hidden browser tabs) now take a zero-dt frame without drawing when `poll` or a Playground step queues input (such as `Tx::send_key`, `Play::press`, or a paste), delivering handlers in that same wake with the poll's clock and draining chained input through `runtime::frame::drain::drain_turn` (bounded to `MAX_POLL_ROUNDS` = 4, logging a debug-only warning once and scheduling a continuation wake if capped) without double-polling.
