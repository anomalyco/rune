- Added `VirtualItems` and `El::virtual_items(Rc<dyn VirtualItems>)`, an app-windowed keyed container for 2D canvases and node graphs that remounts entering and leaving items without re-running `view`, preserves staying subtrees (`NodeId`, paint-keep epochs, `memo`, and `Live` / `LiveText` subscriptions), pins focused items until blur, and supports optional per-frame `mount_budget()` and nearest-first `mount_priority(key)` driven by an explicit fill state machine.
