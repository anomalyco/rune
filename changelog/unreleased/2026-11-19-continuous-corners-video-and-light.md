- **Video surfaces and `rune-light` surfaces, shadows, GI, and glass follow continuous corners.** Elements using continuous corners (negative corner radii) previously drew square corners on native video surfaces and in `rune-light`'s surface buffer, distance field, GI media, CPU shadow reference, and glass renderer instead of following the continuous-curvature curve. All of them now evaluate the same continuous corner shape and antialiasing as Rune's rounded boxes.
