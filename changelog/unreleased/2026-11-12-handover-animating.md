- **Windows: a window that animates while its GPU arrives hands over to it at once.** During the hand-over every CPU frame was drawn by the GPU too, and the window committed to the GPU only on a turn that found its latest frame finished. A spinner drawing every 40 ms kept replacing that frame, so OpenCode (Rune) handed over 1.6 s late, or never, in 4 of 6 launches. Now one frame is drawn by both, nothing more is drawn until the GPU has finished it, and the turn that finds it done commits and draws what was asked for meanwhile. A device lost while it waits leaves the window on the CPU.
