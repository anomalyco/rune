- `TabStrip`: a tab is selected as the mouse button goes down, in vertical strips too (`select_on_drag_start` now defaults to true in either orientation, as the web app's `onMouseDown` does), and a press on the selected tab draws nothing. A strip with neither `on_reorder` nor `tear_out` keeps its tabs still under a drag. An inactive tab squeezed to its icon has no close button, so a click on it selects it rather than closing it; a middle click still closes it.
