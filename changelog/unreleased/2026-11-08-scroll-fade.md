- **`El::scroll_fade` fades a scroll viewport's edges the ways OpenCode's lists do.** `ScrollFade::strength(width)` is `El::scroll_edge_fade`'s fade, full strength once 15 points are hidden (`.full_at(points)` sets another extent; a small one shows the whole fade once anything is hidden, as a toggled mask does); `ScrollFade::feather(max)` feathers each edge by as much as is hidden under it, up to `max`, from nothing at the edge, as CSS `linear-gradient(transparent, black min(16px, scrollTop))` does on Desktop's vertical tabs, so it appears and leaves without a jump; `.linear()` ramps alpha linearly, as a CSS gradient mask does, rather than on smoothstep; `.end_only()` leaves the start edge to the viewport's own `clip_fade`; and `.below_sticky()` starts the top fade under the `sticky_top` header stuck at the viewport's top, for its section's rows, at the strength of what that section hides under it, as Desktop's palettes do (a header being pushed away leaves its rows clear).
