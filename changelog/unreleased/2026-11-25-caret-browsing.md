- **`El::caret_browsing(bool)`: keyboard caret browsing in selectable text.** `El::caret_browsing(true)` gives `El::selectable` text a keyboard caret once a click (or `Tx::select_text`) places one, as a browser's caret browsing does: Left and Right by character (Option, or Ctrl elsewhere, by word, and collapsing a selection to its edge), Up and Down by line, Home and End to the line's edges, ⌘Home and ⌘End (Ctrl elsewhere) to the text's or `El::selection_group`'s ends, and Shift to extend the selection. At a text's edge the caret steps into the next or previous text of its selection group, and each step scrolls it into view, which mounts `virtual_rows` rows around it. Off by default. (#2104)
