- **`El::caret_browsing(bool)`**: opt-in keyboard caret browsing on mounted `El::selectable(true)` text and `El::selection_group(true)` containers (default `false`, leaving existing focus and Tab order unchanged). Focused caret-browsing elements draw a 1.5 pt caret (using `El::caret` or text foreground), share `rune-edit`'s grapheme/word/bidi/selection-collapse/platform-modifier rules across mounted sibling texts, support visual-line sticky `goal_x`, `Home`/`End`, `Ctrl`/`Cmd`+`Home`/`End`, `PageUp`/`PageDown`, `Shift`+selection, `Cmd/Ctrl+C`/`A`, scroll-to-caret, AccessKit `TextSelection` / `SetTextSelection`, and web accessibility mirror selection (demonstrated in `examples/kit-gallery` story `rune-text--caret-browsing`).
