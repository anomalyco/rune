- Clipboard Copy Image: added `Tx::copy_image(&Image)`, which copies an image an `ImageCache` decoded (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF and AVIF at their decoded size, an animation's first frame, SVG at its intrinsic size) as PNG, encoded on the clipboard worker rather than the event thread, with no second decode; an image still decoding, failed, or over `MAX_IMAGE_BYTES` leaves the clipboard unchanged. `ClipboardImage::from_rgba` is now public, and `Image::to_rgba` reads a decoded image back as straight-alpha sRGB RGBA8.
