- `rune-notify`: Linux now uses the XDG Notification portal (`org.freedesktop.portal.Notification`) inside Flatpak sandboxes (`FLATPAK_ID` or `/.flatpak-info`), gating v2-only keys such as `sound` on `version >= 2` and falling back to `org.freedesktop.Notifications` if the portal is unavailable; outside Flatpak it continues using `org.freedesktop.Notifications`. Added `Notification::sound(bool)` (default `false`, preserving silent notifications) to opt into the system notification sound across Linux (FDO `sound-name: "message-new-instant"` / portal v2 `sound: "default"`), Windows, and macOS.
