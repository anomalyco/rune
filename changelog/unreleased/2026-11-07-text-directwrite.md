- **Text: Chromium's glyphs on Windows.** `FontSmoothing::DirectWrite`, the new default on Windows, rasterises each glyph with DirectWrite as Chromium's Skia asks it to (the rendering mode the font's `gasp` table picks, its TrueType hints, grayscale antialiasing, the face at the run's variation coordinates), puts pens on Skia's quarter pixels, and weighs coverage with Skia's text gamma tables (sRGB, contrast 1) by the text's luminance. Inter and IBM Plex Mono now land within about 2% of Chrome's ink at 100% and 150% scale, light and dark, where hinted mono drew up to 19% heavier and body text up to 9% heavier before. macOS keeps `On`; Linux and the web keep `Off`.
