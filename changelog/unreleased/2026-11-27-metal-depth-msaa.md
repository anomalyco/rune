- Native Metal backend (`rune-gpu`): support depth/stencil attachments (including `Depth32Float`, `Depth24Plus`, `Depth16Unorm`, `Stencil8`, `Depth24PlusStencil8`, and `Depth32FloatStencil8`) and multisampled color/depth textures with `resolve_target` in render passes and pipelines, so compositors can draw depth-tested MSAA 3D scenes into `El::effect` slots without switching the app to `GpuBackend::Wgpu`.
