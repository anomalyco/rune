- Motion: `Transition::hold_layout()` now collapses an exiting in-flow child's main-axis slot analytically to the seam between its surviving siblings in non-wrapping flex rows and columns (excluding growth stacks and Reduce Motion), with zero per-frame layout passes and clipping of unclipped collapsing content and children. Existing `hold_layout()` callers (`rune-kit` `TabStrip` tabs and the keycap overlay) now collapse their slots on exit instead of fading over sliding neighbours; the redundant `hold_layout()` on `rune-knobs`'s absolute-positioned `knobs-panel` was removed.
