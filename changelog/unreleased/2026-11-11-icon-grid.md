- **A resting icon draws as Chromium paints an inline SVG: its box's origin rounded to the device grid and the drawing at its exact scale.** A monochrome icon at rest no longer takes the box a centring snap shrank by a pixel (which redrew it at another scale), nor a mask resampled into a fractional box: it rasterizes at its exact size on a canvas that size rounded up and lands 1:1 on the grid. At 125% on a 1.5x display a 1 pt stroke covers two pixels at 94%, as Chromium's does, where it was one whole pixel and two at 40%. Moving, turned and scaled icons keep the sub-pixel path.
