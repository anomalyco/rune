- `rune_kit::scroll_bar` puts a draggable scroll bar over a vertical scroll viewport's right edge (press the track to jump), shown only while the content overflows; `Menu`, `Select` and `PopupList` (so `Combobox`) use it. Page Up and Page Down move a menu's or listbox's highlight by the rows it shows, and `Combobox` takes Page Up, Page Down, and Home and End while its search is empty. `ScrollPos` has the viewport's size, and a rebuilt view's new `on_scroll_offset` handler is told where its viewport stands in the frame that draws it. A `live_rect` without a spring now lands in the frame that sets it instead of the next. `El::on_drag_any` is `on_drag` for a component with no state type.
