- **rune-kit**: `TabStrip::breaks` puts elements that are not tabs between a strip's tabs (`TabBreak::new(key, before, el, length)`): a group's header down a sidebar, a divider across a titlebar. A break has the strip's gap on each side, the tabs between two breaks reorder only among themselves, and a horizontal tab right after a break draws no separator. Strips with no breaks are unchanged.
