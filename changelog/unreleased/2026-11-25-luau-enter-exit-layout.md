- Luau UI (`rune-luau`): `enter` and `exit` props give a node its presence transition as parts separated by spaces, each with an optional amount (`"fade slide-up:24"`; `fade`, `scale`, `blur`, `slide-up`/`-down`/`-left`/`-right`), `spring:<secs>[:<bounce>]` times either side (`El::transition`, `El::transition_motion`), and `none` alone shows or removes the node at once (`Anim::INSTANT`); `layout = true` is `El::animate_layout`. Under reduced motion all three are dropped, except an `enter` or `exit` of `none`.
