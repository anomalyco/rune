- **macOS windows & overlays: added `App::frame_latency(frames)` and `WindowSpec::frame_latency(frames)` and restricted `frame_latency` to macOS.** `App::frame_latency`, `WindowSpec::frame_latency`, and `ConfiguredOverlay::frame_latency` clamp `frames` to `1..=2` (`1` keeps 2 `CAMetalLayer` drawables, `2` keeps 3, default `2`) and are ignored on Windows, Linux, and the web (fixing `ConfiguredOverlay::frame_latency` from #2251 altering Linux's swap-chain latency). On macOS windows, `frame_latency(1)` keeps `LaunchDrawables` steady at 2 drawables across creation, settle, and GPU surface reattach. (#2247)
