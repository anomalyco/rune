- **Effect slots carry their tag and a payload to the compositor.** `EffectSite::mark` is the slot's `El::effect` tag, so a hook no longer needs `Runtime::marks` (or `track_marks`), and `El::effect_data([f32; 4])` sets four floats the hook reads as `EffectSite::data`, bit for bit. Both ride in the slot's record (`border[0..2]` and `uv`), so a removed element keeps the payload it was last built with through its exit fade while its replacement enters beside it, with no bookkeeping in the app. `compose::Compositor` documents the pattern: draw through `Renderer::draw_with`, keep resources per mark, drop a mark's once a frame passes without it. The slot's rect and `scale` follow `El::scale`, `El::offset` and a transition's scale and offset; under `El::rotate` the slot stays upright inside a turned layer.
