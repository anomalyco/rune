- **`NativeSurface` shows D3D shared textures on Windows.** `NativeSurface::push_shared` takes a `native_surface::windows::SharedTexture`: a BGRA8 texture another process or API shares by name, with an optional shared fence and value. Rune reads it the first time it draws the frame, copying it into a texture of its own in one short submission on its D3D12 device, then signals the fence, so the producer knows when it may write the texture again. `native_surface::windows::adapter_luid()` names the adapter Rune made its window's device on, so a producer (such as Chromium with `--use-adapter-luid`) renders where Rune can open its textures.
