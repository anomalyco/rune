- **`El::hold_landing_width` holds a column at the width it lands at while a panel's spring moves its edge.** In an animated commit that changes its width, it and everything in it take their new layout in that frame, so text wraps once rather than on every frame of the travel, and the whole slides as one from where its first child that drew last frame was, on its spring, to its new place; its growth rows' new heights land with it. Window resizes, instant commits and Reduce Motion land as before. Motion checks take that frame's re-layout as designed, and `scripts/motion-audit` starts a path after a step the trace marks as designed and a move where it first shows, so a re-wrapped row or a control laid out in a panel that hasn't opened yet isn't a dog-leg or a reversal.
