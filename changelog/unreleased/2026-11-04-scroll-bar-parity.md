- **`rune_kit::ScrollBar` behaves like a macOS scroller.** Wrapping a viewport with `.scroll_bar(&k)` lays it out exactly as the bare viewport: the wrapper takes the viewport's overflow, so its content no longer grows ancestors that have no `min_h` (drop any `.h(0.0)` workaround). The thumb widens from 6 to 10 points while the pointer is over the bar or holds the thumb, and the pointer near the right edge brings up an overlay bar and keeps it up until it leaves, all through live values with no view run. The press strip is 14 points wide (was 10). New `El::on_pointer_move_any` and `El::on_hover_change_any` take a component's pointer moves and hover changes without the app's state.
