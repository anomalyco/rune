- **`rune_kit::ScrollBar` behaves like a macOS scroller.** Wrapping a viewport with `.scroll_bar(&k)` lays it out exactly as the bare viewport: the wrapper takes the viewport's overflow, so its content no longer grows ancestors that have no `min_h` (drop any `.h(0.0)` workaround). The thumb widens from 6 to 10 points while the pointer is over the bar or holds the thumb, and the pointer near the right edge brings up an overlay bar and keeps it up until it leaves, all through live values with no view run. The press strip is 14 points wide (was 10). Pressing the track pages toward the press (90% of the viewport), again every 60 ms after a 300 ms hold until the thumb reaches the pointer; under the "Click in the scroll bar to: Jump to the spot that's clicked" preference it jumps there instead, and Option-click does the other (new `rune::system_scroller_jumps` and headless `Runtime::set_system_scroller_jumps`). When the content grows or shrinks, the thumb glides to its new length rather than jumping (a hand scroll, an instant transaction or a resize still lands it at once). New `Tx::drag_repeat_after` and `Tx::drag_repeat` let any drag handler hear a held press again. New `El::on_pointer_move_any` and `El::on_hover_change_any` take a component's pointer moves and hover changes without the app's state.
