- **A window's draw backoff is one state, and a quiet timer frame is one.** `window::Backoff` holds when a window may draw again after a frame it couldn't get a drawable for, and how long each kind of miss waits before it tries again: an occluded surface's re-check and a hidden launcher's refresh both double, up to two seconds, from a frame that presents. A timer pacing motion no display link drives, and a surface being replaced after a failed attempt, take their wait from the same state, and one `step` keeps the doubling and the resets together instead of spreading them across the draw, the window events and the surface replacement. The flag saying only a due timer asked for the next frame now lives on `TimerFrame`, so a window event clears it in one place; frame pacing and when a window draws are unchanged.
