- **Tooltips can sit on any side of their element.** `El::help_side(Side)` places an `El::help` tooltip under, above, left or right of it, flipping to the opposite side when there's no room, as a web tooltip's `placement` does: one left or right of its element is centred down it, its second gap clear, and scales from the edge that faces it. `El::help_above` is `help_side(Side::Top)`, and Luau's `helpSide` takes `"left"` and `"right"` too.
