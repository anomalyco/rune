- Growth stacks: when a commit changes the gap above a row's contents and the row's height with it (a row that stops sitting close to the one above it), the contents now slide as the row's growth opens instead of on their own spring. In a list pinned to its end, where the row moves by what it grows, contents whose place doesn't change hold still on every frame, instead of sinking about half a point and coming back. A `Tx::launch` flight that has as good as landed (within a quarter of a device pixel of its place, held up only by its springs' settle tails) and that a commit would re-aim now lands and rides its row, instead of setting off on a new path that lags the scroll under it and sinks a few points.
