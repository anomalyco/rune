- **Text: subpixel modes, `FontSmoothing::WindfoilRgb` and Windows' `FontSmoothing::ClearType`.** WindfoilRgb is Windfoil's box coverage about each third of a pixel (FreeType's light LCD filter) from the outline store; ClearType is DirectWrite's `CLEARTYPE_3x1` with Skia's gamma per channel. Their masks are three texels a pixel, and the window draws them with a dual-source pipeline (the second output blends each channel by its own coverage), built the first frame subpixel text draws and only used on such frames; layers keep the plain pipeline, so text in them draws grayscale, as Chromium's does. Where the device has no dual-source blending they draw as `WindfoilChromium` and `DirectWrite`, and `FontSmoothing::support` says so. The default Windfoil shader is unchanged in cost: its subpixel paths compile out.
