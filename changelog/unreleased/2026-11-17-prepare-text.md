- **`Tx::prepare_text(view, size)` lays a view's text out ahead, into the window's layout cache, without mounting it.** The view is measured as the window would measure it at `size` in its next idle turn, with scratch nodes, layout tree and ids. Nothing draws, hits, animates or is announced, and no handler runs. A view shown later with the same texts at the same widths finds them laid out, so its first frame shapes and re-breaks little. At most 1 MiB of layouts are kept by the cache's own count. What no view shows goes as other stale layouts do, and all of it at the window's next rest. One request is held at a time, the latest, for the window whose state type the view reads.
