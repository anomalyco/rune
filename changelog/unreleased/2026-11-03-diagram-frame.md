- **Diagrams:** `Markdown::diagram_frame(f)` draws `f(card, frame)` in place of each diagram's card (`DiagramFrame`: its fence index, the lines it draws, whether the fence is still open), so an app can add its own controls or key it. `MarkdownStyle::diagram_min_scale(k)` (`DiagramStyle::min_scale`, 0.6 by default) sets how far a diagram wider than `diagram_max_width` shrinks; `0.0` always fits it. One still wider than its card at the floor now fades out at the card's sides instead of stopping at a hard edge.
