- **Text fields choose their spellcheck dictionaries on Windows.** `El::on_spellcheck_languages` adds a Spellcheck Languages submenu under a field menu's Spellcheck item, as Chromium's has off macOS: one checked item per dictionary Windows has, the last one kept, greyed out while spellcheck is off. `rune::spell` is public with `available_languages`, `languages`, `set_languages` and `language_name`; with no choice, fields check one dictionary per display language plus US English, and a word is misspelled only when every chosen dictionary says so. `PopupMenu::submenu_enabled` adds a submenu that can be greyed out.
