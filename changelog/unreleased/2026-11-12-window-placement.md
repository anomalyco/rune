- **Windows come back where they were: `WindowPlacement::restore` and `::display`, `App::full_screen`, and windows made on their own display.** `WindowPlacement` now carries the frame a maximized or minimized window restores to (on Windows the system's own record, `GetWindowPlacement`, converted from workspace to desktop pixels, which follows the window when Win+Shift+Arrow moves it to another display) and the display the window is on, so an app saves what Electron's `window-state` saves. `App::full_screen` / `WindowSpec::full_screen` open a window full screen, over its maximized frame when `maximized` is on too, as a window saved full screen reopens. On Windows a window given a position is made there (`vendor/winit.patch`), so it is born at that display's scale; made on the primary display and moved, it crossed to the other display's scale and was resized to keep its logical size.
