- **rune-fx and rune-light run on the native Metal backend.** It gains compute passes and timestamps at pass boundaries (`TIMESTAMP_QUERY`), as wgpu's Metal backend has them, and new textures read as zero as wgpu promises (a fill's backdrop read garbage before). Simulated fire matches wgpu's frame for frame. A frame of 16 rune-fx layers with 4 backdrop blurs commits 1 command buffer instead of wgpu's 65 and takes 55% fewer instructions to record and submit (1.65 million against 3.69); rune-light with glass commits 1 instead of 31 or 47, with 53–55% fewer. `Device::poll` on Metal no longer reports an empty queue while a completion callback is still waiting, which could stall video timing.
