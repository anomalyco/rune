- **`El::selection_style` recolours selected `El::selectable` text, as a browser's `::selection` does.** It took a text field's selection only; selectable text could set its highlight (`El::selection_color`) but its glyphs kept their own ink. Now the selected glyphs of either take the style's ink, with its inactive pair while the window has no focus, so an error card's red text turns `text-base` where it is selected, over `state-bg-danger`, as `tool-error-card.css` asks, and text outside a styled region can take Chromium's default white on blue (`SelectionStyle::chromium`).
