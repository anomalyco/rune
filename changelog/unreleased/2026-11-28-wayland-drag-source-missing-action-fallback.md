- **Wayland: fall back to the offered action when `wl_data_source.dnd_finished` arrives without `wl_data_source.action`.** On compositors such as Hyprland <= v0.57.2 that emit `dnd_finished` without sending `wl_data_source.action`, `El::drag_out` now reports `DragOutcome::Copied` when Copy (or Copy | Move) was offered and `DragOutcome::Moved` when only Move was offered, instead of reporting `DragOutcome::Cancelled` on every completed drop.
