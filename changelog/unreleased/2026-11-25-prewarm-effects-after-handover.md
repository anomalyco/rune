- Windows: a window that opens on the CPU renderer names its warmed effects (`App::prewarm_effects`, `WindowSpec::prewarm_effects`) when its hand-over to the GPU is committed, not when the GPU arrives. The effect-warm worker used to start in the prepare of the frame both renderers draw, a few milliseconds before the commit; nothing is prepared while the GPU finishes that frame, so a hand-over frame that took 300 ms or more to finish read to the worker as a window at rest and it would compile inside the hand-over. The worker now starts its rest at the commit. A window on a plain HWND swap chain (`WGPU_DX12_PRESENTATION_SYSTEM=hwnd`) has no hand-over frame and names its effects as before.
