- **Quitting is one signal.** `App::on_quit` (and `WindowSpec::on_quit`) runs once in every open window when the app starts to quit, before any window closes for it: `Tx::quit` or `AppHandle::quit` from any window, the last window closing, macOS Quit (⌘Q, the Dock, logout and shutdown, through the app delegate's `applicationShouldTerminate:`), and a Windows logoff or shutdown (`WM_QUERYENDSESSION`, then `WM_ENDSESSION`, heard by a hidden session window that runs the loop until the hooks ran, and closes the windows when the session ends). `rune::quitting()` holds from then on, so an `on_close` can tell the app ending from its window closing alone; a Windows shutdown another app refuses clears it.
