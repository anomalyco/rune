- **Text: runs that keep their own order in mixed-direction text.** `El::isolate(range, dir)` lays a run of a static text out as a UAX #9 isolate (LRI, RLI or FSI .. PDI), so `src/app.ts`, `--no-verify`, `.gitignore` or `#693` keeps the order it was typed in an Arabic or Hebrew sentence, and a paragraph's direction skips it. The marks draw nothing and copying leaves them out. `rune::text::ltr_units(text)` finds such runs in prose: paths, URLs, calls, flags, file names, references and signed numbers. A static text's pills are `Auto` isolates, as a field's are.
