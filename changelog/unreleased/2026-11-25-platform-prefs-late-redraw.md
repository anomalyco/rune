- Linux (`--no-default-features` / `dbus` disabled): `CachedReducedMotion` and `CachedDoubleClick` now notify open windows and overlays to redraw when a slow (> 100 ms) fallback read finishes with a non-default reduced-motion or double-click setting, instead of leaving idle windows on the default until an unrelated redraw or focus change; `platform::preferences::tests` wait on the cache's condvar instead of racing the 100 ms launch timeout.
