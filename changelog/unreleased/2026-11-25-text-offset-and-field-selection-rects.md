- Text geometry queries: added `Runtime::text_offset_at(key, x, y)` and `Tx::text_offset_at(key, x, y, f)` to hit-test a window point to a UTF-8 byte offset in any mounted, non-exiting, non-secure text or text field (accounting for scale, rotation, scrolling and single-line horizontal scroll), and `Cx::field_selection_rects(key)` to read a focused non-secure text field's selection line boxes in window points with layout-read (>0.5 pt) and `memo` tracking.
