- **Text: moving text rasterises at its still ancestor's scale.** Text under an element whose scale or presence is animating rasterised at 1× and drew magnified, so inside a zoomed window (an app's `El::scale` zoom) a fading dock, a row sliding in or a pressed button drew blurred and then sharpened when the motion ended. It now rasterises at the scale of its nearest still ancestor and draws at its own scale from there, crisp throughout and unchanged at rest.
