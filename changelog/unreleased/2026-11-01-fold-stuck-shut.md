- **A fold shut from its stuck header slides up under it.** Shutting an `El::fold_glide` row while `Tx::keep_on_screen` held its sticky header at the top of the viewport used to stream everything above the header past on the fold's spring, often hundreds of points a frame, and the row below could jump. Now the part above the header goes at once, out of sight, with the viewport's offset dropping by the same amount, and what still shows rides the fold's bottom edge up under the header, with the row below rising beside it. At the end of a timeline, the header then comes down on the same spring for whatever the end can't hold. Mid-timeline, the fold still glides shut under the header.
