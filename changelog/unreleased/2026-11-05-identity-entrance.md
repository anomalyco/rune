- **An entrance that changes nothing no longer animates.** A node mounting with an identity insertion (`Transition::identity()`, as `RollingText` glyphs use) ran a presence spring that moved nothing on screen, and inside a delayed parent (a chip waiting on `enter_delay`) motion audits flagged it as invisible work. It now lands present at once.
- **Motion audits don't call a scroll bar's thumb stalled when the wheel slows.** A wheel step or fling sets the scroll's speed, so a thumb that slows with a wheel coming to rest and picks up with a fling follows the hand; a stall at such an input is no longer reported.
