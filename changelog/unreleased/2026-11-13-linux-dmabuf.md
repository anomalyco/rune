- **Linux: `NativeSurface::push_dmabuf` shows frames another process renders into dmabufs.** A producer renders BGRA8 (`DRM_FORMAT_ARGB8888`) into linear single-plane dmabufs (`DRM_FORMAT_MOD_LINEAR`; other formats and modifiers are refused, as are rows or files too small for the frame) on the GPU `native_surface::linux::import_target` names. Rune imports each buffer once on its Vulkan device, copies a frame into its own texture the first time it draws it and draws that copy from then on, and calls the frame's `on_read` from a thread of its own once the GPU has finished the copy, without waiting for another frame, so the producer knows when it may write the buffer again; every failure reaches `on_read` as an error. Imported buffers are let go once their frames are gone, and at most 16 (512 MB) are kept. Vulkan devices on Linux now enable `VULKAN_EXTERNAL_MEMORY_DMA_BUF` when the driver has it.
