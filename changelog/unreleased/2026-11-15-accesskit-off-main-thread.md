- macOS: an accessibility query the process makes about itself no longer aborts it. AppKit asks a view for its accessibility children, focused element and hit test on a background thread when the process itself calls `AXUIElementCopyAttributeValue` for `AXFocusedApplication` or `AXUIElementCopyElementAtPosition`, and one of its windows is focused or under the point; AccessKit's adapter took its `RefCell` there, which panicked inside `extern "C"` and aborted the process while the main thread held the borrow, and raced otherwise. Rune's vendored accesskit_macos now leaves the adapter alone off the main thread and answers as the superclass does (`scripts/vendor-accesskit-macos.sh`).
