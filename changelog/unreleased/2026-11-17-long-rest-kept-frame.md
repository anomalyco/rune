- **A visible window that draws nothing for 60 s gives back its kept last frame without taxing the next input.** After `LONG_REST_AFTER` (60 s; the same single deadline `Rest::Unseen` holds for swap-chain depth when hidden, minimised or covered whole), `window::rest` drops `render::damaged`'s full-window `Retained` texture (~9.2 MB at 1920x1200) and runs one non-blocking `device.poll(PollType::Poll)` so wgpu frees it without waiting for a frame; the first frame back draws whole straight into the drawable with zero allocation or copy, and Windows' `post_frame_turn` re-creates the kept frame on the next idle turn with no input waiting.
