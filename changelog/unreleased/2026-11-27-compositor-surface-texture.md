- **Compositor access to `NativeSurface` GPU textures:** added `Frame::surface_texture(&mut self, surface: &NativeSurface)`, `Renderer::surface_texture`, `SurfaceRenderer::surface_texture`, `native_surface::SurfaceImporter`, and `native_surface::ImportedSurface` (and unhid `native_surface::Orient`), allowing custom `Compositor` pipelines to sample a `NativeSurface`'s current frame as `wgpu::Texture` plane(s) while reusing Rune's per-device importer and texture cache across macOS (`CVMetalTextureCache` on both `GpuBackend::Metal` and `GpuBackend::Wgpu`), Windows shared/decoded textures, Linux dmabufs, and `ByteFrame` uploads.
