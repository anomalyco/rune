- **`El::cutout_circles` punches multiple circular holes through one element and its subtree.** Accepts an iterator of `(center, inner, outer)` circles and appends them onto the element, intersecting their alpha masks through nested isolated layers as CSS `mask-image` layers with `mask-composite: intersect` do. `El::cutout_circle` keeps its existing replacement semantics and single-hole output (`examples/shapes`).
