- **`InlineBox`, `InlineAlign`, `text::measure_inline`, and `text::measure_inline_with`**: added in-flow atomic inline box layout and measurement primitives in `rune-text` (re-exported at `rune::text` and `rune` root), supporting CSS `vertical-align` modes (`Baseline` with optional custom `baseline`, `Middle`, `TextTop`, `TextBottom`) alongside `TextCenter`, atomic whole-box wrapping, RTL visual coordinates, and caret/selection pass-over.
