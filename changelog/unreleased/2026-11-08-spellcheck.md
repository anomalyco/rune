- **Text fields can mark misspelled words, and a field has its own menu.** `El::spellcheck` (`TextInput::spellcheck`, `TextArea::spellcheck`) checks a field's text with the platform's checker, Windows' spell checking API in the user's language or AppKit's `NSSpellChecker` (none on Linux or the web), and puts Chromium's red marker under each misspelled word the caret has left: a wavy line, or a row of dots on macOS. Right-clicking a field, or the Menu key or Shift+F10 in it, opens its menu as a browser's: on a misspelled word (which the press selects) its suggestions and Learn Spelling, then Cut, Copy, Paste and, off macOS, Select All, then a checked Spellcheck item when an ancestor has `El::on_spellcheck`, which hands the app the flipped setting. A field with its own `on_secondary_press` keeps showing that.
