- **macOS: dragging a window between 1× and Retina displays no longer zooms its contents to 2× (or 0.5×) or flashes at the wrong scale.** `raw-window-metal`'s KVO observer updates `contentsScale` and `bounds`/`position` on the `CAMetalLayer` sublayer without disabling implicit Core Animation actions, so macOS animated `contentsScale` over 0.25 s while Rune had already resized the drawable and pinned it top-left. `pin_contents_top_left` now disables implicit actions on the Metal layer through its `actions` dictionary (keeping `delegate` unset for occlusion walks), and `Moved`/`ScaleFactorChanged` sync `contentsScale` and draw synchronously inside the active Core Animation transaction so the new scale and drawable land together.
