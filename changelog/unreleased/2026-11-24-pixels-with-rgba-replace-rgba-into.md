- `Pixels` and `ImageCache`: added `Pixels::with_rgba` for zero-copy, lock-free readback of straight-alpha frames, `Pixels::replace` to return the displaced RGBA `Vec<u8>` for streaming buffer reuse (with `Pixels::set` delegating to it and zero idle spare buffer), and `ImageCache::rgba_into` to populate an existing `Pixels` handle with off-thread linear-light mipmaps using per-handle latest-wins ordering.
