- **`El::stuck_fade(lead, span)` fades a stuck header's children as the next sticky header rises to it, as Desktop's Home fades a stuck day header's title (`home/scroll.ts`).** On an `El::sticky_top` element, its children (not its fill) are whole while the next sticky-top sibling's top is `lead + span` points or more below its drawn top, gone once it's `lead` below, linearly between, worked out each frame from that frame's scroll so it never lags. A header with no sticky sibling after it stays whole.
