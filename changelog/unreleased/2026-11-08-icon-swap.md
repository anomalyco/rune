- **An app can swap in another icon set.** `rune::text::set_icon_swap` draws each icon its function gives another for as that one, in every window's next frame, cached layers included; an icon keeps its element's size. `rune_theme_opencode::new_icons::new_icon` is Desktop's New icons experiment: the new artwork for the 71 catalog icons that have it, generated from `@opencode/ui`'s `new-icons.ts`, with the chevrons and arrows turned for their directions.
