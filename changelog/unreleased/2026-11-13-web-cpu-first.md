- Web: a full-window page paints its first frames on the CPU (`render::cpu_boot`, into a 2D canvas under the WebGPU canvas, which stays see-through and takes the input), live from the first frame; it asks for WebGPU once quiet and hands over when the GPU's first frame is done (same frame on both, 4,430 of 1,024,000 px off by 1). Without WebGPU, or after a lost device, the page stays on the CPU. `globalThis.RUNE_CPU_FIRST` = `off` / `only` for checking either side alone; a page's `marks` are performance marks too.
