- **Text: Chromium's `text-wrap-style: pretty`, opt-in.** `rune::text::set_pretty_wrap(true)` breaks a wrapped paragraph that would end on a lone short word again over its last four lines, scored as Blink's `ScoreLineBreaker` scores them, so a page's lines and Rune's break at the same words; the line count, and so the height, stays the greedy one. Monospace, justified, `wrap_anywhere`, editor and line-clamped text stays greedy, as does text with `El::wrap_greedy`; rune-kit's `Markdown` puts it on the last block of a source still streaming in. Off by default, so no app's lines move until it asks.
