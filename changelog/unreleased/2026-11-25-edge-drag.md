- `El::on_edge_drag(SwipeEdge, width, f)`: a finger that lands within `width` points of a window edge and first moves away from it drives an ancestor's handler (Down, Move, Up) before anything under it: a child's `on_drag` hears a cancelled Up, and a scroll viewport doesn't pan. The innermost one on the finger's hit path wins, so a page's back swipe beats the root's drawer. A mouse never starts one.
