- **Windows: scrolling and motion keep every refresh while another app uses the GPU.** A visible window's swap chain has a third buffer, so the GPU starts the next frame without waiting for the compositor to give one back (a 240 Hz list scroll beside the OpenCode desktop: median 292 → 3 missed refreshes of about 717). The frame latency is unchanged. A window hidden, minimised or covered for 60 s goes back to two buffers.
