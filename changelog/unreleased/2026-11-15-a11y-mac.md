- `Cx::system_increased_contrast`: macOS's Increase Contrast (or a Windows contrast theme) as a flag a view can draw stronger edges for; re-read live on macOS when the display options change, as Reduce Motion now is too. `RUNE_INCREASE_CONTRAST=1` reports it on for captures.
- Words a screen reader should hear as it attaches (a request arriving as it starts) are no longer lost: the first whole tree an adapter starts from raises no events, so the announcer sends them in the update after it.
- A focusable group that takes its own keys and no press (a game's surface) is a group, not a button, even when it steers by drag.
