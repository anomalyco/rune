- **Windows: words and line breaks in Thai, Lao, Khmer, Burmese, Chinese and Japanese come from Windows' own ICU (`icu.dll`), and the exe no longer carries ICU4X's dictionaries (3,057,152 B of a minimal release exe).** `icu.dll` loads the first time such a run is laid out, never at launch. The new `segmenter-dictionaries` feature links the dictionaries instead, for Windows without `icu.dll` (Server Core, Windows 10 before 1903).
