- Overlays: `KeyPolicy::Exclusive` and `OverlayHandle::release_keys`. On Wayland layer-shell, `request_keys` on an `Exclusive` overlay sets exclusive keyboard interactivity, so it keeps the keys wherever the pointer goes (Hyprland's `follow_mouse` moves on-demand focus away), and works on every layer-shell version. `release_keys` sets interactivity back to none and blurs the overlay at once without destroying the layer; elsewhere it does nothing yet, and `Exclusive` acts as `OnDemand`. `KeyPolicy::takes_keys` says whether a policy takes keys. No safety net exists yet: if the event loop hangs while an `Exclusive` layer holds the keys, the desktop has no keyboard until the process exits or is killed. Adding the variant breaks exhaustive `match`es on `KeyPolicy`.
