- **Kit: `ToastStack` runs Sonner's stack, not only its registry.** The pointer over the stack expands it (each toast at its own height, 12 pt apart), leaving collapses it unless a press inside is held, and one toast left collapses it; Alt+T's `Hotkey` expands and Escape collapses. Toasts that close on their own pause while the stack is expanded or held and resume with what they had left (`ToastTimer`, `ToastEffect::Closed`, `ToastEffect::WakeAt`). `ToastStack::place` gives each toast's lift, scale, height and whether its contents show, collapsed 12 pt higher and 5% smaller per toast behind the front one, as tall as it, at most three showing; `toast_stack` holds the timings (280 ms moves, 160 ms fades, 400 ms content fades).
