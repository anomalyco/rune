- **`rune::text::warm_outlines(&[TextStyle])` builds the outlines of printable ASCII in those styles in the idle turns after a window's first frame.** It runs about a millisecond a turn, and on Windows none while input waits, after `on_ready`, the window hooks, the GPU's start and the accessibility adapter. Then it stops, and an idle window wakes for it no more. It builds only outlines (one record per face whatever the size, unless hints fit it to a ppem), not masks. The first frame that shows text in a new place then draws those glyphs instead of building their outlines: 0.3–1.2 ms of a new session's first frame on blade. Where glyphs aren't drawn from outlines, it does nothing.
