- **macOS: the Windfoil uber shader ships precompiled.** `uber_windfoil{,_native}.metallib` and `render/uber/windfoil_hash.rs` are built (scripts/precompile-metal-shaders.sh, Metal Toolchain 27B47), so choosing a Windfoil mode on a Mac no longer compiles its WGSL at run time or logs a missing or stale library; the native backend's builtin library and table take the outline fill pass. The default (Classic) libraries are unchanged.
