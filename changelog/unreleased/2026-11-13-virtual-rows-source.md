- **Virtual lists: `El::virtual_rows_source(id)` names the data a `virtual_rows` list shows, so new data fills on its own budget.** Apps opt in: give a list the id of what it shows (a folder, a query), and when that id changes while the old rows' fill was still waiting or had given up, the new rows start their own fill at once and the old wait's timer no longer wakes the host. They used to inherit the old fill's wait and its spent resumes, so they could stay blank until a scroll. Rows changing under the same id keep the fill's budget, and lists without a source fill exactly as before.
