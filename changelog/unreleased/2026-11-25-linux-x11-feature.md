- **Linux: an `x11` Cargo feature, on by default, so a Wayland-only app links no X11.** Off, winit's X11 backend, `x11rb` and arboard aren't built: no X11 windows, XWayland overlay fallback, X11 global shortcuts (`ShortcutError::Unsupported` outside the GlobalShortcuts portal), X11 window menu or X11 pointer and display queries, and the clipboard and primary selection go through Wayland's data-control protocol with wl-clipboard-rs (text, HTML beside its text, files and PNG images; a secret carries KDE's password-manager hint). That needs a compositor with wlr or ext data control: on one without it copy and paste do nothing, even within the app, with no XWayland fallback. An app built with `default-features = false` must add `x11` to keep X11. Default builds are unchanged.
