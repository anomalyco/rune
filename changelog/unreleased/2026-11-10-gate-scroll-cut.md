- **Motion gate: an input's scroll cut through a virtual list is no jump.** A row that moves in one frame on an input's frame but keeps its place in the content it scrolls in (`ElementInfo::scrolled`) moved with its scroll, as one riding its parent does; a virtual list's stack moves by less than its rows there when rows above unmount into its leading space, which reported the rows of a send that cuts a scrolled-up timeline to its end as jumps.
