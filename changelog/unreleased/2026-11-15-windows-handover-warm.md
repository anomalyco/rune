- **Windows: the CPU-to-GPU hand-over frame costs what a frame does, and every GPU frame resets no D3D12 command list on the thread that records it.** Before it reports ready, the GPU thread draws the window's latest CPU frame once offscreen. That makes the pipelines its layers' formats need, its layer targets, the staging space and the instance buffer. It then keeps 64 command lists armed, each with its own allocator (`dx12::Device::reserve_command_lists` in the vendored wgpu-hal). A thread, `wgpu-dx12-lists`, re-arms each list once the GPU is done with it; it waits on its channel between frames and ends with the device. On Intel's driver, resetting or making a list takes 0.5-1 ms, and wgpu takes two for each render pass. A GPU that's ready hands over at a CPU frame that moves nothing, or within 1 s. OpenCode Home on an Iris Xe, from GPU ready to the GPU's first frame: 26-60 ms before, 7-15 ms after. `CommandEncoder::finish`: 17-35 ms before, 4-9.5 ms after. With ten tabs: 6-10 ms after. Pixels drift by at most one level, as before.
