- **Windows: a window that changed stops presenting when its frames stop changing.** The second of presents every refresh after each change (`Steady`, for windowed G-SYNC, hex#689) is gone: on a fixed-rate display it changed nothing on screen and cost every frame's CPU and GPU, about 15% of a core while typing at 240 Hz, and queued each key's frame behind its own repeats (input to photon, focused window, typing: 32,958 µs p50 before, 12,318-13,836 after). On an NVIDIA display adapter, where G-SYNC can follow a window's presents, the swap chain composites premultiplied instead, which keeps G-SYNC off and costs no frames.
