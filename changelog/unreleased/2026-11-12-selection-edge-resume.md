- A selection drag held still past the end of a list goes on scrolling when rows arrive at that end: the layout that changes the list's extent looks at the held edge again, and the list scrolls on to its new end with the selection following. Before, only moving the pointer started it again. A list that shrinks while it waits ends the wait. While it waits it asks for no frames, and release, Escape, moving back inside or the window losing focus still end it.
