- macOS: `Cx::is_full_screen()` (and `Cx::titlebar_inset()` dropping to `0.0` on enter) now flips at the start of the native Space transition via AppKit's `NSWindowWillEnterFullScreenNotification` and `NSWindowWillExitFullScreenNotification` in an explicit transition state machine, reconciling on `DidEnter`/`DidExit` and failed transitions instead of waiting for `DidExit` (matching Chrome's full-screen state timing).
