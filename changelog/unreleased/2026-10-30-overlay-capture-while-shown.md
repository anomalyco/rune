- **Hidden prewarmed overlays no longer blank Mission Control.** On macOS, `OverlayOptions::exclude_from_capture` (and `MacWindowOptions::exclude_from_capture` on a nonactivating panel) now applies only while the overlay is shown. A prewarmed panel stays ordered in at alpha zero on every Space while hidden, and its `sharingType` none blanked every Space's thumbnail. The panel now turns `sharingType` none before a show's alpha starts rising and back to read-only once a hide's fade-out finishes; a show during that fade keeps it excluded. `OverlayHandle::set_exclude_from_capture` while hidden records the request for the next show, and `capture_exclusion()` still reports `Requested`. Apps that toggled exclusion around every show and hide can drop that. Windows already hides overlays with `SW_HIDE`, so nothing changes there.
