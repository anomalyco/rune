- **Windows video survives a lost GPU device.** When Rune replaces a lost D3D12 device (a removed device, a driver update, a TDR), a playing clip's decoder lets go and the clip opens again at its picture on the new device, as a parked one does; an open that fails as the device goes opens again on the next one. Before, the decoder kept textures and fences opened on the removed device, so the clip showed nothing, and its D3D11 copy could crash the app. Measured with a fault hook that removes the device (`ID3D12Device5::RemoveDevice`) from 2.5 to 8 s after it's made: the clip plays again every time.
- **Staging: a lost device's frame no longer panics.** A staging chunk made on a lost device can't map; the write is dropped (that frame draws nothing and the renderer is made again) instead of panicking with "a mapped staging chunk".
