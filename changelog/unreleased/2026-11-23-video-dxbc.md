- **Windows: the video pass (every native surface: a browser pane's pages, a video's frames) ships precompiled for DX12.** Each of its pipelines compiled `video.wgsl` with FXC: 270 ms of `D3DCompile` (i7-12800H) before a launch's first browser picture, again for the punch pipeline, and on the UI thread for another target format or the additive pipeline. `regen_dxbc` now writes `rune-media/shaders/dxbc/video.{vs,fs}.dxbc` and the WGSL's hash, and `video_dxbc_is_fresh` checks them.
