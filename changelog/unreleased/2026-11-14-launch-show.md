- **Windows: a launch that asks for no activation keeps the user's window in front.** When the process was started with `SW_SHOWNOACTIVATE`, `SW_SHOWNA` or a minimized show (`STARTUPINFO`), its first window doesn't take the foreground as its first frame comes up, as Windows itself treats a first `ShowWindow`. A script, a test or another app that starts the app in the background no longer moves the keyboard away from the user. Later windows activate as before.
