- **`ResizeEdge`, `El::window_resize(ResizeEdge)`, and `Tx::drag_resize_window(ResizeEdge)`: interactive 8-way resize for borderless windows.** `ResizeEdge` (`North`, `South`, `East`, `West`, `NorthEast`, `NorthWest`, `SouthEast`, `SouthWest`, mapping to `winit::window::ResizeDirection`) lets borderless windows start OS/compositor-driven edge and corner resizes (Win32 sizing borders, X11 `_NET_WM_MOVERESIZE`, Wayland `xdg_toplevel.resize`). `El::window_resize(edge)` emits `TitlebarAction::Resize(edge)` on a bare primary mouse or touch press, defaults its hover cursor to the matching resize `Cursor` unless `.cursor(..)` overrides it, and yields to interactive children, `.no_window_drag()`, inert subtrees, and active modals; `Tx::drag_resize_window(edge)` triggers the same resize from custom press or `on_drag` `Phase::Down` handlers via `FrameRequest::resize_drag`. Ignored on macOS, on the web, in overlays, and in headless runs (`examples/launcher` adds 8-way resize grips around its borderless launcher panel).
