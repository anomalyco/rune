- Windows video: a decoder that falls behind while its clip plays on the layer no longer plays its backlog fast. The layer presented every picture it was given, one per refresh, however late; it now skips a picture once the next one is due on the clock (decoded, but not copied or presented), as the window's own path does, and still shows one at least every 250 ms. A clip with more pictures a second than the display has refreshes no longer drifts behind its clock on the layer.
