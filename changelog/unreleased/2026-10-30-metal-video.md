- **Video plays on the native Metal backend.** `NativeSurface` frames from CoreVideo (AVPlayer, VideoToolbox, IOSurfaces) import without a copy through a `CVMetalTextureCache` made on the backend's own device, where they were inert before. Each plane's CoreVideo texture stays alive while any bind group or command buffer using it is, as on wgpu. A 1080p NV12 frame costs about 156 thousand instructions to import, draw and submit, against about 710 on wgpu, whose figure includes its timestamp readback.
