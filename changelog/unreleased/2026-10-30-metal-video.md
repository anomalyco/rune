- **Video plays on the native Metal backend.** `NativeSurface` frames from CoreVideo (AVPlayer, VideoToolbox, IOSurfaces) import without a copy through a `CVMetalTextureCache` made on the backend's own device, where they were inert before. Each plane's CoreVideo texture stays alive while any bind group or command buffer using it is, as on wgpu. A 1080p NV12 frame with GPU timing on both backends takes about 1.05 million instructions to import, draw and submit (2.15 million on wgpu), and 1.95 million through its timing readback (4.3 million on wgpu).
