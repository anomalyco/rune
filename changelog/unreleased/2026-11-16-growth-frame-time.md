- **Growth moves by each frame's own time.** Growth stacks (streaming rows, folds, rows that
  grow or shut), their content slides and a pinned list's closing slack used to step only in
  whole 1/240 s substeps and carry the rest to the next frame. At 144 or 165 Hz that gave
  frames of one substep or two, so a fold or a growing reply advanced unevenly, by up to a
  substep of travel, and the rows under it juddered with it. Each frame now takes its whole
  substeps and then the time left, so motion shows where it is at the frame's time. At 60,
  120 and 240 Hz the steps are what they were.
