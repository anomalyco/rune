- **`frame-profile` builds log a live window's frames by stage: `RUNE_FRAME_LOG=FILE` writes one JSON line per drawn frame.** Each line has, in ns: the input and wakes handled since the last frame, the drawable wait, the build (view, commit, layout, text, paint, apply), upload, encode, submit and present, and the thread's CPU; the node, glyph and instance counts; why it committed and why the window draws again (motion, a commit or relayout due, input, a live value, a timer, the steady run); where frames and timers were asked for (`Win::request_redraw` and `Tx::wake_after` callers); and the drawn nodes whose loop wakes the window. Ordinary builds compile none of it.
