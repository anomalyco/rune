- **`App::full_viewport` covers the browser viewport from a canvas the page passed in.** `App::canvas_id` still mounts inline at the parent's width, as before; adding `.full_viewport()` gives that canvas what Rune's own canvas gets: fixed to the visual viewport, body scrolling off, `Cx::safe_area` insets and `document.title` from the window title. For a single-page app's full-screen route. `fit_content_height` wins over it. The body lock now sets its three properties instead of replacing the body's inline style.
