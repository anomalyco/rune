- **Windows and Linux: a notched mouse wheel glides as Chromium's does.** Whole notches (`WheelEvent::notch`: a run of whole-line deltas) that no `on_wheel` handler takes now ease to where they send a viewport on Chromium's mouse-wheel curve (`cc/animation/scroll_offset_animation_curve.cc`): `cubic-bezier(0.42, 0, 0.58, 1)` over 200 ms for up to 120 device pixels, down to 100 ms at 480, stepped at every frame and drawn on whole device pixels. A notch that comes while it moves sends it on from where it is, keeping its speed, so stacked notches run as one motion and a notch back turns it round; a hand, a key, a fling or `Tx::scroll_to` take over at once, and an ease sent to a following list's end keeps to the end as the list grows. Vertical and horizontal viewports, the horizontal axis's motion now one `XMotion` (rest, momentum, glide, wheel) rather than a velocity and an optional glide. Reduced motion jumps, as does an app that calls `Runtime::set_wheel_glide(false)`; macOS, precise trackpads and free-spinning wheels move 1:1 as before.
