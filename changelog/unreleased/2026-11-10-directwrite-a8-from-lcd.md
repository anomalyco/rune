- **Windows: DirectWrite text has Chromium's smooth edges, without dots above and below round letters.** `FontSmoothing::DirectWrite` rasterises ClearType coverage and averages its three subpixels, as Skia does for Chromium's grayscale text with ClearType on (`kGenA8FromLCD_Flag`), instead of DirectWrite's grayscale, whose 17 levels Skia's gamma turned into isolated light pixels on the overshoots of S, s, o and e at 1x to 1.5x.
