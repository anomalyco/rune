- **OpenCode's theme parses `theme.json` once.** `rune_theme_opencode::kit` read it four times per appearance: the theme, an sRGB-free copy reparsed from a re-serialised document, and the kit's shadows. It now parses once for both appearances and the shadows, and reads authored colours with the new `Theme::authored`. `Theme::from_value` builds a theme from an already-parsed document. On blade, release: the dark theme and kit, cold, 5.5 → 1.4 ms; the light kit after it, 5.0 → 0.3 ms.
