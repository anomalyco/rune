- **`rune_kit::Spinner`'s dots are Desktop's pixels, and a filled `<rect rx>` in an icon covers what Chromium's GPU raster covers.** Each dot is now an icon over the whole box holding its one `<rect>`, as `spinner.tsx` draws it, instead of a rounded element. A filled round rect in an icon goes to the op Ganesh picks for it: its circle op only when the corners Skia reads back from `SkPath::addRRect`'s points come out equal and at least half a pixel round, and otherwise `FillRRectOp`, which squares corners under 0.75 px, rounds the rest at least 1.5 px and shades each arc by `fwidth` across the GPU's 2×2 quads (new `text/convex/rrect.rs`). Which quad a pixel falls in depends on whether the icon's origin is odd or even on the window's grid, or on its own layer's grid for an SVG whose opacity animates, so the icon cache keeps one drawing per parity. At 2x the middle dot's corner pixel went from 213 to 238 on white, as on Desktop. Against Desktop's own spinner at 1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.875, 2 and 3x, at 64 sub-pixel placements each, still and animated, no pixel differs by more than 1 level (at 2x, none differs). The 1-level differences sit on a rounding boundary (at 1x, coverage of exactly one half), where the GPU's own interpolation rounds the other way.
