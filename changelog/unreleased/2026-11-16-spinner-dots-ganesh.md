- **`rune_kit::Spinner`'s dots are Desktop's pixels, and a filled `<rect rx>` in an icon covers what Chromium's GPU raster covers.** Each dot is now an icon over the whole box holding its one `<rect>`, as `spinner.tsx` draws it, instead of a rounded element. A filled round rect in an icon goes to the op Ganesh picks for it, each ported as its own mesh with vertices snapped to 1/256 px as the GPU snaps them (`text/convex/rrect.rs`): `CircularRRectOp` only when the corners Skia reads back from `SkPath::addRRect`'s points come out equal and at least half a pixel round, else `FillRRectOp`, which squares corners under 0.75 px, rounds the rest at least 1.5 px and shades each arc by `fwidth` across the GPU's 2×2 quads. Which quad a pixel falls in depends on whether the icon's origin is odd or even on the window's grid, or on its own layer's grid for an SVG whose opacity animates, so the icon cache keeps one drawing per parity. At 2x the middle dot's corner pixel went from 213 to 238 on white, as on Desktop. Against Desktop's own spinner at 64 sub-pixel placements, still and animated, every pixel matches at 1.25, 1.5 and 2x; at 1, 1.875 and 3x, 0.01–2.6% of pixels differ by one level, where the GPU's arithmetic rounds an exact half (at 1x, a corner where two ramps meet) the other way.
