- **An `El::on_key_up` on any element hears releases while nothing is focused.** Only a handler on the root (or the open modal's root) fired, so one on a stage or other nested element never did. With no focus, the release is now offered to every element with a handler, deepest first and confined to the open modal, until one takes it; a focused element, and the element focused when the key went down, still hear it first.
