- **Text on Linux: grayscale outline modes (`FontSmoothing::WindfoilChromium`, `Slug`, `HintedGpu`, `Hinted`, `WindfoilRgb`) now take Linux Chromium's text gamma instead of Windows'.** Light text on a dark page on Linux (and in non-Windows, non-Apple browsers) now uses the `LINUX_GAMMA` curve (`1.02` at black to `0.83` at white) across all Chromium-weight outline modes and `Off`, matching Linux Chromium instead of drawing heavier with Windows' `0.45` white exponent. Windows, browsers on Windows, and Apple platforms are unchanged.
