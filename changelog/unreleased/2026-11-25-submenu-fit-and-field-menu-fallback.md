- **Cross-axis `El::anchored_fit` clamping for side-placed submenus and opt-in `App::on_field_menu` / `Tx::resolve_field_menu` fallback.** `fit_anchored` now clamps overflowing `El::anchored_fit` surfaces along both the main and cross axes (lowering definite `min_width`/`min_height` floors when they exceed the available room) and `rune_kit::popover::fit` applies `anchored_fit` on `Side::Left | Side::Right` as well as `Top | Bottom`, so overflowing `rune_kit::Menu` submenus stay inside small windows (such as 420×400) and scroll while surfaces that fit remain unchanged. Added `FieldMenuRequest`, `App::on_field_menu`, `WindowSpec::on_field_menu`, `ConfiguredOverlay::on_field_menu`, and `Tx::resolve_field_menu` so apps can render text-field context menus in-window (reading `PopupMenu::entries()`) on platforms where `popup_menu_supported()` is false, leaving macOS and Windows native menus and apps that do not opt in unchanged.
