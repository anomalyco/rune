- Truncated and line-clamped text (`El::truncate`, `El::truncate_middle`, `El::truncate_head`, `El::max_lines`) now clips glyphs and decorations to its content box when the box is narrower than the laid-out cut, and paints nothing at zero width. `El::truncate` keeps the first grapheme clipped rather than replacing it with an ellipsis when only a sliver of room remains, matching CSS Overflow 3 and Chromium.
