- macOS global shortcuts: when `RegisterEventHotKey` with `kEventHotKeyExclusive` fails with `eventHotKeyExistsErr` (`-9878`) on a chord that `CopySymbolicHotKeys` lists only as disabled (such as Spotlight's ⌘Space after turning it off in System Settings), Rune now retries once without the exclusive flag instead of failing with `ShortcutError::Conflict`. Enabled system shortcuts, other processes' exclusive holds, `shortcut_owner`, and successful exclusive registrations are unchanged.
