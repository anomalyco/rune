- **Native `Cx::touch_controls` and `Cx::hover_none` follow live touch vs. mouse/pen input and Windows tablet posture**, re-running views and memos that read them when the primary pointer switches (with `Runtime::touch_controls` and `Runtime::set_touch_controls` for headless tests and embedded hosts). On Windows, `WM_TOUCH` and `WM_POINTER*` (`PT_TOUCH` vs. `PT_PEN`), `GetMessageExtraInfo()` `MI_WP_SIGNATURE` bit 7, `GetSystemMetrics` (`SM_MAXIMUMTOUCHES`, `SM_DIGITIZER`, `SM_CONVERTIBLESLATEMODE`, `SM_MOUSEPRESENT`), and `WM_SETTINGCHANGE` (`ConvertibleSlateMode` / `UserInteractionMode`) keep finger touch distinct from mouse, touchpad, and pen input.
