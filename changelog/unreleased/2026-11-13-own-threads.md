- **Windows: raising D3D12's idle threads no longer walks every thread on the machine.** `rune-unidle` listed the system's threads with a Toolhelp snapshot, twice per launch (67-75 ms a walk on a busy machine, 150-195 ms of CPU); it now walks only this process's (`NtGetNextThread`). A fast exit also raises every one of the process's threads to normal priority before it ends itself, so an idle one can't keep it alive, and `rune::raise_threads` does the same for an app's own needs.
