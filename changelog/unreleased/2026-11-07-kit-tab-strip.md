- **`rune_kit::shell::TabStrip`: OpenCode's tab strip, across a titlebar or down a sidebar, and `WindowTitlebar`, the band it sits in.** Plain data and builders: `TabItem::new(id, title)` with an avatar or icon, a status mark (`TabItemStatus::Busy | Attention | Unread | Failed`), a subtitle, pinning, a rename field and a `decorate` hook; `TabOrientation::Horizontal | Vertical`; `TabOverflow::ShrinkThenScroll` (tabs share the strip from 224 points down to 28 by `TabOverflow::tab_width`, then it scrolls with 24 point edge fades) and `Scroll` (vertical, 16 point fades); and `TabDensity::Full | Compact | IconOnly` from each tab's width, which hides the title under the status slot, then shows the avatar alone with its dot on a cutout corner and the close button centred under the pointer. Tabs are 28 points with a 6 point radius, a 1.5 point separator between neighbours that aren't active or hovered, a pressed overlay on the active tab, an instant hover wash, and a 20 point slot whose status mark swaps for the close button under the pointer, with the title fading out 24 points before the end. A middle click closes a tab, and closing with the pointer keeps the other tabs' width (`TabDragState::width_lock`) until it leaves the strip. Gestures run on an explicit state machine the app keeps, `TabDragState` (`Idle → Pressed → Dragging(Reorder | TearOut) → Dropping`): a 4 point drag reorders inside the pinned or unpinned partition as the passed tabs step aside on `SNAP` (Escape puts it back), and with `tear_out` a drag across the strip lifts the tab out at 1.03 scale with a floating shadow, drawn over the window by `build_layered`, and its release emits `TabTearOut` with the drop point in window and desktop points. Tabs open and close with keyed scale-and-fade transitions, reflow on `REFLOW`, and can carry `El::matched` ids across layouts. In a `.window_drag()` titlebar the gaps between tabs and the space after the trailing action still move the window. `crates/rune-kit/examples/tab_strip.rs` shows both orientations over one list of sessions; `scripts/motion-audit` gains its `tab-strip` scenario.
