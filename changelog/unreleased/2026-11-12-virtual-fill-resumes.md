- **Virtual lists: a fill that runs past its frames resumes, instead of going blank.** A list whose row heights keep changing under a scroll now waits a few frames when it reaches its 12-frame bound, resumes on that wake, backs off each time, and gives up after three resumes without the unfilled range shrinking. A wake the fill no longer holds, or one that comes due after the list has filled or gone, draws nothing and starts nothing. Past the bound, dev kept the spent count and drew nothing until something else asked for a frame.
