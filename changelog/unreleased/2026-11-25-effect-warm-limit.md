- Renderer: the effect-warm worker (`App::prewarm_effects`, `WindowSpec::prewarm_effects`, `Renderer::set_prewarm_effects`) waits at most 1 s for the window to rest before it compiles, not 5 s. A window that draws without pause from its first frame (a reply that streams, a spinner, an animation that loops) never rests, so its named effects were compiled 5 s after the worker started, and an effect first drawn inside those 5 s was compiled by its own frame on the UI thread (81 to 106 ms for backdrop blur with a cold shader cache on an integrated Intel adapter, where a warmed first use takes 10 to 22 ms). The worker still compiles nothing in the second after it starts, and a window that rests is warmed 300 ms after its last frame, as before.
