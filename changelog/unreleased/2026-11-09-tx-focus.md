- **`Tx::focus(key, ring)` focuses an element from a handler**, as a web page's `element.focus()`: a menu's trigger as the menu closes, say, with its ring or without. `Tx::focus_visible()` says whether the keyboard was showing focus as the event arrived. A text field focused through its model in the same change wins. `El::autofocus` names the control a modal opens on, and `El::modal_tx` is `El::modal` whose dismissal gets the change's `Tx`.
