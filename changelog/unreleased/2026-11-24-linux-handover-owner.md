- Linux: a window that opened on the CPU goes to the GPU at one point, before its Vulkan surface is made, and from there its CPU frames can send the display nothing (`platform::cpu_frames::Retired`). On a Wayland compositor with explicit synchronization (`wp_linux_drm_syncobj_manager_v1`) a `wl_shm` commit beside a driver's swap chain is a protocol error; a window whose GPU surface then can't be made goes back to the CPU only on X11 and on compositors without it. A GPU that gave no device no longer touches the window's surface. New mark `gpu.window.switched`.
