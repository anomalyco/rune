- `DragItem::data(mime, bytes)` (`data_for`, `custom_data`, `drag_out::MAX_CUSTOM_DATA_ENTRIES` = 64, `drag_out::MAX_CUSTOM_DATA_BYTES` = 32 MiB) attaches ordered custom MIME payloads to outgoing drags, wired to UTI pasteboard items on macOS (`UTType` / `CoreServices` dynamic `dyn.*` fallback) and `RegisterClipboardFormatW` + `TYMED_HGLOBAL` on Windows (with a 256-format process-wide cache), and `DragImage::hotspot(x, y)` (`hotspot_point`) pins a drag image hotspot under the pointer.
