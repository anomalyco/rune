- **rune-kit `image_zoom`: `Preview`, a full-window image preview with a zoom bar, and `Stage`, a pane's image with preview controls**, as `@opencode/ui`'s `image-preview` and the file viewer's image stage. The preview zooms about the pointer with a pinch or Ctrl + wheel, pans with the wheel or a drag once zoomed, double-clicks to 100% and back, steps by √2 with its buttons and ⌘ + / ⌘ −, fits with ⌘ 0 or 0, and on Escape fits a zoomed image before it closes; steps glide on a 0.3 s spring the app drives from its poll (`ImageZoom::poll`). The stage zooms with a click, a pinch or its controls (1.25 steps) and pans once zoomed. Their gestures are explicit state machines (`image_zoom::gesture`, `StageGesture`).
