- **Delayed hover opens in a window that isn't key.** `on_hover_change` with `hover_delays` (tooltips, hover cards) no longer stays shut while the window is unfocused: blur still ends a shown hover and cancels a wait, and the pointer's next move over the window starts the wait again, as undelayed hover and hover styles already did. Clicks are unchanged; the first click on a background window still focuses it and acts. A window or overlay that ignores the mouse (`click_through`, `OverlayInput::None`) hears no hover; `OverlayInput::Regions` is the opt-in for an overlay that passes clicks through but still hovers (t1136).
