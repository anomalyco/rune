- **Monochrome icons draw SVG groups and transforms.** `transform` (`matrix`, `translate`, `scale`, `rotate`, `skewX`, `skewY`) on a group or an element now places what it draws, so a mirrored panel icon draws mirrored and an inset mark draws inset, without baking the transform into the paths. Elements inherit a group's `fill`, `stroke`, `stroke-width`, caps, joins, fill rule and fill and stroke opacity; a group's `opacity` fades what it holds as one; `polyline` and `polygon` draw; opacities written as percentages (`fill-opacity="16%"`) read as such instead of as 1; and what `defs`, `clipPath`, `mask` and the like hold no longer paints.
