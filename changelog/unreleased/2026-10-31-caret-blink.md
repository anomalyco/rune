- **The text caret blinks.** A focused field's caret shows for 0.5 s, then hides for 0.5 s, while its window is focused. Typing, moving the caret, changing the selection or focusing another field shows it again and restarts the cycle. The window wakes only when the caret turns on or off, two frames a second. The caret stays on, and wakes nothing, when the window isn't focused (or hasn't reported focus, as headless runtimes don't), under reduced motion, and after 60 s without a change. Headless goldens don't move. A window with a focused field no longer counts as resting while its caret blinks.
