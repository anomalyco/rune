- **Windows DXGI swap-chain wait slots reset across resizes, reblends, and surface replacements.** Resizing a window, switching sRGB blending, or failing during surface reattachment, alpha rebind, or CPU-to-GPU handover previously left the frame-latency `Slot` holding a stale wait on the replaced swap chain (stalling frames for up to 100 ms). `Slot` now resets before every reconfigure or drop and tracks the surface's configure generation and waitable handle so stale waits are always discarded.
