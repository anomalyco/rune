- **rune-kit: `ScrollBarStyle::device_pixels(scale)` sizes and places a thumb in whole device pixels, as Chromium does a native scroll bar's.** The length is rounded and the place truncated (a thumb less than a pixel from the start is one pixel in), so a look that copies `scrollbar.css` (`ScrollBarStyle::native`) lands on the browser's pixels after a scroll, not half a pixel past them. Without it, both stay unrounded as before.
