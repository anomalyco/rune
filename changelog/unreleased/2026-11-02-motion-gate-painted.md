- `rune::motion_gate` follows everything an element paints (colours in OKLab, corners, border width, blurs, shadow, effect payload, image, paint order) and the composited window, and finds `snap`, `flash` and `changes-twice` in each, naming the property that broke. `El::motion_intent` marks authored bounces, kicks, steps, cuts and pulses where they're written; `AllowRow` scopes an allow row to cells and caps it with `.max(n)`, and unused rows are listed. Headless `rune` records the gate with `RUNE_MOTION_GATE=<path>`.
