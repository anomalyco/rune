- `rune-kit::Collapsible`: `arrow_always()` keeps the disclosure arrow visible while closed, `arrow_end()` puts it at the end of the trigger row, `arrow_size(pt)` sets the icon size (default `14.0`, in a box of at least 24 points), and `trailing(El)` adds controls such as a reset button or a switch to the header row beside the trigger, so pressing them does not toggle the section; with trailing content the trigger sits at `.../header/trigger` instead of directly under the collapsible (#2429).
