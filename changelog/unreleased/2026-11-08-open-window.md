- **`open_window` opens another window while the app runs.** Called on the UI thread with a `WindowSpec`, it starts the window at the end of the turn with its own state, retained tree, focus and clocks, as a `run_windows` factory's windows start at launch, and returns its `WindowHandle`. The window keeps the app running, so the app ends with the last of them; a key already open is refused, and headless runs open nothing. `WindowSpec::inactive` (`App::open_inactive`) shows a window without taking focus from the one the user works in, for a window a tab is dragged into, and `WindowSpec::maximized` (`App::maximized`) opens one maximized. `WindowHandle::placement` reads where a window is (frame and content position, content size, scale, maximized, minimized and full screen) to reopen it there.
