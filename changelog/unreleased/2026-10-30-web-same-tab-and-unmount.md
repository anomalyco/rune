- **Web: `Tx::open_url_same_tab`, `App::same_tab_router` and unmount on `AppHandle::quit`.** `open_url_same_tab` replaces the page instead of opening a tab, taking page-relative links on web; a link on the page's origin goes to the app's router first (a single-page app pushes history and returns true), else `location.assign` loads it. Native opens it as `open_url` does. On web, `AppHandle::quit` now unmounts cleanly: the GPU device is destroyed, listeners come off, the body style, title and a passed-in canvas's style and attributes are restored, and an app started while another quits mounts once it has gone. `Runtime::take_url_requests` lists same-tab links with the rest.
