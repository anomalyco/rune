- **Native menus support nested submenus and closure actions.** Added `MenuItem::submenu(title, items)` for arbitrarily nested submenus (`NSMenuItem.setSubmenu:` on macOS, recursive lookup and disabled-ancestor checks in headless runs) and `MenuItem::with_action(title, closure)` for data-capturing `Fn(&mut S, &mut Tx) + Send + Sync + 'static` handlers while keeping `MenuItem::new`, `MenuItem: Clone`, and non-`Send` state unchanged.
