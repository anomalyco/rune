- Windows: a window that opens on the CPU hands over to the GPU sooner on a machine whose driver has not compiled Rune's pipelines yet (a first launch, or an exe at a new path). The atlas fill pass's pipeline is made on a thread of its own while the uber pipeline is made (`RUNE_GPU_PIPELINES=serial` makes them one after the other, as before), and the DX12 device's indirect command signatures, which Rune never draws with, are made at the first indirect draw instead of with the device. On Intel Iris Xe the hand-over comes about 290 ms earlier (670 ms to 400 ms after the process starts). Later launches are unchanged: Direct3D's shader cache (per user and per exe path, kept across updates) answers both.
