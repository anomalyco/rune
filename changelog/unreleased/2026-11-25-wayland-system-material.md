- Linux Wayland per-element system materials: `El::system_material` now drives shaped compositor behind-window blur regions (`ext_background_effect_v1` with `org_kde_kwin_blur_manager` fallback) on transparent Wayland GPU windows and overlays (`zwlr_layer_shell_v1` and toplevel fallbacks) by default when `WindowBackdrop::None` is active and the compositor supports blur (opt out with `RUNE_SYSTEM_MATERIAL=off`). Translucent fills tint the untinted compositor blur, unfilled material elements draw transparent instead of the opaque neutral fallback, outer-shadow interior holes are punched, opaque fills (`alpha >= 1.0`) remain unchanged without a blur region, elements below `0.10` opacity leave the region, and all four rounded corners plus ancestor clips are decomposed conservatively inward into surface-local logical `wl_region` spans (capped at 512 rects per surface) flushed right before present.
