- **A `Tx::prepare_text` pass is bounded in time as well as bytes.** It starts no new layout after 4 ms of one idle turn and lays out at most the request's share of 1 MiB; past either, a text it asks for is not shaped (a view that shows it later shapes it then). A pass its time cut short goes on in the next idle turn unless a newer request came. On a page of 400 paragraphs a pass took 110-170 ms of one turn and kept a tenth of what it shaped.
