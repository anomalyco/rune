- **A `Tx::prepare_text` request is the window's own and works in 1 ms slices, one per idle turn.** Its first turn measures the view and shapes for one slice; past that, a text node's full layout is queued (an empty layout of its style stands in for it in the scratch pass and isn't cached), while truncation cuts, runs, ink and measures from view code always shape for real, and a text laid out by paragraph is left out. Each later idle turn shapes queued texts for one slice, stopping between texts, up to 1 MiB, skipping a text that doesn't fit. A primary press or a key press in the window drops the request and what is left of it, and so do a new viewport or scale, new font families and the window's rest. Before, on a page of 400 paragraphs, one turn shaped for 156-204 ms and kept a tenth of it.
