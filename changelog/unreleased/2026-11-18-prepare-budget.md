- **A `Tx::prepare_text` request works in 1 ms slices, one per idle turn.** Its first turn measures the view and shapes for one slice; a text it asks for after that is queued (the empty layout of its style stands in for it in the scratch pass) and each later idle turn shapes queued texts for one slice, stopping between texts, up to 1 MiB in all. A press or a key drops the request and what is left of it; the frame it causes shapes what it needs. Before, on a page of 400 paragraphs, one turn shaped for 156-204 ms and kept a tenth of it.
