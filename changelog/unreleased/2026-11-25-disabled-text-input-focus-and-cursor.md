- Disabled text inputs (`El::disabled`, or inside a hard-disabled ancestor): text fields with `El::on_edit` no longer take or retain focus via pointer click, Tab/Shift+Tab, focus-group arrows, `Tx::focus`, modal autofocus/restore, `edit.focused`, or AccessKit `Focus`, and disabled / `El::aria_disabled` text fields show `Cursor::Default` (unless `.cursor(..)` overrides it) and reject edits, pastes, deletes, and submits while `El::aria_disabled` fields remain focusable and `El::read_only` fields remain focusable with a caret and text cursor.
