- Inline links with a real address (`Link::href(url)` or an `http:`/`https:`/`mailto:` `id`) now show a default **Open Link** / **Copy Link** context menu on right-click, touch long-press, Menu / Shift+F10, and AccessKit `ShowContextMenu` (before a separator and **Copy** on selectable text), without selecting a word on macOS right-click. Bare internal ids, `role("button")` chips, non-openable schemes, and redacted text never get it; `El::on_link_secondary` and element or ancestor `El::on_secondary_press` still win when set. Added `Link::href`, `El::link_menu(bool)` (default `true`), `Markdown::link_menu(bool)` (default `true`), and `Label::{OpenLink, CopyLink}`. Apps that want the previous Copy-only behaviour can opt out with `.link_menu(false)` on the `El` or `Markdown`.
