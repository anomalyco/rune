- **A window drawing native surfaces no longer panics on a resize or reconfigure.** On GPUs with timestamp queries (all Apple GPUs), each frame that drew a `NativeSurface` submitted a timestamp resolve from wgpu's completion callback, usually on the video completion thread. When that landed while the window's surface reconfigured (a resize, an outdated drawable, a frame latency change as the window rests), wgpu's `Surface::configure` found the queue busy after waiting for idle, and panicked with "Failed to wait for GPU to come idle before reconfiguring the Surface". Surfaces now configure under a lock that those submits also take: a resolve that comes due mid-configure is parked and submitted by the completion thread once the configure returns, so frame timing still arrives while the window is idle.
