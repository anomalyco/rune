- Input & rune-kit overlays: a secondary press outside an open menu modal now follows Google Chrome's OS split—closing the menu and reposting the press to what lies under it on Windows, and closing and consuming it on macOS, Linux and web (suppressing the browser's context menu on web). Dialogs (including `modal_trap` dialogs without `on_close`), `Select` listboxes and non-modal `scrim` overlays (`Popover`, `PopupList`) dismiss when allowed and consume the secondary press on every platform, preserving modal background inertness.
