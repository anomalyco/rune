- **`El::icon_one_color` draws a colour icon in one colour, as `@opencode/ui`'s FileIcon `mono` does.** A file-type badge or any icon that names its own colours draws as its silhouette in the element's colour, every part's coverage kept (an SVG mask of the icon filled with `currentColor`), so a file tab can show its badge in the tab's text colour until it's selected or hovered. Monochrome icons draw as before.
