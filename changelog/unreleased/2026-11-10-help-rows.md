- **`El::help_row`: tooltips with several rows, each with its own keycaps.** `.help("Save").help_keys(["Enter"]).help_row("Send").help_keys(["Ctrl", "Enter"])` lists two commands, as a web tooltip does: rows stack `HelpLook::row_gap` apart (Kit's look: 6 pt), each its words then its keycaps from the start edge. The element tree, assistive technology and macOS's own tooltips read the rows a line apart.
