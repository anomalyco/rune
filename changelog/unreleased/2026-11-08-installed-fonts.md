- **Text can be in any installed font, by family name.** A family the sans or mono slot names (`AppSpec::families`, `Tx::set_font_families`) or a `Family::Named` that the app didn't register loads from the system on first use, found through DirectWrite's system font collection on Windows, CoreText on macOS and fontconfig on Linux (opened at run time), in a lookup collection of its own, so only the families text asks for join the app's and its own still come first. `Family::Named` with a comma is a CSS family list, tried in order before the sans slot. A one-line `TextInput`'s value and placeholder stop at its padding, cut as an `input` cuts its text, rather than running on to the border.
