- **Wayland IME: keep `zwp_text_input_v3` alive across `wl_seat` capability changes (updates `vendor/winit.patch`; apps pinning `dev` must update their `[patch.crates-io]` `winit` rev) (#2746).** In vendored `winit`, `SeatHandler::remove_capability` destroyed the seat's `zwp_text_input_v3` proxy whenever any capability (such as a virtual touch device) was removed, leaving windows sending discarded `enable()` requests and breaking IME until restart. `zwp_text_input_v3` is now kept across capability changes and detached from windows and destroyed only in `SeatHandler::remove_seat`.
