- **`Tx::link_rect() -> Option<[f32; 4]>` exposes the activated link's first drawn line box inside `El::on_link` (and `Markdown::on_link_tx`).** Returns `[left, top, width, height]` in window points after scrolling, transforms, and single-line text-field horizontal scroll on pointer click (primary, middle, or touch tap), keyboard Enter/Space on a focused link, and assistive-technology activation, and `None` in every other handler or when the link was not drawn this frame (`examples/kit-gallery` adds `--scene rune-markdown--link-popover`).
