- **`El::selection_style` paints a text field's selection as a browser does.** A `SelectionStyle` gives the highlight and the ink of the selected glyphs, one pair while the window is active and another while it is not; `SelectionStyle::chromium()` is Chromium's default (#1967D2 and #C8C8C8 made see-through by `BlendWithWhite`, with white and #323232 text). The field repaints when the window gains or loses focus, and a range selection hides the caret, as a browser's field and a native one do.
