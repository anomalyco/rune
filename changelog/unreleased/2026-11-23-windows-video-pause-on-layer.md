- Windows video: pausing a clip that plays on its layer holds the picture the layer shows, and `Player::time` reports that picture's time, instead of the picture and time from when the layer took over. The decoder notes each picture it presents on the layer, a pause stops the clock and takes its place from that picture, and the surface gets that picture when the window draws the clip again. The same holds for a clip that ends on its layer (it shows its last picture) and for one the window takes back while it plays. If the held picture is no longer queued, the clip decodes it again, and a paused clip whose decoder was let go while its surface was behind opens again when drawn.
