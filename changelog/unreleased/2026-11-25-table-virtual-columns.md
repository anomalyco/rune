- **`rune-kit`: `Table::virtual_columns(bool)` and `Table::frozen_columns(usize)` for wide tables, plus vertical `scroll_xy` remounts and web-mirror ARIA grid coordinates.** Opt-in `Table::virtual_columns(true)` inside `scroll_wide(true)` windows non-frozen columns horizontally with fixed-width spacers and a hysteresis band (pinning resized, reordered, and focused or caret-browsing columns), while `Table::frozen_columns(count)` pins leading visible columns with `El::sticky_left` and opaque fills under the sticky header. Both builders default off (`false` and `0`) and, when enabled, emit ARIA `row_count`/`column_count`/`row_index`/`column_index` on AccessKit and the web DOM mirror (`aria-rowcount`/`aria-colcount`/`aria-rowindex`/`aria-colindex`). Vertical `El::virtual_rows` nested inside a wrapper under `El::scroll_xy` now takes `CommitKind::Remount` with zero `view` runs.
