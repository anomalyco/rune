- **A jump is judged on what shows.** The motion gate's `jump`, `offset-jump` and `jerk`, and `scripts/motion-audit/analyze.py`'s `jump`, `sibling-jump` and `step`, now need the element's visible part in the window (its rect cut by its clips) to move, or something it holds that shows to move with it, as a scroll's rows do when its content jumps. An edge that's off-screen on both frames can't jump. `analyze.py` also sizes a `step` by what shows. A fold shut from its stuck header drops its rows above the window in one frame and moves the scroll offset by as much, then glides in from where it showed: its content's top and height step, nothing that shows jumps, and it no longer reports `offset-jump` and `jerk` (`jump` and `step` in `analyze.py`). A row that shows and teleports inside a scrolled list still reports `jump`. The gate's `offset-jump` counts only the rows that move with the content, not every row that shows.
