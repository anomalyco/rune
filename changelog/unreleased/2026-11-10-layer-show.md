- **A group inside an `El::cache` subtree no longer vanishes for a frame when the cache stops fitting its budget.** The planner hid everything inside a cache it expected to composite from its texture, and kept it hidden when the cache then drew as a plain group instead. Internally, each planned layer now carries how it draws (hidden, covered by its cache, inline, rendered into a target, or composited from a kept cache) in place of a `needs_render` flag, zero opacity standing for "hidden", and one field that held edges while planning and a rectangle after.
