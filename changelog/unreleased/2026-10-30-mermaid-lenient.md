- **Mermaid diagrams draw what they can read instead of falling back to source.** A statement that doesn't parse is skipped and the rest draws; a shape opener without its closer (`B[/items split view]`) is a plain rectangle with the text as written; subgraphs, composite states, frames and class bodies left open close at the end; unknown `@{ shape: … }` names are rectangles. Only an unknown diagram type, a source where nothing reads, or one past the size bounds (400 items, nesting 8 deep, now also for sequence frames) still falls back. Also adds `classDiagram`, sequence `box` groups and `rect` bands, `@{ shape, label }` nodes, edges to subgraph ids, `linkStyle` and diagram titles (front-matter `title:` or a `title` line), on one shared layered renderer for flowchart, state and class diagrams (t1058).
