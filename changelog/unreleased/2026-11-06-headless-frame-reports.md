- **Headless runs report their frames to `App::on_frame_encoded`.** A single-window `--snap`, `--video`, `--script` or `--tree` run now calls the hook for every frame on its virtual clock, as `Drawn`, with `update_ms`, `encode_ms`, wall-clock `started` and `refresh_hz` at its `--fps`, so an app's own frame meter measures headless runs too. Apps that assumed the hook only fires for a real window see it called there now.
