- **Tooltips (`El::help`) run on one explicit state machine, fade in and out, and keep through a click.** The tooltip is idle, waiting out its delay, open, warm (within the skip delay of one closing, so the next opens at once) or suppressed, stepped once a frame with what the pointer is over. `HelpLook::fade(enter, exit, scale)` fades and scales a tooltip in (ease-out) and out (ease-in) about the edge facing its element, the closing one fading out under the next; reduced motion never fades. A press on the open tooltip's element keeps it, as a web tooltip's trigger does; a pop-up menu, modal or popover over the element hides it until the pointer leaves the element. `rune_kit::TooltipStyle::help_look` fades as `@opencode/ui`'s tooltip does: 120 ms in and 80 ms out from 0.96.
