- **Apps can build their own popovers from the kit's pieces.** New `rune_kit::popover` has `anchored` (mount a surface next to a keyed trigger, flipping and fitting to the window), `raised` (the popover surface style), `fit_scroll` (a long list that scrolls inside a fitted surface) and `anchor_rect` (the pure geometry: where a surface of a given size goes beside a trigger rect in a window, flipped and clamped). `rune::anchor_rect` is the same geometry for an `El::anchored` side and align. Apps no longer need local copies of the kit's private helpers. (t886)
