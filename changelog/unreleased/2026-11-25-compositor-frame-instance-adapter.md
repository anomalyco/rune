- **Compositor shared-GPU handles and callback restoration:** added `Frame::instance() -> Option<&wgpu::Instance>`, `Frame::adapter() -> Option<&wgpu::Adapter>`, and `Frame::restore_device_callbacks()` on `rune::compose::Frame` so custom compositors can initialize external renderers (such as Bevy) on Rune's active GPU once per `DeviceKey` and hand device-lost and bounded uncaptured-error ownership back to Rune across Windows, Linux, macOS (wgpu and native Metal), iOS embed, and web (WebGPU and WebGL2), without changing `Frame`'s fields.
