- **Text: a cell of a shaped run (El::advance_in, which RollingText and every odometer draw with) rasterizes at its text's raster factor, as a paragraph does.** Under a zoom or a scaled group it drew its glyphs at the unscaled size and magnified the masks, so at an app zoom of 110% the digits of a count looked soft and blocky beside the words around them. The frame such a cell lands on also places its masks, and every text's landing frame is followed by one that draws them, so nothing rests drawn from its landing outlines. RUNE_RASTER_CHECK=1 (debug builds) paints each frame that comes to rest a second time as a fresh text would and names any glyph that differs or magnifies its mask.
