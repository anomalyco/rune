- Web: text in every script. A run no loaded font covers (Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi, Thai, emoji) is shaped, measured and drawn by the browser: every family stack ends in a face of last resort, "Rune Platform" (a font with no outlines that covers every character), whose runs Parley gives to the page (`parley_engine::platform`). The page measures each unit a line can't break inside on an offscreen 2D canvas, gives each grapheme its advance from measuring prefixes, and draws it grapheme by grapheme, or whole where letters join; the images are atlas masks or colour images like any glyph's. Caret, selection and wrapping follow the browser's own: a Thai paragraph breaks where Chrome's `<p lang="th">` does at 160, 280 and 420 px. No download, no permission.
- Web: no ICU4X dictionaries in the module (Parley without `complex-scripts` on `wasm32`): the words of Thai, Lao, Khmer, Burmese, Chinese and Japanese runs, and the SA scripts' line breaks, come from the browser's `Intl.Segmenter` (`parley_engine::dictionary`, one segmenter per language), and a double-click's words too. `globalThis.__runeTextProbe` gives a harness `__runeLines(text, size, weight, width)`.
