- **`rune-theme-opencode` ships Desktop's provider marks.** `provider_icons::named("anthropic")` returns the `@opencode/ui` mark for 21 providers (OpenAI, Google, xAI, Mistral, GitHub Copilot and others) as a tintable `Icon`, and `FALLBACK` is Desktop's stand-in for any other provider. They are generated by `themes/opencode/tools/provider-icons.py` from the opencode checkout; a mark that needs a transform or a non-square viewBox is skipped. Nothing else changes.
