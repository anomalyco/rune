- **A view can highlight matching words in every text it holds.** `El::highlight_texts(f)` calls `f` with what each `text` and `rich` element in the subtree shows (inline links and styled runs included) and adds the `TextHighlight`s it returns, so a settings search can mark its matches on whatever page it opens without wrapping each builder. Highlights only paint: they never reshape text or move line breaks. Text fields, secure and live texts are skipped, as are `memo` subtrees and rows `virtual_rows` builds later (call it inside those builders too); ranges that are empty, past the text or off a char boundary are dropped.
