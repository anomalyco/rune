- **The CPU renderer draws layers turned by a multiple of 90° (`El::rotate(FRAC_PI_2)` and the like, such as a vertical chart axis label):** `Scene::needs_gpu` no longer answers `NeedsGpu::TurnedLayer` for them, so a frame with one draws on `CpuPainter` (CPU-first startup on Windows, Linux and the web, and `CpuFirst::Only`) instead of keeping the previous frame, which on a fresh web page under `globalThis.RUNE_CPU_FIRST = "only"` left the page blank. The layer's contents paint upright into a buffer of their own, including parts outside the window that the turn brings into it, and the layer's end composites that buffer turned, sampled bilinearly as the GPU's turned-layer composite samples its texture, by the layer's opacity, clip and hole. Any other angle, and turned layers that are cached or plus-lighter, still answer `NeedsGpu::TurnedLayer`. Not compared against a GPU frame (no GPU on the test host). Refs #1233.
