- **Scroll bars follow the system "Show scroll bars" preference, and any scroll viewport can opt in with one call.** `rune::ScrollBars` (`WhenScrolling`, `Always`, `Automatic`), `Cx::scroll_bars` and `Cx::overlay_scroll_bars` read macOS `AppleShowScrollBars` and `NSScroller.preferredScrollerStyle` and update live on `NSPreferredScrollerStyleDidChangeNotification` across windows, overlays and `memo` scopes, defaulting to overlay-when-scrolling elsewhere and headless (`--scroll-bars when-scrolling|always|automatic` overrides it) and staying overlay on iOS. `rune_kit::ScrollBar` (`viewport.scroll_bar(&k)` or `k.scroll_bar(viewport)`) wraps any vertical scroll viewport while keeping its outer sizing, key and `on_scroll_offset` handler: `Always` shows whenever content overflows, `WhenScrolling` pulses on scroll and fades out after a short hold via `Live::pulse` and `Binding::rest`, and scrolling and thumb dragging run through live values with zero view commits per frame.
