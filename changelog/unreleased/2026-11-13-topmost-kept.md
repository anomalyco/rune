- **Windows: a window launched in the background keeps another app's Always On Top.** A Rune window asked for the foreground as its first frame came on screen, even when Windows would refuse (a launch from a script or a background process); the refused request held the window under the foreground window from then on, so PowerToys' Always On Top and other tools' `HWND_TOPMOST` succeeded and changed nothing. It now asks only when this process may take the foreground, and otherwise stays where a bare Win32 window does.
