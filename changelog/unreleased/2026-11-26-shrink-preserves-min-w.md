- Layout (`El::shrink`, `El::set_shrink`, and single-line truncation builders `El::truncate`, `El::set_truncate`, `El::truncate_middle`, `El::truncate_head`, `El::truncate_fade`, `El::truncate_fade_width`): elements with an explicit `.min_w(w)` that previously relied on `.shrink()` or single-line truncation silently erasing that floor back to `0` now stop shrinking at `w` regardless of builder order (call `.min_w(0.0)` after to override an earlier explicit minimum down to zero); `flex-shrink: 1` is still set unconditionally, automatic `min-width` still lowers to `0`, and `min-height` stays untouched.
