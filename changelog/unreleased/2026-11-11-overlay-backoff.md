- **An overlay's draw backoff is one state, the window's.** The overlay's `retry`, `occluded_wait` and the places that reset them are now `window::Backoff`, stepped by the same events a window's is: a miss, a present, a due timer, a frame drawn and a reveal. A covered overlay's re-checks still start at 250 ms and double to two seconds, a timer pacing motion with no display link still waits a frame, and when an overlay draws is unchanged.
