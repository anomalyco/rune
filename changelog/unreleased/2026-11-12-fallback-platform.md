- **Text: macOS and Linux find fallback fonts as Windows does, through the platform's own fallback, with no folder walk and no spawned process.** A character the app's fonts lack goes to CoreText's fallback on Apple platforms (`CTFontCreateForStringWithLanguage`, with the family's other faces from its descriptors) and to fontconfig's match for its script and language on Linux, in process; before, the first non-Latin text walked up to 4,096 font folders (`/System/Library/AssetsV2` included) and, on Linux, ran `fc-list` and `fc-match`. Every script now finds its font (Ethiopic, Khmer, Tibetan, Sinhala and the rest, not only the five listed), named families (`Family::Named`, the symbol and mono families) are found by name, Linux's `monospace` is fontconfig's own, and on macOS Chinese takes Hiragino Sans GB or Heiti TC where CoreText names PingFang, whose `hvgl` outlines can't be drawn.
