- **A small RGBA image that's ready at once wakes the app again, as a decoded one does.** Since #929, `ImageCache::rgba` of 64x64 pixels or fewer finished on the calling thread and called no waker, so an image requested outside a view's build was never drawn until something else caused a frame: a hidden overlay's icon set from its state, and OpenCode's project tiles, which its icon worker makes with `rgba` and which relied on the cache's wake. It now wakes once after the inline finish (after the cache's lock is released); a view that asked for it still draws it in the same frame.
