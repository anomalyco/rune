- Overlays on a Wayland compositor without layer-shell (GNOME) that can't use XWayland (no XWayland, `KeyPolicy::OnDemand`, or pointer input without `OverlayOptions::xwayland_input`) now show as an undecorated, transparent xdg toplevel, destroyed on hide, instead of going `Unavailable`. `OverlayCapabilities` gains `placement`, `always_on_top` and `click_through`: `Supported` on every other backend, `Unsupported` on the toplevel fallback (`click_through` only under `OverlayInput::Regions`), so apps can adapt. Code that builds `OverlayCapabilities` as a literal needs the new fields. (#20)
