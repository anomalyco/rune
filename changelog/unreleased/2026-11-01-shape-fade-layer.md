- **A fading canvas no longer darkens where its strokes meet.** A childless element whose `Shape` draws more than one piece (two or more strokes or fills, or a polyline of two or more segments) used to fade each piece on its own, so where two overlapped, at a crossing or a polyline's joint, the overlap blended twice and showed darker mid-fade (alpha 191 instead of 128 at 50%). While its opacity is between 0 and 1 it now paints through a layer that takes the opacity once. At rest, faded out, or with a single piece, it paints straight to the window as before, with the same pixels.
