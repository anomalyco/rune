- **Linux: the primary selection, as Chromium keeps it.** Text a pointer selects (a drag, a double or triple click, Shift+click), in selectable text or a text field, goes on the primary selection (X11 `PRIMARY`, Wayland's through data control) when the button comes up; a middle click in a text field puts the caret where it landed and pastes the primary selection there. A secure field's text never goes on it; Cmd/Ctrl+C and Cmd/Ctrl+V never see it. Its own clipboard worker. Written and checked for `x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`, not run: no Linux machine.
