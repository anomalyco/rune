- **On Windows, bold text in a system face with no bold draws in synthetic bold, as Chromium's does.** Text asking for weight 600 or more of a face under 600 whose family has no bolder face and no `wght` axis (SimSun, MS Gothic) drew at the face's own weight. Chromium's Skia fills and strokes such a glyph's outline at once (`Weight() >= BoldThreshold() && !typeface->isBold()`), the stroke `size × 1/24` wide at 9 px down to `size × 1/32` at 36 px, through its text gamma, and Blink widens each glyph's advance by a pixel; Rune now does the same (`text::synthetic_bold`), Windfoil dilating such a glyph's outline by half the stroke more.
