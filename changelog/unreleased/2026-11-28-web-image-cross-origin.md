- **Web: `El::web_image` and `El::web_image_fit` display cross-origin raster images without CORS or pixel access.** On web, visible `web_image` elements synchronize per-frame with a display-only `[data-rune-web-images]` DOM `<img>` layer (never setting `crossOrigin` or reading pixels), supporting border insets, rounded corners, `SurfaceFit`, `El::surface_anchor`, `El::surface_unscaled`, `El::image_filter(ImageFilter::Nearest)`, opacity, rotation, nested scroll/rounded clips, `App::web_document`, and `--html` static export. Native and headless runs keep layout identical and inspect draws through `Runtime::web_images`.
