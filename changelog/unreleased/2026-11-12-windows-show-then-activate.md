- **Windows: a window shows without activating and takes the focus once its first frame is up.** Activating a window starts its thread's text services (TSF), about 7,500 µs on the UI thread, and the first show did it before the frame; the window now shows cloaked without activating and activates (`SetForegroundWindow`) as its cloak lifts, so the frame comes first. From the native window to the GPU's first step: 23,488 µs before, 11,501 µs after (p50, 15 alternating runs each). A window hidden before its frame uncloaks without taking the focus.
