- **`App::gpu_backend(GpuBackend::Metal)` turns on the native Metal backend from code.** An app launched from Finder can't set `RUNE_GPU_BACKEND`, so the choice now lives on the app, with Rune's `metal-backend` feature. `RUNE_GPU_BACKEND=wgpu` or `=metal` still overrides it without a rebuild. It applies process-wide from the call on, and a `prewarm_gpu` already running on the other backend starts over on the chosen one, so calling `prewarm_gpu` first in `main` keeps working. Without the feature it says so once and draws with wgpu; off macOS it does nothing.
