- **CPU renderer:** a frame that changes nothing costs `CpuPainter` one comparison of the instance list (no binning, no tile hashing, empty `Painted::rects`), and one that only recolours (a spinner, a caret, a hover) repaints just its instances' tiles without binning again. Half floats in layers round as the window's GPU vendor stores them (`Scene::half_rounding`, `HalfRounding::of_vendor`). `CpuPainter::window_shows` lets a first frame leave tiles of the window's own background colour unwritten (`fill_absent` writes them later). Big frames hand threads a page-wide run of tiles at a time, so a fresh buffer's page faults don't queue; backdrop blurs run on the painter's threads.
