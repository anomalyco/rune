- Linux: when the XDG desktop portal answers `org.freedesktop.appearance` `color-scheme` with `0` (no preference, as GNOME's Default style and light-mode shells report) or an unknown value, `Cx::system_dark()` now resolves to `false` (light) instead of falling back to dark, matching Chromium, Firefox and libadwaita; unreachable portals and missing session buses still keep the dark fallback.
