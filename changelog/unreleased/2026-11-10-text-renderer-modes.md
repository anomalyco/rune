- **Text: every TEXT-LAB technique is a `FontSmoothing` mode, for comparing renderers in an app.** `WindfoilChromium` (Windfoil's box coverage of the design outline, no dilation, Skia's text gamma), `Slug` (Lengyel's horizontal and vertical ray winding, averaged, in the same outline shader), `HintedGpu` (skrifa's light autohint, then Windfoil) and `Hinted` (the light-autohinted outline on whole-pixel pens: what Chromium draws on Linux) join `Windfoil`, `DirectWrite`, `On` and `Off`; all draw text at rest and in motion from the same outline store. `FontSmoothing::ALL` lists them and `FontSmoothing::support` says whether this machine draws one as named (DirectWrite is Windows only; the outline modes need the renderer's Windfoil shader, and Slug can't fall back to the CPU's fill). Defaults are unchanged.
