- **A growth row's motion, glide stop and content lift are one state each.** `GrowTrack` keeps its kind as `Motion` (the capped spring, a whole glide, or a fold with its own spring, feather and window limit), its glide stop as `Stage` (`Free` or `StopAt`), and its lifted contents as `Lift` (`None`, riding the bottom edge, or easing), instead of flags and a `(a, b)` pair whose `-1.0` slope meant "riding". Every frame draws exactly as before. `whole` is no longer a public field (ask a row for its kind through the runtime).
