- An app's instant scroll (`Tx::scroll_to_instant`, `Tx::scroll_to_y` and the like) holds hover under a still pointer for a fixed two frames at 60 Hz (33 ms) rather than two frames of whatever the display runs at, so a 30 Hz display no longer holds it twice as long as a 120 Hz one. The frame a jump lands in still holds, and hover resolves once at the pointer when the hold is over.
