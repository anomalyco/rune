- `ConfiguredOverlay::fit_content_height` sizes an overlay's height to its content at its width, as `App::fit_content_height` does for a window. Each show measures what changed while the overlay was hidden, without committing or settling, and takes that height before drawing, so a candidate list or HUD whose height follows its text never shows at a stale size first. While shown, a change of height resizes the overlay on the next event-loop turn. `OverlaySpec::size` and `OverlayHandle::resize` set only such an overlay's width, and `Tx::resize_window` stays ignored. Give the root no fixed or `fill` height. (#2307)
