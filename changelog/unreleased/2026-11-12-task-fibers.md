- **Web: background work waits on the network without holding the page.** `rune::task::start_runners` (from a `#[wasm_bindgen(jspi)]` export) runs task work on four JS Promise Integration fibers; `rune::task::wait` suspends one on a promise (a `fetch`, a timer) while frames, input and the other fibers go on, and `rune::task::sleep` sleeps a thread natively and a fiber on the web. `Latest` and `rune_luau::spawn_blocking` hand their work to `rune::task` on the web, so their requests wait there too. Without runners nothing changes.
