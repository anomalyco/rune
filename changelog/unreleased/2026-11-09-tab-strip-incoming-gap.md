- **A tab strip opens a gap for a tab dragged in from another strip.** `TabDragState::set_incoming(Some(point))` (another window's tab is over this strip) puts an empty slot the tab's length where it would land, from the tabs' own lengths and the strip's scroll, which the tabs after it make room for on the reorder spring; the tabs already share the strip with it, so it lands as is. `TabDragState::incoming_slot` says where it lands, and `set_incoming(None)` closes the gap.
