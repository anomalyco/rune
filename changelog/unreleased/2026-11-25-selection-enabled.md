- Added `El::selection_enabled(bool)`, a subtree switch for selectable text in the spirit of SwiftUI's `.textSelection(.disabled)`: `false` turns off every `El::selectable` text in the element and below it (no I-beam, drag, double- or triple-click, Cmd/Ctrl+A, `Tx::select_text`/`Tx::select_all_text` or copy; they leave their `El::selection_group`, unbuilt `virtual_rows` included, and a selection holding one clears at the next commit), and `true` on a descendant turns them back on for values meant to be copied. Set it on the view's root for an app-wide "Enable text selection" setting; trees that don't use it behave as before.
