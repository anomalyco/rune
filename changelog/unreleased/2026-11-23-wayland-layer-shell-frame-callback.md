- **Wayland: layer-shell overlays now pace presentation with `wl_surface.frame` callbacks instead of blocking in `vkQueuePresentKHR`.** `LayerSurface` requests a frame callback before each present on its private event queue, skips acquiring or presenting while a callback is outstanding, steps due timers and pending input without drawing (pausing ambient visual loops until the compositor paints again), and tears down the surface immediately if the GPU device is lost during present.
