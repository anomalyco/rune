- Accessibility: when the focused element leaves the tree (a deleted row, a closed tab, a decided prompt) and nothing else asks for focus, focus goes to the next control after it that is still there, else the one before, inside its nearest surviving container first, instead of to nothing (a screen reader fell back to the window and Tab restarted at the top). F6 and Shift+F6 move focus between landmarks (`banner`, `navigation`, `main`, `complementary`, `form`, `search`, labelled `region`s), back onto what was focused last in each.
