- **`Tx::relaunch` and `AppHandle::relaunch`: the app quits and starts again,** as Electron's `app.relaunch()` with `app.quit()`: every window's `App::on_quit` runs as for a quit, and once the event loop has ended the app starts its own executable again with the same arguments and working folder (an app bundle through `open -n`, as the Finder launches it). The new process waits for the old one to exit before `single_instance` or its first window, so it finds the app's claim and files free. The quit's state machine carries what follows the exit; a session's end (shutdown, logoff) doesn't restart the app.
