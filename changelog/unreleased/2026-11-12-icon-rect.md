- **An icon path that is one rectangle strokes as a rectangle, however thin.** Chromium draws a closed, axis-aligned four-sided path as a rectangle (`SkPath::isRect`, then `drawRect`), covering its stroke's exact area, where any other stroke a device pixel wide or thinner draws as a hairline. Desktop's `outline-dots` (three 1 x 1 squares stroked 1 wide) are now three solid 2 x 2 dots at 1x, as Desktop's are (`#808080` in both themes), where the hairline left them at 75%.
- **A hairline under a device pixel wide fades each piece by its width before the pieces combine,** as Skia's GPU hairlines do, so a corner where two 0.8 px sides meet is `1 - (1 - 0.8a)(1 - 0.8b)`, not `0.8 (a + b - ab)`: Desktop's review and panel toggles (20-unit boxes drawn at 16) now match its raster at 1x within 1 of 255 at every pixel, where their corners were 11 lighter.
