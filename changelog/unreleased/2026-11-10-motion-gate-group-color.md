- **Motion gate: text in a hover group's colour (`El::group_color`) traces the colour it paints.** The trace's and the gate's text colour now mix in the group's hover, as paint does, and a group colour that lands with the pointer is a hover landing, as a hover fill is, not a snap of unexplained pixels. A row whose content slid out from under a still pointer (a fold toggle, a compaction divider) no longer fails the gate.
