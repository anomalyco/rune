- **Focus: `El::preserve_focus_on_press(true)` stops focusable elements and `on_key` or scroll ancestors from taking focus on click.** A focusable element with `preserve_focus_on_press(true)` now takes focus only from the keyboard or `Tx::focus` rather than on pointer down, and clicking its unfocusable descendants no longer focuses an `on_key` or focusable scroll ancestor while explicitly focusable descendants still take focus normally.
