- **rune-notify: `adopt` takes an identity an installer registered without writing it again.** `register` writes `AppUserModelId\<id>` and points the activation scheme at the running executable on every call, so any copy of an app, from any folder, repointed the installed app's registration; `adopt` sets the process's `AppUserModelID` (once per id) and the scheme its toasts open, and writes nothing.
