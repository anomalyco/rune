- macOS `DisplayLink` pacing now adapts automatically to `NSProcessInfo.thermalState` (`Fair`: 60 Hz unsteered / full steered; `Serious`: 30 Hz unsteered / 60 Hz steered; `Critical`: 20 Hz unsteered / 30 Hz steered), extends Low Power Mode's 60 Hz cap to external high-refresh displays, re-polls power and thermal state every second during continuous active streams, drops stale steering immediately when a window loses focus, and adds opt-in `App::unfocused_fps` / `WindowSpec::unfocused_fps` for capping visible unfocused windows without pausing them.
