- **`ScrollBarStyle::native` sizes and places its thumb as Chromium does, and `inside` draws it over the viewport's edge.** The thumb runs on a track 4 points in from the viewport's ends and is sized and placed with its 2 point clear border (32 points long at least), then drawn without it, as `scrollbar.css`'s `margin: 4px 0` and `border: 2px solid transparent` do, so its pill sits 2 points further along and 2 points shorter than before. `ScrollBarStyle::inside` puts the 8 point strip over the viewport's own end edge, in the padding its content leaves while it overflows (a sidebar's plain `overflow-y: auto` list), rather than beside it.
