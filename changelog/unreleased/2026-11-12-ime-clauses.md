- **Input methods: a composition carries its clauses, and committed text can be converted again.** `Ime::Preedit` gains a third field, the composition's clauses (`rune::Clause`: a byte range, and whether it is the one being converted); each draws its own underline, the converting one thick, and the candidate window opens under it. New `Ime::Select(range)`: the input method takes committed text back (Japanese 変換 on a word, macOS ⌃⇧R), the field selects it, and the composition that follows replaces it. Windows reads `GCS_COMPCLAUSE`/`GCS_COMPATTR` and answers `IMR_RECONVERTSTRING`, `IMR_DOCUMENTFEED` and `IMR_CONFIRMRECONVERTSTRING` from the focused field's paragraph; macOS reads `NSMarkedClauseSegment`/`NSUnderline` and answers `selectedRange`, `attributedSubstringForProposedRange:` and `setMarkedText:`'s replacement range (vendored winit). Wayland, X11 and the web give no clauses: the field takes a cursor range that isn't empty as the converting clause, as before. Scripts: `clauses` on a preedit, `select` on an `ime` event. An app matching `Ime::Preedit(text, cursor)` adds `, ..`.
