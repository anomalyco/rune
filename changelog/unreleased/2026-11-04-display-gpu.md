- **On Windows, windows and overlays draw on a GPU that drives a display, and every device commits about 180 MB less.** Rune took the lowest-power adapter, which on a desktop is often an integrated GPU no monitor is plugged into. Every swapchain there is presented across adapters: on a desktop with an RTX 5070 Ti driving the monitor, each one cost the NVIDIA driver about 37 threads (one waking 64 times a second), about 100 MB per full-screen surface on each GPU, and 50 to 160 ms to make instead of 6 ms. A surface now gets the lowest-power adapter among those driving a display, so a laptop whose panel is on its integrated GPU keeps it, and offscreen work keeps the lowest-power adapter. `WGPU_ADAPTER_NAME` and `WGPU_POWER_PREF` still override the choice, and `RUNE_GPU_LOG=1` names the new reason. Devices on every platform ask wgpu for `MemoryHints::MemoryUsage`: `Performance` suballocated from 128 MB and 64 MB blocks that an app holding a few atlases never fills.
