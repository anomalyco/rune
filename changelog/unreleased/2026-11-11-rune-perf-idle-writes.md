- **`rune-perf` writes nothing while the app is idle.** `perf.json` is written with each incident and at shutdown instead of every 2 seconds (30 temp-file renames a minute in the app's data folder before), memory is still checked against the incident rules every 2 seconds, and `perf.log` stays open for appending, so a logged line costs one write instead of an open, a stat and a close.
