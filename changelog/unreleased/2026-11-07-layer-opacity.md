- **`El::layer_opacity` fades a subtree as one layer, like CSS `opacity`.** A label over its button's fill no longer shows the fill faded through it, and overlapping children don't show through each other. Like `El::dim`, it is static: a change lands at once, and animation and hit testing stay with `opacity`. Only an element below 1 whose paint overlaps draws offscreen. Kit's disabled buttons, icon buttons, menu and list items, radios, segments, selects, switches and text inputs use it in place of `dim`, so a disabled Contrast button's label stays as white as Desktop's.
