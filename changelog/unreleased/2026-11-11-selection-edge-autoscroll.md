- **A drag selecting `El::selectable` text keeps scrolling its list while the pointer holds still at or past the list's top or bottom edge, as AppKit text views do.** It stopped as soon as the pointer stopped moving, so selecting further down a long list meant jiggling the pointer below it. Now the list scrolls every frame at ten points a second for each point the pointer is past the edge (80 at the edge itself, 4,000 at most), and the selection follows it to the last row it brings in, into virtual rows that weren't built. A list stuck to its bottom lets go of it to scroll up. It stops on release, Escape, when the pointer moves back inside, when the window loses focus, at the end of the list, and when a step moves nothing. Escape during a selection drag now ends the drag where the selection stands instead of reaching the app, and a modal opening or a drag-out taking the pointer ends it too.
