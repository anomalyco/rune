- **A drag selecting `El::selectable` text keeps scrolling its list while the pointer holds still at or past the list's top or bottom edge, as AppKit text views do.** It stopped as soon as the pointer stopped moving, so selecting further down a long list meant jiggling the pointer below it. Now the list scrolls every frame at ten points a second for each point the pointer is past the edge (80 at the edge itself, 4,000 at most), and the selection follows it, into virtual rows that weren't built. It stops on release, when the pointer moves back inside, when the window loses focus and at the end of the list.
