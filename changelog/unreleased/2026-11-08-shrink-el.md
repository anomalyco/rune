- **An `El` is 192 bytes, three cache lines, down from 696.** Rarely set element fields wait behind one shared pointer, allocated on their first write, and booleans pack into bits, so a plain row, column, box or text allocates nothing extra and moves a quarter of the bytes through every builder, child list and memo hit. `Visual` is 40 bytes: border, hover, press and per-corner radii wait behind their own pointer. Every by-value builder keeps its signature, and the hot ones (size, padding, margins, gaps, alignment, fill, radius, border, hover, press, opacity, text style and colour, key, role, label, help, scroll, children) gain an in-place `set_*` form (`push_child` and `extend_children` for children) for code that builds an element across branches. A compile-time check keeps `El` at 192 bytes.
