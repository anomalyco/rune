- **Windows: a frame that changes hundreds of instances draws whole without looking for where they are.** A scroll moves every row, and `window::damage` hashed, counted and bounded every instance in both lists only to find they covered most of the window. Past 256 instances the new list has that the old doesn't (`MANY`), the frame draws whole at once; a typed glyph, a streamed line or a hover changes far fewer and stays partial. GPUI-BENCH's 5,000-line list scrolling at 240 Hz on blade's Iris Xe, frame log: the UI thread's CPU per frame 1,819-2,236 µs before, 1,452-1,519 after; its encode stage 548-649 µs before, 197-213 after.
