- **Windows: the first GPU frames after the hand-over cost what later frames do.** On D3D12 a command allocator's first two cycles at a given number of lists cost about 0.5 ms a list on an Iris Xe (OpenCode records 11-14 lists a frame), and wgpu gives each encoder its own allocator and rotates encoders while frames are in flight, so a launch met 7 slow frames of 4-15 ms in its first 34. The vendored wgpu-hal now keeps the device's free allocators in one pool and gives a frame the one that has recorded the most (wgpu's pending writes keep their own), and the warm frame records the window's frame on three encoders at once, twice, before the hand-over. OpenCode release-quick, Iris, whole-window redraws, n=8: the first frame after the hand-over 4.1 / 10.5 / 15.1 ms → 2.7 / 4.6 / 4.0 ms (Home, a restored session, ten tabs), the second 2.5 / 8.3 / 13.9 → 1.8 / 2.9 / 2.4 ms; warm frames unchanged. The hand-over comes 30-40 ms later (the warm-up runs on the GPU thread, the CPU frames serve meanwhile), and the warmed allocators cost at most 2.0 MB private and 1.2 MB GPU memory on the Iris, 2.5 MB and 2.5 MB on an RTX, at rest after launch (measured with four).
