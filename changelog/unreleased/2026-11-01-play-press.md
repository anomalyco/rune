- **A playground step can press a key.** `Play::press(Key::Down)` (or a `KeyEvent`, for modifiers; `Key` now converts into one) sends a real key on the step's frame, through focus, `El::on_key`, `AppSpec::on_key` and the rest of the key path, as if a person pressed it with the stage holding the keyboard: the story list, its filter and the side column don't take it, and keep the keyboard after. The motion gate's `Run::step` records that frame as input, and a motion trace gets a `press …` event, so a scroll the key asks for (`Tx::scroll_key_to`) is no longer reported as an input-less teleport. A story replay standing in for a keypress no longer needs an audit allow.
