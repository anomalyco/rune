- **`rune-kit`: `Kit::motion` exposes theme-level `hover` and `press` motion defaults.** `Motion` adds `hover: Option<(Anim, Anim)>` and `press: Option<(Anim, Anim)>` (plus `Default`, `Hash`, `PartialEq`, `Motion::hover_motion(enter, exit)`, and `Motion::press_motion(down, up)`), defaulting to `None` so existing themes keep today's runtime hover/press tracks and `El::hover_motion_within` behaviour unchanged. Setting `hover` and/or `press` applies independently configurable enter/exit and down/release curves across kit controls (`Button`, `IconButton`, `SegmentedControl`, `Checkbox`, `RadioItem`, `Switch`, `Slider`, `Accordion`, `List`, `Table`, `Select`, `TextInput`/`TextArea`/`TextField`, `Toast`, `Tree`, `DiffView`, `Markdown`, and `Kit::interactive`/`interactive_with`) via `El::token_hover_motion` and `El::token_press_motion` (also added alongside `El::hover_motion_curves` / `El::press_motion_curves`), while respecting explicit `El::hover_motion`/`El::press_motion`, enclosing `El::hover_motion_within`, and Reduce Motion. Code constructing `Motion` as an exhaustive struct literal without `..Motion::default()` needs the `hover` and `press` fields.
