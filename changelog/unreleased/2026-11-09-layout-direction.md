- `El::dir(Direction::Rtl | Ltr | Auto)` lays a subtree out right to left, as HTML's `dir` does: rows, start and end alignment, padding, border sides, corner radii, soft clip edges, linear gradients, anchored overlays' sides and horizontal scrolling mirror, text takes a right-to-left paragraph direction (its `Start` is the right edge, a field's placeholder and scroll follow it), `Ltr` keeps an island in its own order and `Auto` takes the first strong character's direction (`Direction::of`). Left and Right move a field's caret across mixed Arabic, Hebrew and Latin text as it's drawn. `El::mirror_in_rtl` flips a directional icon (and turns a rotated one the other way), `El::unmirrored` keeps an element's physical place, `RichLayoutOptions::rtl` lays a paragraph right to left, and `WindowTitlebar::rtl` keeps the room for the window's controls on their side. A window with no right-to-left element lays out and draws as before.
