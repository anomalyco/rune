- **Motion checks judge an element against the scroll it rides.** `ElementInfo::scrolled` says how far its scroll viewports' offsets carry it this frame (a pinned bottom following growing content included, which isn't flagged `scrolling`; a `Tx::launch` in the air, and what it carries, only the part of the scroll its flight has taken on), and `--trace` writes it per element when it changes (`sc`: `[id, x, y]`). The motion gate's overshoot, jiggle and reversal checks, and those plus stall and dog-leg in `scripts/motion-audit/analyze.py`, add it back instead of skipping scrolling elements. A node gliding inside a list that follows its end is one glide, no longer a reversal or jiggle from the scroll adding to it on screen, and a node that wobbles while its list scrolls is now reported.
