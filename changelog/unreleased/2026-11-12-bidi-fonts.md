- **Fonts: Arabic and Hebrew at their own weights, and in code at the code's width.** On Windows a script's fallback loads its whole family, so a medium label in Arabic or Hebrew draws in Segoe UI Semibold and a heading in Segoe UI Bold rather than a faked bold. Monospace text tries a monospace face with Arabic and Hebrew before the proportional fallback (Courier New on Windows and macOS, whose Arabic and Hebrew are 0.6 em wide like IBM Plex Mono's; DejaVu Sans Mono on Linux). The Arabic comma, semicolon and question mark after Latin text no longer draw as missing glyphs. Diff views read left to right in any window.
