- **Windows and Linux: an app that never decodes JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP or ICO no longer links their codecs (578,048 B of a minimal release exe).** The clipboard's images, drag-out pictures and cursor images are PNGs, but they decoded through `image::load_from_memory_with_format`, whose run-time format dispatch links every codec the `image` crate is built with; they now call the PNG decoder alone, with the same limits. `ImageCache` still decodes every format it documents.
