- **A selection across Markdown blocks copies as plain text.** `El::copy_breaks(before, after)` asks for the newlines copying leaves between a selectable text and its neighbours in a `selection_group` (the larger of the two asks, one by default), and `El::copy_prefix` adds text, such as a list item's `- `, before a text taken from its start along with others; a copy of one text alone leaves it out. `Markdown` uses both, so a copy keeps a blank line between paragraphs, headings, quotes and code blocks, puts list items on their own lines after `- `, `1. ` or `- [ ] ` (nested lists indented past their parent's marker), and keeps a code block's lines, where it ran every block together with single newlines and no markers. `TextSelection.text` reads the same. `Markdown::quote_frame` wraps each block quote, handing the app its text as that copy gives it (`QuoteFrame::text`), for a button that copies the whole quote.
