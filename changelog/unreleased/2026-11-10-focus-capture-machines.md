- **Focus and the pointer's hold are explicit state machines.** What has focus, how it shows and where Tab starts while nothing has it is one `Focus` (nothing, or one element, lit by the pointer or not) beside a `Modality`, and type-select's prefix is `TypeSelect`; a held press keeps its origin, its down time, whether it's inside, moved or cancelled, its double-press pairing and an armed drag-out in one `Held`, and only the first click of a possible double press outlives the release. A menu or listbox modal the pointer opened hands focus back to its trigger without a ring, as OpenCode Desktop's menus do, whatever keys moved inside it. On Windows, a click or right-click at the spot where a pop-up menu closed, without moving the mouse, no longer goes missing, and a right-click on selectable text no longer selects the word under it (macOS still does, as AppKit does).
