- **A view can switch its whole titlebar, and keep parts of a drag band still.** `Cx::set_titlebar` replaces `App::titlebar`'s while the view keeps setting it, and `Titlebar::traffic_lights(x, y)` places the macOS traffic lights for that titlebar, so a layout switch moves the lights and changes `ChromeInsets` from the next frame on; `Cx::titlebar_height` still sets the height over it. `El::no_window_drag()` marks a part of a `.window_drag()` band whose presses, double presses and secondary clicks leave the window alone. `Drag::window_point` gives the pointer in window points and `Drag::screen_point` in desktop pixels (`None` headless), for opening a window under a dragged tab.
