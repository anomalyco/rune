- **Overlays advance the runtime once per missed frame.** A resting overlay woken by a timer previously advanced the runtime in `quiet_step` and then advanced it a second time when drawable acquisition failed (`stepped` was passed as `false`). An occluded overlay whose timer and backoff expired in the same turn, or a macOS overlay that timed out on a display tick before `about_to_wait`, could also step twice in one turn. Overlays now forward `built.is_some()` to `missed`, forget the resting frame on miss, align deferred display-link claiming with the window host, and track per-turn frame state in `TurnFrame`.
