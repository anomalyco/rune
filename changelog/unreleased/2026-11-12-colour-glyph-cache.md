- **Colour emoji ride the cross-launch mask cache.** A colour glyph's RGBA image is saved beside the masks, under its own key (a `color` flag in the key, four bytes a texel, cache format 2), and placed from those bytes on the next launch with no paint, as a mask is. Thirty new emoji in a reply cost a first frame 3-12 ms of COLR v1 painting before; a second launch pays a copy each. Every font in the cache is now named by its length, its first and last 64 KiB and its `head` table rather than a hash of the whole file, so the first lookup in a 13 MB emoji font costs microseconds, not milliseconds. Colour glyphs placed after the cache's first write count toward its next one.
