- **A drag inside a link in static text no longer opens it, drops its pressed look, and selects text when `selectable(true)`.** Once pointer travel crosses the drag threshold (`4` pt per axis for mouse, `8` pt Euclidean for touch), the press latches as a drag (so moving out and back no longer opens the link on mouse or touch), ends the link's pressed look, and starts text selection from the press byte when `selectable(true)` (matching `text_input` `Mark::Link`); trembling clicks within 4 pt and touch taps within 8 pt still open the link.
