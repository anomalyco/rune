- **A drag inside a link in static text no longer opens it.** Pressing on a `Link` in `text`,
  dragging and releasing inside the same link ran `El::on_link`, even though the drag selected
  nothing. Now only a click does, as in a text field's `Mark::Link`: a mouse press that moved
  no further than the 4 pt click slop, or a touch that moved no further than the 8 pt touch
  slop. A finger that wobbles a few points while tapping still opens the link.
