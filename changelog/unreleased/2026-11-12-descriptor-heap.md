- **A GPU device asks for 65,536 bound views and buffers, not a million.** wgpu sizes D3D12's shader-visible descriptor heap from `max_non_sampler_bindings`; its default (1,000,000) is a heap of tens of MB on Windows. A bare wgpu window's private bytes went from 82.0 MB to 24.8 MB (a bare D3D12 one is 14.1 MB), and OpenCode (Rune)'s from 318-322 MB to 195-269 MB with working set 208-212 MB -> 154-164 MB, 2.5 s after launch on a Windows laptop. Other backends size nothing from it.
