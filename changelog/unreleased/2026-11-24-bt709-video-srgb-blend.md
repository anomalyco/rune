- Video / native surfaces: BT.709 frames rendered with `srgb_blend` enabled (the default for `App` windows) now decode the BT.709 transfer curve and sRGB-encode the result instead of passing raw BT.709 values through as sRGB (which darkened shadows and mid-tones by up to 16/255 levels). `Transfer::Srgb` in sRGB blend mode still passes through unchanged, and linear blend mode (`srgb_blend: false`) is unchanged.
