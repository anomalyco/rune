- **`El::paint_order(n)` paints an element over its siblings without moving its focus order**, as CSS `z-index` orders positioned siblings: a higher order paints over siblings with a lower one wherever they come in the tree and takes the pointer first where they overlap, while Tab and assistive technology keep tree order. Unlike `El::raise` it stays inside its parent's layers. A floating button placed before the page, for its Tab order, still paints over the page.
