- **Input methods: a composition left mid-way becomes text, typed compositions undo as typing, and the converting clause draws as a native field draws it.** A field that loses focus, a press in the field and the window going inactive now write what the composition shows into the text, as a browser's field and an `NSTextView` do, and tell the platform's input method to drop its own copy (`ImmNotifyIME` with `CPS_CANCEL` on Windows, `discardMarkedText` on macOS) rather than wiping it from the field and leaving it in the input method for the next field. A committed Hangul syllable or kana phrase joins the open undo step as typing does. The clause being converted (the preedit range an input method marks) draws with a 2-point underline, the rest with a 1-point one, each clause a point short at both ends, as Chromium draws them, and the candidate window opens under that clause's start rather than at the caret after it. `App::on_ime` editors hear their last preedit committed when the window goes inactive. A script's `--tree` reports the window's `imeArea` and the focused field's `preedit`. `Runtime::cancel_composition` is now `Runtime::finish_composition`.
