- Windows: `Cx::system_contrast()` reports the system's contrast theme (Settings > Accessibility > Contrast themes) as the colours it asks apps to paint with (`SystemContrast`: window, text, highlight, highlight text, disabled, button, button text, link; CSS `forced-colors`' system colours), and reduced motion and the contrast theme are read again the moment Windows says they changed (`WM_SETTINGCHANGE`, `WM_SYSCOLORCHANGE`, `WM_THEMECHANGED`), not only when a window is next activated.
