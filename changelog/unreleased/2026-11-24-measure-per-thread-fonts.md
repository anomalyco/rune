- Text: `rune::text::measure`, `advance`, `ink`, and `font_epoch` now keep a thread's own `TextSystem` fonts and families isolated from `TextSystem`s built on other threads, while threads that never build a `TextSystem` still inherit the process-wide fonts and follow `Tx::set_font_families`. Added `rune::text::with_fonts` and `FontsGuard` for thread-scoped font and family overrides.
