- **Kit: `TextInput::dir` sets the value's direction.** `TextInput::new(&edit).dir(Direction::Auto)` takes the direction from the value's first strong character, as `dir="auto"` on an `input` does: a name in Arabic or Hebrew starts at the right in a left-to-right window, and an English one at the left in a right-to-left window. Without it the field reads the window's way, as before.
