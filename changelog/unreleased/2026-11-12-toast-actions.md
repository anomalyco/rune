- **rune-notify: notifications you answer where you are (`Notification::action`, `Notification::reply`, `Click::action`, `Click::input`), and the toast activator (`serve_activations`, `wait_activation`).** Windows toasts carry up to five buttons and a text box; a click comes back to `on_click` with the button and the words typed, and the app doesn't come forward. While the app runs the toast's own event takes it; where an install registered the app's COM activator (`CustomActivator`, `LocalServer32`), buttons are background activations that reach `serve_activations`'s class object in the running process, or start the app for `wait_activation` after it quit.
