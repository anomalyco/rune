- **Native surfaces: a window's first video no longer freezes it while the video shader compiles.** The native-surface renderer was made in the first frame that drew a surface, compiling its pipeline on the UI thread (FXC on Windows: about 300 ms on blade, during which no input or wake was handled). A window or overlay now compiles it on a thread of its own when its first surface is drawn and draws that surface once it's ready; its live wake asks for the frame. A clip that's opening has no picture yet, so nothing is lost. Headless runs and tools still compile in place.
