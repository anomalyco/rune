- **Text: Windfoil's glyph masks are kept across launches.** `text::mask_cache`: a glyph whose mask an earlier launch of the same build made is placed from those bytes, with no colour check, outline or fill. Keys name each font by a hash of its bytes and the whole cache by the build's link stamp (Windows), so another build, font, size, phase or mode misses and fills as before. The app hands the saved bytes in with `offer` (any thread, decoded there) and gets them to keep through `save_with` (a thread of the cache's own, 3 s after the first frame, then at most every 30 s while the masks in use change; 1 MiB, masks in use first). On a dense page at 2880x1800 the cold first frame's glyph placement drops from 5,374 µs to 1,890 and its mask fill from 6,118 µs to none, pixel-identical.
