- **Motion has a cause.** A commit that no input in its window caused (a poll, a wake, data a server brought, a launch's frames after the first) mounts and unmounts at once: no entrance or exit transition, no `enter_delay` stagger, rows new to a growth stack land at their height, and text it mounts shows whole. What was already on screen keeps its motion (layout and colour springs, streaming growth and reveal, loops), and a handler that means the motion calls `Tx::animated`. Every element's default 0.2 s entrance fade had made content that arrived after the first frame fade in: OpenCode (Rune)'s Home list took 1.7-2.2 s after its window showed to stop changing on Windows (0.4-0.45 s now), and on a Mac its text faded 142 ms past the content being live.
