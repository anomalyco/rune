- **Windows: winit's hidden helper window no longer shows up to screen readers and window pickers.** UI Automation listed it beside the app's window as an unnamed 22×22 pane at the screen's corner, in the views Narrator and other screen readers walk. It is now DWM-cloaked. It stays visible to Windows, because winit needs `WM_PAINT` on it to keep the loop running during a live resize or move, and a cloaked window still gets `WM_PAINT` where a hidden or message-only one doesn't.
