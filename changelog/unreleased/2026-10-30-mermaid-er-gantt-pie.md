- **Mermaid draws ER diagrams, gantt charts and pie charts.** `erDiagram` entities are attribute tables (type, name, keys, comment) joined in crow's-foot notation, symbolic or worded, with labels. `gantt` reads `dateFormat`, `axisFormat`, `tickInterval`, sections, `done`/`active`/`crit`/`milestone`/`vert` tasks, durations, `after`/`until` links, `excludes` and `inclusiveEndDates`, on a calendar-aligned time axis. `pie` draws slices with percentages and a legend, with values under `showData`. Charts colour their series from a palette that follows the diagram's light or dark surface. These three no longer fall back to source (t1058).
