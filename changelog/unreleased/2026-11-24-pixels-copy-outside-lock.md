- Streaming `Pixels`: `ImageAtlas::place_at` now snapshots the frame's metadata and `Arc` pixel buffer under the lock and releases the mutex before allocating atlas pages or copying rows, so a producer thread's `Pixels::set` no longer blocks behind the render thread's copy. Replaced buffers in `Pixels::set` and `Pixels::store_mips` are also dropped outside the lock, and `Pixels::rgba` clones outside the lock.
