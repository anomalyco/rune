- **A click on a `TabStrip` tab no longer runs the view a second time when the button comes up.** The press selects the tab, and the release of a press that never became a drag changes nothing the strip draws, so it's `Tx::live_only` (as is the click that the press already handled), and only a drag's release animates. In OpenCode a tab switch commits once for the press instead of once more, with no pixel changed, five frames later.
