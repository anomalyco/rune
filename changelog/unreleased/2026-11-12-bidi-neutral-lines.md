- **Direction: text with no strong character follows what's around it.** `El::dir(Direction::Auto)` with no strong character (empty, digits, a path) takes its parent's direction rather than left to right, so an empty field in an Arabic window starts at the right. In a by-paragraph text (`El::dir_by_paragraph`), a line with none takes the direction of the line before it, and the first lines take the element's, as a word processor does: an empty line under an Arabic one starts at the right, with its caret there. Left and Right collapse a field's selection to the end drawn that way (Left in an Arabic line goes to its logical end). `El::truncate_fade` fades where the text is cut, the end of its own paragraph, so an Arabic title in a left-to-right row keeps its first words.
