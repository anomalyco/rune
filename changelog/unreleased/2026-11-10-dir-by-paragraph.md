- **`El::dir_by_paragraph`: each paragraph of a text reads in the direction of its own words,** as CSS's `unicode-bidi: plaintext` lays out a `white-space: pre-wrap` block in Chromium: an English line under an Arabic one runs left to right from its left edge, an Arabic line under it right to left from its right edge, and a line with no letters left to right, while the element keeps its own direction, box and place. A text whose lines all take one direction stays one text; one whose lines differ becomes a column of one text per line that selects and copies as one.
