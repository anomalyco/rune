- Linux: `rune::desktop_setting(namespace, key, on_change)` follows any key of the XDG Settings portal (`org.freedesktop.portal.Settings`), such as `org.gnome.desktop.interface` `text-scaling-factor`, `cursor-blink` or `cursor-blink-time`, until the returned `DesktopSettingWatch` is dropped: the shared appearance-portal worker reads the key between its `SettingChanged` signals and `on_change` gets each new `DesktopSetting` (bool, integer, double or string) in order on a dispatch thread of its own, where a panicking callback is dropped without stopping Rune's own `color-scheme`, reduced-motion and double-click updates. `on_change` is never called on other platforms, without the `dbus` feature, or when no portal answers.
