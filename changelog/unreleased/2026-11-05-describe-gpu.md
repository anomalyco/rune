- **`rune::describe_gpu(device)` names the backend a device draws with, its GPU, and why.** For an app's log: "Rune's Metal backend on Apple M4 (the default)", or "wgpu Metal on Apple M4 (a compositor wants …, which Rune's Metal backend lacks)", or the `RUNE_GPU_BACKEND` or `App::gpu_backend` choice. A window's compositor finds its device in `compose::Frame::device`. With `RUNE_GPU_LOG=1`, Rune prints it for each window and overlay GPU it makes, and the adapter line names the backend of the adapter it picked rather than the one the app chose.
