- **`ScrollBarStyle::scroll_view` draws `@opencode/ui`'s ScrollView thumb.** A 4 point `border_muted` pill down the middle of a 12 point strip, 8 points in from the ends and at least 32 long, `border_strong` under the pointer or while dragged. It is always an overlay, whatever the system's scroll bar setting: hidden at rest, shown while the pointer is over the area or for 800 ms after a scroll, fading over 200 ms.
