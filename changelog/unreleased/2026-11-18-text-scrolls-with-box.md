- **Text in a scrolling list moves in whole pixels with its box.** A scroll's offset and a growth stack's window offset no longer move what text lays out against, so while a list follows its end as a reply grows or a prompt arrives, text no longer drifts by fractions of a pixel against its row (up to 0.9 px on 24-48 frames of a delivered prompt at 1x, 1.5x and 1.875x before). At rest, text inside a scroller is drawn from the scroll offset as the rows round it, as Chromium draws a scroller's contents from its pixel-snapped offset; resting pixels change only where the scroll offset sits on a fraction of a device pixel.
