- Added opt-in `El::reveal_caret_on_focus(bool)`, `rune_kit::TextArea::reveal_caret_on_focus(bool)`, and `rune_kit::TextInput::reveal_caret_on_focus(bool)` (default `false`) to scroll enclosing scroll viewports to a text input's caret when focus moves to the field (via Tab, F6, `Tx::focus`, `TextEdit::focused`, modal focus, or assistive technology), leaving already-visible carets and wheel-scrolled focused fields untouched.
