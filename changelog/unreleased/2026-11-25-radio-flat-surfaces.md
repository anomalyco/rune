- **rune-kit `RadioItem`: the control's look follows `@opencode/ui`'s radio.** A control that is not selected is flat: its surface with no top light (it had a 6% one). An invalid one's ring takes `c.danger.fg` (it took `c.danger.border`). The selected dot has the depth gradient over its grey, a ring in `c.icon_contrast` (it was the depth highlight) and `elevation.elements` as its drop (the same drop in the OpenCode and neutral kits; none in a kit whose `elements` is empty). New `RadioGroup::surface` / `RadioItem::surface` with `RadioSurface::{Base, Layer1}`: the fill of a control that is neither selected nor invalid, `c.bg_base` unless set, `c.layer_01` for a group on a raised layer. Sizes, hover and focus are unchanged. kit-gallery has `ui-radio--surfaces`.
