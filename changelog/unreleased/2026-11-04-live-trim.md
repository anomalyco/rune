- **Shapes trim live, so a countdown ring or a travelling dash is one clean line drawn every frame.**
  `Shape::trimmed(start, end)` keeps the stretch of a shape's strokes between two fractions of
  their combined length, with caps at both cut ends (`Shape::drawn(t)` is `trimmed(0, t)`), and
  `El::live_trim` binds that `[start, end]` span to a `Live` or `Tween`, repainting the shape
  each frame with no view rebuild. A presence draw traces the trimmed span on from its start.
