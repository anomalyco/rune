- Window recreation: windows destroyed when hidden (`App::release_gpu_when_closed`, Wayland unmapping, or GPU surface loss) now preserve their last maximized, full-screen (including whether maximized underneath), and restored size and position when recreated instead of reopening restored at the initial size; `App::maximized` and `App::full_screen` still govern the first show, and adopted macOS panels and non-resizable windows remain unaffected.
