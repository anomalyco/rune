- **COLRv1 colour glyphs draw in colour.** Emoji fonts that only carry COLRv1 paint graphs, such as the Noto Color Emoji that Fedora installs, render as colour images instead of falling back to outlines. Layers, glyph and box clips, solid fills, linear, radial and sweep gradients with pad, repeat and reflect, transforms, every composite mode and the default palette are supported, and palette entry `0xFFFF` takes the text's colour. Gradients interpolate in premultiplied linear light, as the COLR specification requires. Bitmap (CBDT, sbix) and COLRv0 glyphs still render through swash, and SVG-only fonts stay ineligible.
