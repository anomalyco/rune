- **Windows: a CPU-drawn window answers a repaint the system asks for with its whole frame.** When Windows asks a window to paint again (an update region: a display change, a surface DWM threw away), the CPU renderer drew only what changed, which is nothing, and the system then counted it painted: the window could show nothing until something changed. Now such a frame is drawn whatever the occlusion tracker said last and copied whole. On the visible desktop a CPU window covered whole for 4 s comes back with its current content in the first composed frame.
