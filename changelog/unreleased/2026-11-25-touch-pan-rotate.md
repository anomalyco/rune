- Touch: added `WheelEvent::touch: bool` (default `false`) and `WheelEvent::touch(bool)` so two-finger touchscreen drags deliver midpoint panning to `El::on_wheel` (handler-only, locked from `Began` to `Ended`, never scrolling outer scroll views, 8 pt slop subtracted on activation, emitted before `El::on_magnify` on the same move), and routed two-finger touchscreen turns to `El::on_rotate` (counterclockwise degrees after 8° slop with a 16 pt minimum span, locked from `Began` to `Ended`, alongside macOS trackpad rotation).
