- **A canvas draws from its box's origin on the device grid, at its exact scale.** At rest a shape (`rune::canvas`) draws from its box's exact origin rounded to the device grid, as Chromium draws a canvas or an inline SVG, so what it holds keeps its geometry against the grid. It was centred in its snapped box, which moved its drawing by up to half a device pixel whenever the box's size snapped: a sheet's 1 px header line at 1.5x covered a different split of rows than Desktop's (off by up to 39 levels). A view-boxed shape still stretches to its box; a moving or scaled one stays centred in its snapped box, concentric with the icons and fills in the same box.
