- **Web: mirror unfocused text inputs, text areas, and password fields in `[data-rune-a11y]` with zero secret leakage and single-node focus hand-over.** Unfocused `text_input`, `text_area`, and password fields now appear in the web accessibility DOM mirror as `role="textbox"` (or `searchbox`) with their accessible name (label or placeholder), `aria-multiline`, `aria-readonly`, `aria-disabled`, `aria-invalid`, `aria-required`, `aria-placeholder`, `aria-autocomplete`, `aria-description`, and plain value (always empty for secure/password fields). Activating or focusing a mirrored textbox focuses the real Rune field through `focusInput(h)`, syncs `aria-label` and ARIA states onto the active `<textarea>` or `<input type="password">`, clears the canvas focus proxies, and omits the focused input from `[data-rune-a11y]` so assistive technology sees one control before and during focus.
