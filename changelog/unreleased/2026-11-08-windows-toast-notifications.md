- **Windows posts system notifications.** A new `rune-notify` crate holds the notifications `rune_mac::notify` had (which now re-exports it) and adds Windows toasts: `register(&Identity::new(id, name).icon(path))` gives the process its `AppUserModelID` (its windows group under it on the taskbar) and registers the name and icon its toasts show, for an app without a package or Start-menu shortcut. `post`, `remove`, `on_click`, `app_focused` and `bring_to_front` work as on macOS: a toast is silent, carries its user info as launch arguments, and a click reaches the handler while the posting process runs; `bring_to_front` restores and focuses its frontmost window.
