- **The motion gate fails a scroll that runs the view.** A new `scroll-commit` finding is reported when an app's `poll`, a handler or a layout read commits on 6 or more of a scroll's frames and at least a quarter of them; a cell that scrolls this way needs a fix or an `Allow` row. Debug builds' scroll-commit warning now names the file and line of a `Cx::layout`, `layout_with`, `layout_size`, `window_layout` or `window_layout_with` read that moved.
