- `rune-kit` `Table`: added `Table::row(|s, i, el| -> El)` to customise each mounted virtual row after the table's styling and press handlers (for context menus, drag handlers, or row decorations while preserving `Cell::text` truncation tooltips), `Table::stick_bottom(bool)` (default `false`) to keep appended rows in view while scrolled to the end, and `Table::cursor_outline(bool)` (default `true`) to hide the 1 pt multi-select cursor focus outline without changing `TableStyle` literals.
