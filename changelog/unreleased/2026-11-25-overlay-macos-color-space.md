- **macOS overlays: added `ConfiguredOverlay::macos_color_space(MacColorSpace)` opt-out.** Overlays stay colour-managed as `MacColorSpace::Srgb` by default, and apps that want raw display-native values on a macOS overlay panel can now opt out with `.macos_color_space(MacColorSpace::Unmanaged)` (clearing both `NSPanel.colorSpace` and `CAMetalLayer.colorspace` to `nil`), matching `MacWindowOptions::color_space`. Ignored on other platforms.
