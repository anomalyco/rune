- **rune-theme-opencode: the elevation tokens list their layers in Figma's order**, as `@opencode/ui` now does: `raised`, `floating`, `overlay`, `input`, `button_neutral`, `button_contrast`, `switch_off` and `switch_on` are the same layers reversed (the 0.5 px ring first, then the drops, nearest first; in dark the top light line before the ring). The first layer paints on top, so in dark, where a light ring meets dark drops, the ring of every card, popover, menu, dialog and neutral button now paints over the drops instead of under them: a little lighter where a drop lies on it, mostly along the lower edge. In light only the contrast button can differ (its opaque grey ring now lies over its drop, and its lower inset over the upper one); the other light tokens are black over black, which looks the same in either order. An app that reads a layer of `Kit::elevation` by index gets another layer.
