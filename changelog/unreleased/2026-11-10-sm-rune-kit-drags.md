- **rune-kit drags end when their release is lost:** a sidebar row being reordered, a pane's grip, a table column's edge and a scroll bar's thumb or track press each hold one grip from press to release. When the release never arrives (the dragged element unmounted mid-drag, or a modal took the pointer), the next press starts clean and the next pointer move the drag doesn't hear lets go, so no row stays lifted, no thumb stays wide, and the next reorder moves the row that was pressed instead of the stale one.
