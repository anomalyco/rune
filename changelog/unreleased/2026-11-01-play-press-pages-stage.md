- **A Page Up, Page Down, Home or End a playground step presses pages the stage.** With no focus inside the stage and the pointer elsewhere, `Play::press` fell back to the first scroll view in the tree, the story list, rather than one on the stage. While a step's key is routed, every page-key fallback now looks only inside the stage, and pages nothing when nothing there overflows; a person's keys page as before.
