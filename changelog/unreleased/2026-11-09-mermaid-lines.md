- **rune-mermaid: thin lines at any size, and subgraphs that never overlap.** Lines now shrink with the rest of a diagram, as an SVG's do. Before, they stopped thinning at 80%, and that floor was stored in the full-size drawing, so a diagram shrunk to fit a narrow column (`min_scale(0.0)`) drew 3–4 pt lines once it was shown larger. Sibling subgraphs keep one order on every rank, the one most ranks agree on, like dagre's subgraph constraints. Before, two subgraphs whose ranks interleave could swap places from one rank to the next, and their boxes overlapped. Arrowheads are a little smaller, closer to Mermaid's marker, and `classDef … stroke-width` now sets a node's outline.
