- **Right-to-left keys and copies.** In a right-to-left window, Left and Right swap in a horizontal focus group (tabs, segmented controls, radio rows), on a slider, and in a Kit tree (`Tree::right_to_left`: Left opens a folder, Right closes it). Copying cells across a right-to-left table row joins them with tabs as a left-to-right row does. A tab's title fades at the end of its own words, so a Latin title in a right-to-left tab starts at its left rather than under the close button. Luau UI text reads the way its words do (`dir="auto"`) unless the node sets `dir`.
