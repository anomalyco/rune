- `OverlayHandle::after_hidden`: runs a one-shot callback on the event thread once an overlay's hide has taken effect—waiting for a Wayland `wl_display.sync` roundtrip after the surface is destroyed (keeping the overlay live for `wayland_wake` while the sync is out), or the turn after the hide elsewhere; showing the overlay again before the answer keeps the callback waiting for the next hide, and closing or ending the app drops it unrun (#2460).
