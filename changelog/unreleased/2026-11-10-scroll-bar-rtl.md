- **A right-to-left viewport's scroll bar sits on its left:** `ScrollPos::direction` says which way a viewport runs, and Kit's scroll bars take their end edge from it, as a browser places a `dir="rtl"` box's scroll bar and Desktop's ScrollView its `inset-inline-end` thumb: a vertical bar draws, reveals and drags on the left, and a sideways bar's thumb starts at the right and runs left as the content scrolls. A sideways thumb also keeps its length while it widens under the pointer.
