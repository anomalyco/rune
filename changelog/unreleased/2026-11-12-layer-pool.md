- **Opening a menu, a popover or the command palette again makes no GPU texture: a layer target no layer drew into is kept for the next layer of its size.** Targets were dropped when their layer closed or moved in the frame's plan, and made again on the next open (a D3D12 committed resource, its view, buffers and bind groups). Spare targets are kept up to 32 MB, least recently used first out. OpenCode's command palette opening a second time encodes in 128-142 µs instead of 1,642-1,977 (blade, release). Also: `frame-profile`'s live log gives each GPU stage's CPU time (`acquire_cpu`, `encode_cpu`, `submit_cpu`, `present_cpu`), so a wait reads apart from work.
