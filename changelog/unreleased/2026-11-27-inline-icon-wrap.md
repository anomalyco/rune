- **Text: inline icons and leading pill/span spacers stay on the same line as their word when wrapping long URLs or tokens.** When a leading inline box (such as a Markdown link icon) starts a line before an unbroken word wider than the remaining room, line breaking no longer leaves the box orphaned on a zero-height line that overlaps the row above: under `wrap_anywhere` (`overflow-wrap: break-word`) it breaks inside the word within the line width, and under normal wrapping it keeps the box with its first word. Non-whitespace prefixes immediately before an inline icon or pill (such as `(` in `(https://...)`) move down together with the icon and word instead of stranding the opening punctuation at the end of the previous line.
