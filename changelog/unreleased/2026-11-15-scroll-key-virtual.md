- `Tx::scroll_key_to` lands a virtual row that isn't mounted yet: the jump goes where its list's estimates put it, which mounts it, and is placed again from its layout before the frame draws. It used to do nothing for such a row, so a viewport stayed where it was (a `stick_bottom` one on its end) unless a `Tx::scroll_into_view` had mounted the row first. The rows a jump mounts show at once instead of fading in as insertions. A long transcript opened at a place kept from the last run draws that place in its first frame.
