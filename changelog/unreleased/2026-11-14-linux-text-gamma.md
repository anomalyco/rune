- **Text on Linux: `FontSmoothing::Off` takes Linux Chromium's text gamma, not Windows'.** With smoothing off on Linux (and in a browser on Linux) glyph coverage follows a fit of the curve Skia draws there (`SK_GAMMA_EXPONENT=1.2`, `SK_GAMMA_CONTRAST=0.2`), so light text on a dark page draws 7-14% lighter than before. Only `Off`: Linux's default, `WindfoilChromium`, and the other outline modes keep the curve they had. Not yet checked against a Linux capture. Windows, a browser on Windows and Apple platforms are unchanged.
