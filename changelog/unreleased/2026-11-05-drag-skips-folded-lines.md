- **A drag in a selection group stays out of the hidden lines of a partly folded text.** A
  long text folded to its first lines (a long prompt in a chat) still lay out under the words
  after it, so a drag through a reply below it took its focus to the hidden lines and selected
  everything from there, as if the selection started at the top of the reply. Bands now use
  only the part of each text that shows.
