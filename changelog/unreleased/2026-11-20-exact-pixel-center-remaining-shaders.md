- **Backdrop composite, Wayland CSD corners, `rune-fx` effects and `rune-light` G-buffer shaders evaluate coverage at exact pixel centres.** `backdrop_composite.wgsl`, `csd_corners.rs`, `rune-fx/common.wgsl` (plus `distort`, `extras`, `glow`, `pulse`, `electric`, `fire` and `motion`) and `rune-light/gbuffer.wgsl` now evaluate shape SDFs, clips, corner fades and sample UVs from `@builtin(position)` (with `projection.xy` in `backdrop_composite.wgsl` and `pixel(in)` in `rune-fx` to preserve `draw_layer_in`'s shifted viewport origin) rather than vertex-interpolated quad coordinates, eliminating subpixel ULP drift on D3D12 that leaked edge coverage in `Rgba16Float` targets, broke `draw_layer_in` origin invariance and caused top/left edge asymmetry in `rune-light`'s G-buffer.
