- **Metal: GPU-side synchronization with other queues, devices and APIs.** `rune_gpu::metal::wait_for_event(encoder, event, value)` makes the GPU wait, before the passes an encoder records next, until a Metal event reaches a value; `signal_event_on_finish(encoder, event, value)` sets an event once all the encoder's work finished. Both are encoded in the encoder's own command buffer, as Dawn and Skia Graphite do, so they order exactly that work.
