- **Linux: one running process per app, as on Windows.** `rune::single_instance` binds a Unix socket in the user's runtime folder (`$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, else a 0700 folder of the user's own under the temporary folder). A later launch sends its arguments there and ends once the first process answers: its links go to `App::on_open_url`, and a launch without links reopens the app. A socket left by a process that died is removed and claimed; one held by a process that doesn't answer within 50 ms is taken over; a launch while the app quits waits for it to let go (`rune::release_instance`). Launches claim one at a time, behind a lock beside the socket. Before, every Linux launch opened a second process.
