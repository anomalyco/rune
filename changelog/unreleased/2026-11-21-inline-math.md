- Markdown (`math` feature) renders inline math, `$e^x$`, `\(…\)` and `$$…$$`, on the text's baseline in paragraphs, headings, lists, quotes and tables, and a paragraph that is one `$$` or `\[` equation as display math; prices and shell variables (`$0.60 to $12.50`, `$HOME/$USER`) stay text, and math still arriving or failing shows its source. `El::inline_image` sets an image in a line in place of its bytes, which selection, copy and search still see; `math::may_have_math` tells a host which documents ask the math cache.
