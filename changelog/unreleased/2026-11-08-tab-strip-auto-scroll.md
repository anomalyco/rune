- **`TabStrip` scrolls while a tab is dragged near its ends, and Tab reaching a tab shows its close button, as Desktop's tab strips do.** Within 5% of the strip's length from an end that has more to show, the strip scrolls every frame by up to 8 points, faster nearer the end, and the dragged tab keeps to the pointer as it does (dnd-kit's `AutoScroller` with `acceleration: 8`). A drag starts once the pointer has travelled 4 points either way, as dnd-kit's `Distance` constraint measures it; a finger's 8 points are the runtime's touch slop. A tab focused from the keyboard shows its close button, hides its status mark and takes the hover wash, as `tab-nav.css`'s `:is(:hover, :has(:focus-visible))` rules do, through the new `El::hover_group_focus`, a hover group that keyboard focus on it or inside it hovers too.
