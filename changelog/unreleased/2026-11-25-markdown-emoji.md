- **Added `Markdown::emoji` for custom emoji pictures.** `Markdown::emoji(|name: &str| -> Option<Pixels>)` draws each `:name:` shortcode in prose as the app's picture, 1.2 em tall on the baseline and as wide as its aspect ratio, in place of the shortcode's bytes. Shortcodes inside inline code, math, or a link that shows its own address are left alone, as is any shortcode the app has no picture for. Selection, copy and search still see `:name:`, and table columns size for the picture rather than the shortcode text. The pictures are drawn only: they take no hover or press, and this isn't a general hook for custom inline elements. A document with `emoji` set skips Markdown's whole-document render cache. Settled blocks are still reused, keyed on the pictures they draw.
