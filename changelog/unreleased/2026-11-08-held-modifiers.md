- **`App::on_modifiers` hears the modifier keys held down each time they change, for a reveal while a key such as Alt is held.** It runs wherever focus is and whatever takes the key, as a web page's window-level `keydown` and `keyup` for `Alt` do, and once with none held when the window loses focus, so an app can show more while Alt is down (Desktop's Home shows every session's worktree and branch) without turning on `App::modifier_keys`. `Windows::on_modifiers` does the same per window, and a headless script's `{"hold": "alt"}` holds modifiers until `{"hold": ""}`.
