- **A diff of two texts can keep another number of context lines.** `FileDiff::from_texts_with_context(path, old, new, context)` and `FileDiff::between_with_context(...)` diff two texts with `context` unchanged lines around each change instead of `git diff`'s three, merging hunks whose context would touch; four matches jsdiff and so OpenCode Desktop's review. `from_texts` and `between` keep three.
