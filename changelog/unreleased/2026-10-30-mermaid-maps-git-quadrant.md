- **Mermaid draws mindmaps, timelines, user journeys, git graphs and quadrant charts.** `mindmap` nests by indentation and splits its branches to either side of the root, each in its own colour, with the square, rounded, circle, bang, cloud and hexagon shapes. `timeline` draws periods and their events by section, running top to bottom when they don't fit across. `journey` draws tasks under their sections with a face for each score and a legend of actors. `gitGraph` draws a lane per branch with merges, cherry-picks, tags and the reverse and highlight commit types, left to right or (`TB:`/`BT:`) down and up. `quadrantChart` draws the four named quadrants, axis ends and styled points. These no longer fall back to source (t1058).
