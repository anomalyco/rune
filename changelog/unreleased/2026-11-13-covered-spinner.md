- **Windows: a covered window with a spinner on screen costs nothing but a look now and then.** Its check whether it's still covered (`window::look`) ran by asking for a frame, which the covered window then drew for no one (`CoveredDraw`); the look now runs on its own, without a frame, and backs off from 250 ms to once a second while the window stays covered. Windows has no signal for an uncovered window to use instead: on blade's Iris Xe, a test present on a DirectComposition flip swap chain returns `S_OK` and DXGI's occlusion-status event stays clear whether the window is covered whole or minimised. OpenCode with its spinners, covered and still, on blade: 29.6 wakes a second before, 9.88 with the look apart from frames, 2.4 with the backoff too, and no frame drawn in 5 s; uncovered, the spinner moves again 290,000-420,000 µs later.
