- **Read-only text fields (`El::read_only(true)`, `rune_kit::TextInput::read_only`, `rune_kit::TextArea::read_only`).** Bound `text_input` fields marked `.read_only(true)` (directly or on an enclosing `role("textbox")` wrapper) now match HTML `<input readonly>` / `<textarea readonly>`: they keep focus, a visible caret, pointer/keyboard navigation, `Shift` selection, `Cmd/Ctrl+A`, `Cmd/Ctrl+C`, and AccessKit `Focus`/`Click`/`SetTextSelection`, while refusing typing, `Backspace`/`Delete`, `Enter`, `Tab` indentation, Cut, Paste (`Cmd/Ctrl+V`, `Shift+Insert`, `Runtime::paste`), Undo/Redo, rich formatting chords, IME composition (`ime_area` and web editing mirror stay inactive), spellcheck underlines/suggestions, and AccessKit `SetValue`/`ReplaceSelectedText`; the field context menu disables Cut and Paste, omits spellcheck entries, and keeps Copy and Select All. Added `TextInput::read_only(bool)` and `TextArea::read_only(bool)` forwarders in `rune-kit` and the `rune-text-input--read-only` story in `examples/kit-gallery`.
