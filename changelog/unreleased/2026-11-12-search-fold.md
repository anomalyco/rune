- **`rune_kit::search`: search that matches as a person expects in every language.** `Fold::for_language(tag).find(text, query)` folds case by Unicode's full folding (`ß`/`ẞ` as `ss`, final `ς`, `İ` as `i`, and `I` as `ı` in Turkish and Azerbaijani). It folds full-width ASCII and half-width katakana, voiced marks joined (`ＡＰＩ`, `ﾃｽﾄ`, `ｶﾞ`). Accents fold only when the query has none: `viet` finds `Việt`, `tiếng` skips `tieng`. Matches are byte ranges of the text as given. `markdown::Case::Folded` finds the same way in rendered Markdown. The data is the NFD tables Parley already links.
