- **`App::turn_scope(enter, leave)`.** `enter` runs before each turn of a native window's event handling that can reach its state (an event, a wake, a frame, a hook, a quit), and `leave` after it, once however deep the turn goes, so state an app's windows share can move into the state of the window whose turn it is and back with no `unsafe` and no shared borrow: the windows of one event loop take turns, never two at once.
