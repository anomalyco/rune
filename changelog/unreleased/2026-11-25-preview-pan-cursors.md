- **rune-kit `Preview::pan_cursors(bool)`: whether a zoomed image in the full-window preview shows the open hand (and the closed one while it is dragged).** The preview set `Cursor::Grab` and `Cursor::Grabbing` on its body after the app's hooks ran, so an app whose design keeps the arrow there could not have it. On unless set, so nothing changes for an app that doesn't call it; off, the preview sets no cursor. Dragging pans either way.
