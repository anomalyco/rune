- **Enumerate installed system font families with `rune::text::installed_families()`.** Added `InstalledFamily` (`name()`, `monospace()`, and sorted `100..=900` `weights()`) and `installed_families() -> &'static [InstalledFamily]` backed by CoreText on Apple platforms, DirectWrite on Windows, and fontconfig on Linux/Unix (returning `&[]` on `wasm32` and Android), populated lazily once per process in a `OnceLock` with zero launch cost and excluding `.`-prefixed hidden families.
