- **`El::enter_in_instant()` and `El::matched_in_place()`:** `El::enter_in_instant()` lets a newly mounted element play its `El::transition` entrance even during a `Tx::instant()` commit while layout and removals still snap and first frames, `Tx::settle()`, concealing commits, unprompted commits, and ancestor `El::instant_changes()` still land at once. `El::matched_in_place()` keeps a flying `El::matched` element in its destination's paint order and ancestor compositing layers (clips, rounded clips, opacity, blur, turns) instead of lifting above the whole window, so sliding indicators such as segmented-control pills stay behind labels painted after them (`examples/matched --scene in-place`).
