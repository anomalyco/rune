- macOS: an overlay shown without a fade (`OverlayHandle::show`, prewarmed or not) presents its first frame at once instead of about 250 ms late. AppKit reports a panel as occluded for some milliseconds after it orders in, and the overlay took that reading as covered and waited out its occluded retry; its first two frames now presume the panel on screen, as a launcher panel's do, until AppKit reports it visible or it hides.
