- **`App::titlebar(Titlebar::new(height))`: a titlebar the app draws itself, with the window's own controls over it on every platform.** On Windows the native title bar goes while the window keeps its resize edges, Aero Snap, the Windows 11 snap layouts flyout over maximize, its animations and its window menu (Alt+Space, or a right-click on a `.window_drag()` region); Rune draws the minimize, maximize and close buttons at the trailing edge as Electron's `titleBarOverlay` does (46 points wide, the band's height, hover and press washes of the symbol colour, close turns red), named for assistive technology. On macOS it sets a full-size content view with drag regions and centres the traffic lights in the band unless `Chrome::traffic_lights` places them; Linux keeps its native frame. `Cx::chrome_insets()` reports what the controls take (`leading`, `trailing`, `height`, all 0 where the native frame stays), so a view stops hard-coding a traffic-light gap; `Cx::caption_color` sets the caption symbols' colour for a theme that isn't the system's, and `Tx::toggle_maximized` maximizes or restores the window. `TitlebarAction` gains `SystemMenu`. Headless runs on Windows draw the caption buttons at rest. `crates/rune/examples/custom_titlebar.rs` shows it.
