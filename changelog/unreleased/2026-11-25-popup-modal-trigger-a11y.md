- Accessibility: anchored popup modals (`menu`, `listbox`, `tree`, `grid`, `treegrid`) no longer report the `modal` state (matching WAI-ARIA 1.2 and Chromium) and now keep their anchor trigger—and any enclosing outer modal holding that trigger—readable (inert to actions) in both AccessKit and the web DOM mirror while pointer and keyboard input stay blocked outside the modal; `rune-kit` `Menu` and `Select` triggers now expose `has_popup` and `controls`, and `Select`'s open popup uses `role("listbox")` directly instead of wrapping it in a modal `dialog`.
