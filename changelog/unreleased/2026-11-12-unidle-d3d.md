- **Windows: a Rune app with a GPU ends at once when it's killed.** D3D12 runs four `D3D Background Thread`s at idle priority (base 1), and a thread has to run to end: on a busy machine they sat Ready after `TerminateProcess` (Task Manager, an uninstaller closing running copies) and kept the process alive 3.4-106 s (8 kills). Once the first GPU frame is done, and again 2 s later, Rune raises them to below normal: the same kills ended in 75-180 ms. Apps no longer need to do it themselves, and with the CPU-first window they couldn't (the GPU comes after their first frame).
