- **Windows: a window's taskbar button shows progress and a count (`WindowHandle::set_taskbar`, `Taskbar`, `TaskbarProgress`), and flashes on request (`WindowHandle::flash`).** `ITaskbarList3`, made on the first call that shows something (never at launch): the indeterminate bar (Explorer animates it, so the app wakes no more), a full amber or red bar, and an overlay badge drawn with GDI at the taskbar's small-icon size (1 to 9, then 9+), set again on each new button after Explorer restarts. A flash blinks twice and leaves the button lit until the window comes forward; it does nothing for a window in front or while Focus Assist holds notifications back.
