- **Startup CPU-first policy (`CpuFirst`, `App::cpu_first`, `WindowSpec::cpu_first`):** added a `#[non_exhaustive]` `rune::CpuFirst` enum (`Auto` default, `Only`, `Off`) with `App::cpu_first` and `WindowSpec::cpu_first` builders so apps (including `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` crates) can opt into staying on `CpuPainter` (`CpuFirst::Only`) or skipping CPU-first startup (`CpuFirst::Off`) on Windows, Linux, and full-window web pages without mutating `RUNE_CPU_FIRST`. `Auto` preserves existing startup and GPU handover behavior; `RUNE_CPU_FIRST` (and `globalThis.RUNE_CPU_FIRST` on web: `0`/`off`, `only`, `auto`) continues to override the builder without a rebuild.
