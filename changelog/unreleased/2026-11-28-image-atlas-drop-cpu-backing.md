- **Image atlas pages no longer keep a permanent CPU copy beside their GPU textures.** Once a window or overlay runs on the GPU (`Backing::Gpu`, set automatically by GPU hosts and `Renderer::upload_images`), `ImageAtlas` drops `ImagePage::data` and stages changed rectangles directly from `Pixels` through `Staging::write_texture_rect` (and Metal `replaceRegion` on idle Apple Silicon shared textures). Shelf growth, rest shrinking, wake from hide, and device-loss recovery re-upload live slots from their `Pixels` handles without retaining full-page CPU shadow buffers, while `cpu_boot::Scene` still borrows `ImagePage::data` during CPU-boot and reconstructs pages on demand if called after GPU upload.
