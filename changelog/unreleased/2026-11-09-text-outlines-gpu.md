- **Text whose scale moves can draw straight from its glyph outlines on the GPU, so a zoom no longer blurs it and it doesn't sharpen when it lands.** `FontSmoothing::Windfoil` (opt-in) draws moving text with Windfoil's exact box-filtered coverage over each glyph's quadratic outline (text/outline/, outline.wgsl), with CoreText's dilation and y autohint applied per pixel, at its resting pen and size magnified; still text draws as `On`. The outlines live in one store texture, bound by a Windfoil variant of the uber shader (render/variant.rs). Renderers start with it everywhere but macOS, whose Classic shader, layout and `uber.metallib` are unchanged until text asks for outlines; a Windfoil shader that can't be built leaves the renderer Classic and text draws as `On`. `Renderer::uber_shader` reports which draws. The DX12 bytecode is the Windfoil variant's (`cargo run -p rune --example regen_dxbc`), and `scripts/precompile-metal-shaders.sh` also builds `uber_windfoil{,_native}.metallib`; until a Mac has, Windfoil compiles its WGSL at run time and says so once. `examples/text_gpu.rs` snaps and times text at rest, scrolling and zooming.
