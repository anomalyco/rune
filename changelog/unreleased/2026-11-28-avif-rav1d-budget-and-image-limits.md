- **Native AVIF decoding enforces pixel budgets directly inside `rav1d` and adds per-load `ImageLimits`.** `rune-image` now drives `avif-parse`, pure-Rust `rav1d`, and `yuv` directly so `Dav1dSettings::frame_size_limit` rejects oversized primary and alpha frames before frame-plane allocation and converts decoded planes straight into a checked, `try_reserve_exact`-allocated RGBA8 buffer. Callers with smaller preview surfaces can tighten per-load dimension, pixel-count, and byte limits via `ImageLimits` with `ImageCache::load_with_limits` and `ImageCache::load_scaled_with_limits` (re-exported as `rune::ImageLimits`).
