- **Reopening a window no longer panics while assistive technology is on.** A window closed and
  opened again gets a new AccessKit adapter but keeps its runtime, whose tree diffed against the
  old adapter: the new one got a partial update first, and AccessKit panicked ("Tried to
  initialize the accessibility tree without a root tree"). Each adapter now drops the runtime's
  tree on first touch and sends the whole tree once its own client asks. New
  `AccessibilityLink` drives that half of an adapter in tests.
