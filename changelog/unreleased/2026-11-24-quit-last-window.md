- **Closing an app's last main window now runs its `App::on_quit`.** Previously `Windows::cleanup` dropped finished hosts before starting the quit (under `orderly_exit` and wasm) and `Host::quit_hook_in_turn` skipped finished windows anyway, so a one-window app closed by its close button or `AppHandle::close` ran `on_close` without `on_quit`. The quit now starts before finished hosts drop, and `quit_hook` runs in the window whose close ended the app (after its `on_close`) as well as in any remaining open windows, while fast exit still leaves finished hosts undropped.
