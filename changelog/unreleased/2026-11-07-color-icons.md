- **Icons can paint their own colours.** An `Icon` whose body names colours (`fill="#…"`, `stroke="#…"`, gradient stops) draws in them, rasterized as full SVG by resvg (groups, transforms, `use`, gradients) at the size it draws, so file-type badges keep their colours in both appearances. Its `currentColor` parts take the element's colour, and the element's opacity and the colour's alpha still fade it; it draws upright, ignoring `icon_rotation`. Monochrome icons draw as before. Without the `svg` feature every icon draws monochrome.
