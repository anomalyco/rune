- **`rune::Tray` now has a macOS `NSStatusBar` / `NSStatusItem` backend, and `TrayIcon::template(bool)` marks icons as macOS template images.** Primary click sends `TrayEvent::Activate { token: None }`, secondary click or Control-click opens the flat `NSMenu` whose picks send `TrayEvent::Menu(id)`, icons are sized in points to `NSStatusBar.thickness` preserving aspect ratio, `set_menu` and `set_tooltip` update live, and dropping `Tray` removes the status item. `TrayIcon::template` defaults to `false` and is ignored off macOS.
