- **Overlays show on GNOME Wayland through XWayland.** When the Wayland compositor has no layer-shell (Mutter), an overlay that takes no input (no pointer regions, no keys) now opens as an override-redirect ARGB X11 window on XWayland with an empty input shape, at its edge of the primary display (or the pointer's), instead of reporting `Availability::Unavailable`. Its scale comes from `Xft.dpi`. The rest of the app stays on Wayland; without `DISPLAY` nothing changes.
