- **Windows menus are dark in dark windows, and behave after they close.** Pop-up, context and window menus follow what the window draws (dark or light, Windows 10 1903 and later), and tray menus follow the system until a window says. A click right after a menu closes lands even if the pointer didn't move, a menu opened from an overlay closes on a click outside and takes the keyboard, `wake_after` timers and overlays keep running while a menu is open, and selectable text's Copy shows `Ctrl+C` (Backspace hints read `Backspace`) instead of `Super+C` and `⌫`.
