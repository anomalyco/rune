- **Windows: the app's jump list (`set_jump_list`, `JumpList`, `JumpItem`).** Tasks and named categories, each entry a shell link to the app's executable with one argument, which a running app receives through `single_instance`. It's built and committed through `ICustomDestinationList` on a thread of its own (made on the first list, asleep in between), never on the UI thread; an unchanged list does nothing, and entries the person removed stay out.
