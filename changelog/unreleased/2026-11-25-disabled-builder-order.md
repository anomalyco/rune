- Builder order with `El::disabled` and `El::focusable(false)`: calling `El::disabled(true)` or `El::focusable(false)` before `El::link`, `El::on_step`, `El::focusable(true)`, or `El::dir_by_paragraph` now produces the same non-focusable result as calling it after; disabled links also ignore pointer/keyboard activation, keep `Cursor::Default` unless overridden, and expose disabled state without focus/click actions in AccessKit and the web accessibility mirror (while `El::aria_disabled` links and sliders stay focusable without activating).
