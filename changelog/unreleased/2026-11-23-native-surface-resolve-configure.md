- Native surfaces: resizing a window (or toggling full screen) while a `NativeSurface` with an observer plays no longer makes wgpu refuse the new surface size (`Failed to wait for GPU to come idle before reconfiguring the Surface`), which cost the window its surface. On a device with timestamp queries, a completion callback submitted each frame's timestamp resolve from the `rune-video-completion` thread, and one that landed while the window's thread configured its surface left the queue non-empty at wgpu's check. Surface configures and those submissions now go through one gate (`rune_gpu::configure`): a resolve that comes up during a configure is parked and submitted by the completion thread once the configure is done.
