- **Windows: closing an app's last window destroys it at once.** When the app ends fast and nothing keeps it running, the window's `on_close` runs and the window is destroyed in the same close, as a bare Win32 window's `WM_CLOSE` does, with no hide before it. The quit's hooks, the storage's last write and the exit deadline run after it as before. `WM_CLOSE` to window destroyed, measured with a poll that doesn't sleep a timer tick: 2,896 → 2,358 µs p50 (GPUI 2,794).
