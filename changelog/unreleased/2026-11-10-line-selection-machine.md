- rune-kit: `line_selection::LineSelection` is the line-selection gesture as one explicit machine (idle, selecting, unselecting the one selected line, pressing and dragging over code), shared by every code and diff view. `DiffState` uses it: Shift-click keeps the end of the selection Pierre keeps (its start, unless the press is above it), and Shift comes from the press's own modifiers rather than tracked key events.
