- **Closing a window no longer waits on interaction timing.** With timing on, a window's runtime waited for its last rollup to reach the collector as it closed, freezing the main thread for at least 100 ms. It now queues the rollup and moves on; the event loop waits once, as the app exits. Timing on now costs within 1% of off in `examples/interaction_cost` (wheel 0.160 → 0.069 ms a frame), where it had cost up to 2×: macOS ran the thread that just slept on a slower core for the frames after.
