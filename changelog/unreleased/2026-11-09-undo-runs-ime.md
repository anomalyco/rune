- **`TextEdit` undo steps follow Chromium's editor, and its IME composition is a state of its own.** Typing, line breaks and deletions join one open step the way a browser's typing command does: deleting after typing starts a new step that more deletions and typing join, Return joins the step, the first character typed onto an empty line (after a line break, or into an emptied field) ends it, a word or line deletion (Ctrl+Backspace, Ctrl+K) always starts one, and a caret move, selection or focus change closes it; pauses and word boundaries don't split a run, as in Chromium. Before, each Backspace and line break was its own step and a typing run was one. **Breaking:** the public `TextEdit::preedit` field is now a method (`preedit()`, returning `Option<(&str, Option<(usize, usize)>)>`); `is_composing()` asks whether an input method is composing, and `cancel_composition()` replaces `preedit = None`.
