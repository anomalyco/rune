- **A fold's moving edge feathers shutting as well as opening.** An `El::fold_glide` row used to feather its bottom edge only while it opened; shutting, the edge was a hard cut. Now the edge softens both ways. The feather grows over the first ~50 ms of motion, holds steady through a reversal, and thins to nothing as the glide lands, so nothing is faded at rest. The top edge isn't feathered, so a header above the fold stays sharp. The new `El::fold_feather(pt)` sets one fold's feather depth, overriding its stack's `StreamEdge::mask_max`.
