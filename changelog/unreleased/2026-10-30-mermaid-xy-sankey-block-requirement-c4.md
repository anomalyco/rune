- **Mermaid draws XY charts, sankey, block, requirement and C4 diagrams.** `xychart-beta` draws bar and line series over category or numeric axes, upright or `horizontal`. `sankey-beta` reads CSV rows and draws columns of nodes joined by bands as thick as their flow. `block-beta` lays blocks out in `columns` with spans, `space`, nested groups, block arrows and edges. `requirementDiagram` draws requirements and elements with their fields, joined by the seven relationship kinds. `C4Context`, `C4Container`, `C4Component`, `C4Dynamic` and `C4Deployment` draw people, systems, containers and components (stores as cylinders, queues on their side, external ones in grey) in nested boundaries, with relationships and their technology. These no longer fall back to source (t1058).
