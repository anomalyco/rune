- **A window with a compositor that draws nothing of its own rests like one without: a due timer whose frame builds the same instances draws nothing.** `Compositor::draws_own` (default true) says whether the last frame drew anything besides `Frame::draw_ui`; while it's false the window records its frame and skips the next unchanged one, as it does with no compositor. OpenCode attaches its Office compositor to every window, so every timer wake (a plugin's retry, a poll's timer) drew a whole frame, 2,000-4,700 µs of UI-thread CPU, though nothing on screen changed. `frame-profile`'s live log also counts the build a timer's frame does before it takes a drawable (`prebuild`).
