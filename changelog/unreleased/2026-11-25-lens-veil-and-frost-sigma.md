- **`rune-fx`'s `LensParams` adds `veil` and `frost_sigma`.** `LensParams::veil` (0..1, default `0.0`) mixes the glass body toward `tint` after the multiplicative tint and under the specular rim light, so dark floating glass pills and menus stay readable over busy backdrops without growing `InstRaw`. `LensParams::frost_sigma` (default `LensParams::DEFAULT_FROST_SIGMA` = `18.0` pt, read through `LensParams::frost_blur_pt()`) lets frosted lenses blur past 18 pt up to the blur pyramid's 64 device-px reach (32 pt at 2×, 64 pt at 1×), while `FlutedGlassParams` keeps 18 pt. `veil` and `frost_sigma` are new `pub` fields on `LensParams`, so struct literals without `..Default::default()` need them (or `..Default::default()`); lenses that leave both at their defaults draw bit-identically.
