- **`Cursor::ResizeN`, `Cursor::ResizeE`, `Cursor::ResizeS`, `Cursor::ResizeW`, `Cursor::Alias`, and `Cursor::Copy` (CSS `n-resize`, `e-resize`, `s-resize`, `w-resize`, `alias`, `copy`).** Additive on `#[non_exhaustive] Cursor`; mapped to AppKit's one-way resize and drag link/copy cursors on macOS, Wayland `wp-cursor-shape-v1` (including layer-shell overlays) and X11 theme names on Linux, CSS keywords on web, and Windows's two-way system arrows for one-way resizes plus Chromium's Windows bitmaps for `Alias` and `Copy`; `examples/sketch` (`--scene handles`) shows them.
