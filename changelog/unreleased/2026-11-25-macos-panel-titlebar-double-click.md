- macOS: ordinary windows adopted by `Panel::adopt_window` (`MacWindowOptions::nonactivating_panel(true)` and `WindowOptions::launcher_panel()`) now ignore titlebar double-clicks, `Tx::toggle_maximized()`, `Tx::toggle_full_screen()`, and `App::maximized()` / `App::full_screen()`, and `WindowHandle::placement()` reports `(false, false, false)` for their maximized/minimized/full-screen flags instead of calling `miniaturize:`, `zoom:`, `toggleFullScreen:`, or `setStyleMask:` on winit's hidden source `NSWindow` (which previously could miniaturize the hidden window or resize a non-resizable launcher panel to fill the screen).
