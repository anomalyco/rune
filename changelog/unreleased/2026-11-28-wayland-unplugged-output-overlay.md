- Wayland layer-shell overlays now distinguish an unplugged output (`wl_registry.global_remove` observed before a post-`closed` `wl_display.sync` answer, or without `closed`) from a compositor surface close: unplugging hides the overlay without marking it `Unavailable` or setting `OverlayHandle::failure`, surfaces placed without an explicit output follow `wl_surface.enter`/`leave`, and `show()` aimed at a disconnected display makes no surface on another output and reports no failure.
