- **Headless `--realtime`: updates interrupt the frame tick wait instead of sleeping until the next tick.** The realtime loop now waits on `HeadlessWake` against an anchored frame deadline, draining queued updates, shortcuts, menus, and window requests as soon as they arrive and then waiting out only what remains until the tick, removing frame-interval and `nanosleep` lateness from update delivery without drifting cadence.
