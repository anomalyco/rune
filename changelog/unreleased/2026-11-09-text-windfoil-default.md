- **Text draws with Windfoil everywhere but macOS: one coverage function for text at rest and in motion, with the macOS weight.** `FontSmoothing::Windfoil` is now the default on Windows, Linux and the web; macOS (and a Mac's browser) keeps `On`, unchanged, and every mode stays selectable on every OS (`DirectWrite`, `On` and `Off` are the fallbacks). Atlas masks at rest are filled on the GPU by the uber shader that draws moving text from outlines (render/fill.rs), so a zoom lands without a pop; the CPU fills the same coverage before a renderer can (text/outline/cover.rs). Dilation is CoreText's times `TEXT_WEIGHT` (0.93), whose ink matches CoreText's within 1% at 1x to 2x, and pens sit on quarter pixels. CJK faces up to 24 ppem draw their TrueType-hinted outlines from whole-pixel pens at half the dilation, so dense ideographs stay legible at 1x. Each mask's residency (uploaded, pending, filled) is a state machine: a GPU-filled mask is filled again when CPU rows are uploaded over it.
