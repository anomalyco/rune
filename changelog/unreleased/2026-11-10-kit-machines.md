- **Kit: `ComboboxState`, `ShortcutRecorderState`, `TabDragState` and `PaneState` are explicit state machines.** A combobox is closed or open with a highlight (`ComboboxState::step` with `ComboboxEvent`; an edit lets go of a chosen row, so deleting back to an earlier query never brings back a row chosen for it); a recorder is idle or capturing, and only a capture has the error and the chord it refused; a tab strip's gesture keeps where the press landed, the torn-out tab's rect and the click to swallow after a drag inside the phase they belong to (`TabDragState::phase()` replaces the public field); a pane is open or closed with its peek resting, shown or leaving. `ToastStack` keeps a region's toasts as OpenCode Desktop's toast registry does: a toast shown again while in front updates and pulses, a buried copy moves to the front. BREAKING: `ComboboxState`'s fields and `TabDragState::phase`/`suppress_click` are private; use `is_open`, `search`, `active`, `step` and `phase()`.
