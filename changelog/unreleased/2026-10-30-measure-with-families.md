- **`rune::text::with_families` scopes `measure` to one thread.** Tests that switch font families (`Tx::set_font_families`) shared one process-wide set, so they had to run one at a time. `let _g = rune::text::with_families(sans, mono);` makes `measure`, `advance`, `ink` and `font_epoch` on this thread use those families until the guard drops; guards nest, and a headless window's switch under a guard moves only that thread. Real apps are unchanged: one switch still moves every thread.
