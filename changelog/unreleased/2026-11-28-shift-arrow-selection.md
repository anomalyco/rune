- **Text selection: `Shift` + navigation keys extend and shrink active selections on plain `El::selectable(true)` text.** When a non-empty selection exists (from mouse drag, double/triple click, `Cmd/Ctrl+A`, or `Tx::select_text`), `Shift+Left/Right/Up/Down`, word steps, `Shift+Home/End`, and `Shift+PageUp/PageDown` (including key repeat while held) move the selection's focus end across lines, spans, paragraphs, and virtual rows without requiring `El::caret_browsing(true)`, while leaving keys untouched when no selection is active or when an app `on_key` handler consumes them.
