- **A touchscreen works in Rune windows.** winit's touches reached only the web build; a window on Wayland, X11 or Windows dropped them, so taps and drags did nothing. `Runtime::touch` now takes them on every platform as a browser does: the first finger is the primary button, so a tap clicks and dragging an element with `on_drag` drags it; any other drag pans the scroll viewport under the finger, rubber-banding at its ends and flinging on with momentum, and cancels the press. Further fingers are ignored while one is down, and a touch the system cancels clicks nothing. On Windows the mouse events the system makes up from a touch or a pen are ignored, so nothing presses twice. The web's touch scrolling is the same code.
