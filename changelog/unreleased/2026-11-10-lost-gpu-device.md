- **A GPU device that goes away no longer panics the app.** wgpu reports a lost device only through `Device::set_device_lost_callback`, and drops the `DeviceLost` that `Surface::configure` returns without recording the surface's error sink: the surface was left with no presentation, and its next drawable panicked with "Surface is not configured for presentation" (seen after a Windows resume from Modern Standby). Rune now marks the device lost when it goes, never parks, warms or hands out a lost device, and fails `Surface::configure` for one. A window whose device is lost, or whose surface refused its configuration, gives both back and makes new ones before its next frame, keeping its window, size and place; an overlay hides and makes both again on its next show. `Gpu::is_lost` reports it, and `Gpu::from_device` builds a `Gpu` over a device an app made, with the same loss watch. A `Gpu` literal outside Rune no longer builds: use `Gpu::from_device`. Rune also installs an uncaptured-error handler that logs (`rune: a GPU error went uncaptured: ...`) instead of letting wgpu's default handler panic the app.
