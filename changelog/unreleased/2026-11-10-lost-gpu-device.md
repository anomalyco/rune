- **A GPU device that goes away no longer panics the app.** wgpu reports a lost device only through `Device::set_device_lost_callback`, and drops the `DeviceLost` that `Surface::configure` returns without recording the surface's error sink: the surface was left with no presentation, and its next drawable panicked with "Surface is not configured for presentation" (seen after a Windows resume from Modern Standby). A device `destroy`ed but not yet reported has the same shape, so `configure` polls it before deciding. Rune now marks the device lost when it goes, never parks, warms or hands out a lost device, and fails `Surface::configure` for one. A window whose device is lost, or whose surface refused its configuration, gives both back and makes new ones before its next frame, keeping its window, size and place; it retries a failed replacement a bounded number of times and then releases the window (`AppHandle::window_error` says why, and opening it again makes a window, device and surface). An overlay hides and makes both again on its next show. `Gpu::is_lost` reports it, and `Gpu::from_device` builds a `Gpu` over a device an app made, with the same loss watch. A `Gpu` literal outside Rune no longer builds: use `Gpu::from_device`. A device that draws surfaces also logs its uncaptured errors (`rune: a GPU error went uncaptured: ...`, each distinct one once) instead of letting wgpu's default handler panic the app; a headless device keeps the panic.
