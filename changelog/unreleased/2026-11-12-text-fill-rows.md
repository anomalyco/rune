- **Text: the CPU mask fill takes 40% less time, with the same bytes.** Masks of one glyph at several x phases share their rows (the outline's pieces, y crossings and constants are worked out once), texels fill four at a time with branch-free masks, and only texels a piece's row stretch crosses take its integral, four at a time across the row (SSE2 on x86_64, simd128 on the web when enabled, four plain lanes elsewhere: the same bits on every target). A first screen of a session at 2880x1800 (412 masks) fills in 4,400 µs on one thread instead of 7,200, and 1,000 instead of 1,900 on six. `RUNE_FILL_RECORD=<dir>` (debug builds) records each frame's queued fills for `text::fill_bench`.
