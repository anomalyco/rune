- **Linux: a window with Rune's own frame has a shadow, rounded corners and resize handles outside its edges.** Where a window with `App::titlebar` takes Rune's frame on Wayland (GNOME's case), it opened without decorations: square, with no shadow, resized from a band inside its edges. It now keeps the client-side frame the toolkit already draws around other windows there, less its header bar: the shadow, the one-point border, all four corners rounded while the window floats (the content is cleared outside the arcs, on the GPU and in a CPU-painted frame alike, and a blurred window blurs inside them), and the resize handles in the 12 points outside the edges, as a GTK window has them. A maximized, tiled or full-screen window is square with no shadow, as before. The band inside the edges remains only where that frame cannot be made: a borderless window, a compositor without subsurfaces, or one that keeps its own decorations. Rune still draws the caption buttons over the app's titlebar, and nothing changes for windows under a compositor that draws the frame itself, on X11, macOS or Windows (#591).
